### You don't need a better model. You need a studio.

Most AI writing tools are a chat window with a prompt. When the chat fills up, the story softens into generic prose, plot holes, and forgotten arcs. Your book ends up trapped inside someone else's app — or lost in a disposable thread.

Buckram Studio puts novelist planning on your computer: Cast, world bible, scene plans, and editorial passes tied to plain Markdown files. Each chapter can start from a fresh chat without the book falling apart.

- **No prompt-pasting marathon** — You just focus on the story. The Cast, world, and open threads are attached for you automatically — no more pasting the bible into every chat.
- **Continuity that holds** — Voice, world rules, and open threads stay available chapter after chapter.
- **Test before you commit** — Try an edit style on a sample first. Your real draft stays untouched until you say so.
- **Your files, always** — Plain Markdown on your disk. Uninstall tomorrow; keep every byte.

## How Buckram helps you finish the book

### Continuity — so Chapter 30 still agrees with Chapter 1

- **Cast & world bible** — Character voice, places, research, clues, relationships, and who's off-stage, all on disk.
- **Smart chapter briefings** — When you write with the AI, you only have to care about the story. Cast, world rules, and active threads for *this* scene are assembled and attached automatically — no bible-length paste into the chat. The model gets what this chapter needs, not 80,000 words of past text. You still choose how much context to send (light, standard, or full).
- **World memory & continuity checks** — Later chapters are less likely to re-introduce the same tavern or limp. See who vanished, which clues are unpaid, and which threads went cold.
- **Import world from prose** — Pull setting and Cast from a manuscript or EPUB **without** importing chapter drafts — useful for shared-world work or a new story on familiar terrain.
- **Dramatic layer** (optional) — Track how emotions and stakes shift across the book. Completely optional and hidden until you choose to use it.

### Control — you set the beats; AI follows

- **Work Tree** — Your book as a clear map: canon, chapters, editing, export, and help. One obvious next step per row.
- **Outlines & Director notes** — Scene cards, pacing notes, and expand-beats-into-prose when you are ready to draft.
- **Generate Chapter from Outline** — One guided run turns outlined scenes into narrative prose, with this chapter's briefing already attached.
- **Chapter Council** — Fresh eyes on the outline or draft; you meet the plan or let it auto, then revise surgically.
- **Chat about** — Opens a focused writing chat with the right files attached. Bigger runs wait for you to confirm before they spend.
- **Safe sandbox editing** — Build multi-pass polish packs. Try a style on a test sample, compare results, then run on real chapters when you are ready (production runs are Premium).
- **Craft guardrails** — Catch stock AI phrasing and tired gestures. Free gets underlines; **ProseGuard** (Premium) adds stronger protection at draft time.

### Ownership — your manuscript is never held hostage

- **Plain files on your computer** — Manuscript and canon stay readable Markdown whether you are Free or Premium.
- **Export when you need it** — KDP-ready DOCX, Markdown, PDF, and EPUB. No extra software for the basics.
- **Help where you are** — Every studio page has a **?** that opens the right article. Full docs live in the Work Tree.

## Getting started in 3 steps

You do not need to be a developer. Buckram runs inside [Cursor](https://cursor.com) (the editor writers use for Agent chat).

1. **Open your book folder in Cursor.** (Note: Use the standard Editor window, not the chat-only view, so extensions are visible.)
2. **Install Buckram Studio** — From Extensions, search for Buckram Studio ([Open VSX listing](https://open-vsx.org/extension/buckram/buckram-studio)).
3. **Click the bold B icon** — That opens your Writing Studio (the Work Tree). Then open **Get Started** and pick a path: brainstorm a new idea, create a series or book, import an existing draft, or pull world and Cast from prose without importing chapters.

That is it. Your manuscript stays on your computer. Guided setup and full help live under **Work Tree → Documentation**.

## Who this is for

- **The epic fantasy builder** — Locks Cast and the world bible, then uses smart briefings so Chapter 30 still matches Chapter 1 without drowning every chat in series lore.
- **The discovery writer** — Drafts with **Chat about** and Co-writer, then runs continuity checks later for plot holes and missing people.
- **The mystery / thriller architect** — Uses Director notes for tension and clue tracking so red herrings stay honest.
- **The self-publisher on deadline** — Imports Markdown or DOCX, catches stock phrasing, proves polish in the Testing sandbox, then exports KDP-ready DOCX and EPUB.
- **The writer with a manuscript already** — Imports Markdown, DOCX, EPUB, or Scrivener; optional canon bootstrap; then specialists until Cast, world, and Co-writer feel like yours.
- **The shared-world / fanfic writer** — Imports world and Cast **without** the original chapters, then drafts new people on that terrain.

We charge for **workflow automation at scale**, not for your words. Chat and drafting run through your Cursor setup — you pay your AI provider directly; Buckram never bills per word or marks up tokens.

Your book stays plain Markdown on disk either way. Uninstall tomorrow and you keep every byte.

- Full-length writing with Cast, world bible, smart briefings, and craft underlines
- Unlimited building of editing packs; **Testing** sandbox to try styles on a sample first (fair-use rate limits apply)
- Gentle nudge at **50,000 words** for manuscript drafts — you can keep writing past it; files never lock

- Unlimited Testing and unlimited manuscript size
- Full-book KDP-ready export

## A few practical notes

- **Needs Cursor** — Buckram is a Cursor extension. Writing chats and chapter runs use Cursor's Agent. Plain VS Code is not enough.
- **No extra software for the basics** — No Python, LibreOffice, or Pandoc required for Work Tree, Chat about, bundled editing packs, or DOCX / Markdown / PDF / EPUB export.
- **Optional extras** — DeepL translation packs need your own DeepL key. Pandoc is only an optional EPUB fallback. Neither blocks core writing or export.
- **Settings** — Open **Settings → Buckram Studio** (or the Buckram Settings panel in the Work Tree) for change history, craft underlines, name click-through, Work Tree refresh, and DeepL plan.

Chat models only see a fixed amount of text each turn. Stacking the same pages again and again is how answers go soft — that is how models work, not a Buckram quirk. Inside the extension, **Documentation → Read me first → How chat context works** explains habits that keep chats sharp.
