# Endurance cost

## What this method changes

Make hardship credible by tracking the accumulating effects of exertion, environment, scarcity, injury, compromise, and incomplete recovery.

**Narrator priority:** Record available resources, bodily capability, environmental resistance, choices made to continue, and the cost carried into the next beat.

## Best fit

Survival, travel, labor, conflict, aftermath, siege, illness, deprivation, and moral choices made under material pressure.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Not unmodulated misery. Include neutral procedure/cooperation/practical care/temporary relief but account for cost and incomplete restore.

- Do not use suffering as proof of virtue.
- Do not repeat generic pain/exhaustion/blood/cold without changing function/cost.
- Do not let injured bodies perform full capacity without adaptation/consequence.
- Do not aestheticize wounds/starvation/exposure/death.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters replaces only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml after confirmation.
