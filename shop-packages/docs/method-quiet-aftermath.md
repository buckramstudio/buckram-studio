# Quiet aftermath

## What this method changes

Organize scenes around what remains after change: altered routine, incomplete account, belated recognition, material residue, and emotion that the narrator approaches without fully resolving.

**Narrator priority:** Attend to residue and discrepancy, distinguish present fact from remembered account, and allow recognition to arrive late, partially, or indirectly.

## Best fit

Loss, reunion, regret, social change, memory, aftermath, unreliable self-understanding, and scenes where omission carries more force than declaration.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Quiet ≠ vague/bloodless/inert. Scene still needs present action/choice/encounter/changed understanding. Ambiguity from incomplete knowledge/mixed motive not missing basic info.

- Do not announce poignancy/sadness/nostalgia/significance/healing.
- Do not make every sentence hedged/evasive.
- Do not conceal facts viewpoint certainly knows solely for twist.
- Do not convert memory into complete cinematic replay without present mediation.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters replaces only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml after confirmation.
