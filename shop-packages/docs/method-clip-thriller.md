# Clip thriller

## What this method changes

Keep every beat under pressure by centering immediate objectives, obstacles, operational observation, fast choices, and consequences that alter the next move.

**Narrator priority:** At every moment make clear what the viewpoint wants now, what blocks it, what usable information changes, and what action follows.

## Best fit

Pursuit, confrontation, infiltration, escape, investigation under time pressure, and scenes driven by tactical uncertainty.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Speed ≠ constant staccato. Occasional medium sentences for synthesis. Details that affect choice + enough for geography/causality.

- Do not open with weather/waking/biography/atmosphere unless active obstacle.
- Do not narrate every movement equal weight.
- Do not insert interior essays while threat demands action.
- Do not fake urgency via fragments alone.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters replaces only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml after confirmation.
