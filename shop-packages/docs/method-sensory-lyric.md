# Sensory lyric

## What this method changes

Build emotional pressure through precise sensory selection, recurring material images, and controlled syntax variation while keeping agency, setting, and meaning legible.

**Narrator priority:** Select the sensory detail the viewpoint cannot ignore, connect it to present action or memory, and let recurrence alter meaning without explaining symbolism.

## Best fit

Memory, intimacy, place, transformation, grief, desire, and moments where present sensation carries layered emotional association.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Lyric ≠ maximal description. Every sensory detail must orient, pressure memory, alter emotion, or change action. Delete decorative details that do none.

- Do not use beauty/haunting/ethereal/timeless/somehow/ineffable/significance as description substitutes.
- Do not stack multiple metaphors or sensory channels in one sentence.
- Do not personify setting by default.
- Do not attach symbolic explanation to recurring image.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters replaces only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml after confirmation.
