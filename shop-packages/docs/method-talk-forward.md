# Talk-forward

## What this method changes

Let dialogue perform most of the scene's action while lean narration tracks leverage, behavior, silence, and physical consequence.

**Narrator priority:** Keep speakers oriented, make every exchange change knowledge or leverage, and preserve the gap between literal wording and actual intent.

## Best fit

Negotiation, reunion, interrogation, flirtation, conflict, group scenes, and any scene where people pursue incompatible goals through speech.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Not transcript-only. Narration preserves viewpoint/physical state/space control/consequences. Distinction from conversational strategy not catchphrases/phonetic spelling.

- Do not use greetings/names/repeated questions unless pressure/relationship work.
- Do not let characters tell each other shared info for reader.
- Do not explain subtext in narration after exchange.
- Do not give every speaker same sentence length/profanity/wit/directness.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters replaces only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml after confirmation.
