# Warm domestic

## What this method changes

Reveal relationship pressure through ordinary care, household logistics, tactile objects, social memory, and the gap between what people do for one another and what they can say.

**Narrator priority:** Notice who performs care, who receives it, who avoids it, and how ordinary spaces and tasks register changing relationships.

## Best fit

Family, friendship, community, workplace intimacy, caregiving, reunions, departures, and conflict carried by routine rather than spectacle.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Warmth coexists with inconvenience/resentment/asymmetry/failed care. Cozy detail only for items used, relationally noticed, or materially changed.

- Do not equate domestic work with automatic virtue.
- Do not use food/warmth/soft fabric as generic comfort without relational function.
- Do not sentimentalize every care act.
- Do not explain subtext immediately after gesture/dialogue.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters replaces only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml after confirmation.
