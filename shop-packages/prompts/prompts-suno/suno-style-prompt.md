# Suno — style field

Plain descriptors, comma-separated. **No square brackets.** Brackets are for the lyrics field.

## Order (front-load what matters)

genre, mood, instruments, vocals, production, BPM, key

Example shape (not a recipe to copy blindly):

`dark electric blues, spare, late-night, male vocal, dry room, 72 BPM, E minor`

## Do

- Short tags, not sentences
- Vocal gender / delivery as words: `soft female vocal`, `shouted male vocal`
- `120 BPM` and `D minor` as plain text

## Don’t

- `[120 BPM]` or `[Verse]` in Style
- Contradict (calm + aggressive) without a reason in one clause
- Twenty tags (dilutes). Prefer ~6–12
- Paste lyrics into Style
