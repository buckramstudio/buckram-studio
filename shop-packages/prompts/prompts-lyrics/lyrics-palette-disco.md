# Palette — Disco

Four-on-the-floor chorus. Joy as labor — you work the night.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Chorus is shine. Optional four-word chant tag. Bridge can be a breakdown with fewer words, not an emo collapse.

## POV

You / we. Invitation. Sweat is the point.

## Line density

Chorus: punchy 6–8. Verses: night logistics + body, still short.

## Rhyme

Clean dance-floor rhyme. Repeat the title. Open vowels on the hold.

## Imagery bank

Mirror ball, sweat, night, shine, tiles, last song, a grin, silver, heat.

## Chorus job

Joy you can dance. Title is the instruction or the shine-claim.

## Refuse

- Emo collapse as default
- Goth industrial cold as default
- Folk witness verses

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Put the shine on**

```
[Verse]
The tiles know our names by now
your hair still wet from rain
We didn't come to talk it out
we came to spend the pain

[Chorus]
Put the shine on
let the night do labor
Put the shine on
that's how we get later
```
