# Palette — Metal

Heavy metal. Pick **sung vs shouted**. Pick **mythic or street**. One lane. No pop chorus padding.

## Form

Verse – chorus – verse – chorus – bridge or break – chorus. Optional intro call. Chorus can be a chant; it is still simpler than the verses.

## POV

I / we / they. Mythic: oath, we. Street: I, named grit. Do not mix Olympus and a parking lot 50/50.

## Line density

Verses may be denser (still singable / shoutable). Chorus: fewer words, bigger vowels. Shouted chorus: even shorter.

## Rhyme

Hard landings. Internal hammer OK. Do not write a teen-pop AABB that smiles.

## Imagery bank

Mythic: iron, ash, oath, gate, winter, crown of rust. Street: pit, oil, night shift, brick, busted light. Pick one bank.

## Chorus job

Oath or threat. Title is the blade, not a love-summary.

## Refuse

- Pop chorus padding (“I need you tonight” filler)
- Mixing sung-pretty with shouted-random without a reason
- Mythic + street blend with no override line

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Ash in the oath** (mythic, sung chorus)

```
[Verse]
We signed it in the cold
the gate still knows our names
The rust remembers better
than the men who came

[Chorus]
Ash in the oath
we don't take it back
Ash in the oath
the winter keeps track
```
