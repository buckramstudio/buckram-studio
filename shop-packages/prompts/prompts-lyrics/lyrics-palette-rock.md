# Palette — Rock

Classic / stadium rock. Wide chorus. Verses walk to it.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Gang shout on the last chorus OK. Bridge can be a lift or a break, not a rap verse.

## POV

I / we. “We” is the band-and-room, not a committee.

## Line density

Chorus: punchy, 6–8 words. Verses: story in boots — still cut the 14-word clause.

## Rhyme

Strong end-rhyme on chorus. Verses can be looser. Title on a held vowel.

## Imagery bank

Night road, amp heat, cracked leather, exit sign, fists, rain on a windshield, last encore, a town you drive through.

## Chorus job

A line the room can yell. Promise = stand, go, refuse, or claim the night.

## Refuse

- Whisper-diary as the default voice
- Pop pre-chorus glitter on a bar-band brief
- Abstract “raise your energy”

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Run the red**

```
[Verse]
Dashboard light on empty
your laugh still in the seat
We promised we'd go home
the on-ramp disagreed

[Chorus]
Run the red
we're already late
Run the red
let the town stay
```
