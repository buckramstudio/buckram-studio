# Palette — Country

Named place. Small plot. Title-as-moral.

## Form

Verse plot, chorus = the moral in the title. V–C–V–C–B–C is fine. Bridge: the cost or the joke that hurts.

## POV

I. Named town, road, or bar. You is specific.

## Line density

Verses carry the little plot (still cut long clauses). Chorus: title + one restatement, not a TED talk.

## Rhyme

Clean country couplets OK. Chorus landing perfect. Title must be singable on the hold.

## Imagery bank

Town name, truck, porch, mile marker, diner coffee, church lot, river bend, a job, a weather you can smell.

## Chorus job

Title-as-moral. The verses prove it with a small story. Do not moralize without the town.

## Refuse

- Abstract “heart” with no place
- Pop dance-floor with no plot
- Fortune-cookie title that could be any genre

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Don't take the river road**

```
[Verse]
I left a note on the diner napkin
your sister said you'd gone to Greenville
The truck still ticks in the church lot
like it knows a shorter way

[Chorus]
Don't take the river road
it remembers every lie
Don't take the river road
unless you mean goodbye
```
