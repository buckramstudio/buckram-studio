# Palette — Lo-fi

Mumble-close. Loop-friendly. Bedroom. Rain is a texture, not a plot.

## Form

Short verse – short hook – repeat. Think loop: the chorus can be two lines that return. No stadium rock. No drop.

## POV

I. Indoor. Quiet you.

## Line density

Soft, short. Hook even shorter. OK to feel unfinished — that’s the loop.

## Rhyme

Soft / slant. Repeat over punch.

## Imagery bank

Bedroom, rain, homework light, tape, mug, window, headphones, 2am cursor.

## Chorus job

A mumble you can loop. Title is a small indoor sentence.

## Refuse

- Stadium rock
- Anthem we
- Hyperpop max-volume as default

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Tape still turning**

```
[Verse]
Homework light on the desk
rain does the same bar
Your chat sits unread
I don't pick a side

[Chorus]
Tape still turning
I let the hiss stay
Tape still turning
that's my whole day
```
