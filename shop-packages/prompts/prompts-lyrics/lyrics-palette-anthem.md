# Palette — Anthem

Pick **empowerment** or **villain chant**. One. “We” that can be shouted.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Chorus is a stand. Last chorus may add a shout line. Verses: why we stand — still concrete.

## POV

We / I. Crowd is allowed. Villain chant: I/we claim the dark without cartoon mustache unless the brief wants camp (name it).

## Line density

Chorus: short, stampable. Verses: evidence, not a manifesto page.

## Rhyme

Strong chorus rhyme or repeat. Title on the downbeat of the line.

## Imagery bank

Crowd, rise, the stand, a named line in the sand (not a literal flag unless the brief is that), hands, night, a door kicked in or held.

## Chorus job

The stand. Empowerment: we rise. Villain: we don’t apologize. Do not mix both 50/50.

## Refuse

- Apologetic quiet verse forever
- Mixing empowerment and villain without an override line
- Pop love-chorus filler

## Mini example (original)

Title: **We don't sit down** (empowerment)

```
[Verse]
They sold the quiet as a virtue
we learned it as a lock
The hallway kept our names
in a smaller font

[Chorus]
We don't sit down
say it till the room does
We don't sit down
that's the whole of us
```
