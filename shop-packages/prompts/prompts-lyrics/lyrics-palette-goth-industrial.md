# Palette — Goth / industrial

Cold refrain. Machine pulse. Beauty is a fluorescent, not a sunrise.

## Form

Verse – refrain – verse – refrain. Optional spoken or half-spoken bridge. No sunny pop lift. Industrial: leave space as if a beat occupies it.

## POV

I / it. The room or the machine can be a character. You is distant.

## Line density

Measured. Short clauses. Repetition is the point, not variety for its own sake.

## Rhyme

Obsessive repeating end-words OK. Perfect rhyme can feel too pop — slant and repeat the title instead.

## Imagery bank

Chrome, night shift, empty nave, wire, black water, clock-in light, concrete, a pulse you can count.

## Chorus job

A cold refrain. Title as a label on the machine, not a hug.

## Refuse

- Sunny pop lift / “dancing through the dark” as default
- Metal mythic swords unless the brief asks
- Disco joy

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Clock-in light**

```
[Verse]
The hallway hums in fours
my badge forgets my face
Water in the black tray
doesn't move

[Chorus]
Clock-in light
it knows my name
Clock-in light
it stays
```
