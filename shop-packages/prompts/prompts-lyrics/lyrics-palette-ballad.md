# Palette — Ballad

Power ballad or heartbreak ballad. Slow climb. Last chorus can lift — verses stay specific.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Quiet verse, wider chorus. Bridge: the cost or the last honesty. No party-start energy.

## POV

I / you. One relationship, one night or one year — not a crowd.

## Line density

Chorus: open vowels, 6–8 words. Verses: one image per line, not a eulogy paragraph.

## Rhyme

Perfect on chorus landings. Verses may slant. Title on the hold.

## Imagery bank

Rain, room, last time, hands, a photo, a key, a street you both used, a season.

## Chorus job

The feeling named simply. Title is the wound or the stay. Power: bigger vowels, still not a paragraph.

## Refuse

- Party-start / dance-floor default
- Three palettes (folk verse + metal bridge + pop chorus)
- Abstract “forever in my heart” with no room

## Mini example (original)

Title: **If you go, go slow**

```
[Verse]
Your key is on the dish
like you might still be kind
The rain does the window
I do the time

[Chorus]
If you go, go slow
leave the light in the hall
If you go, go slow
that's all I ask at all
```
