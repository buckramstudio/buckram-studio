# Palette — R&B / soul

Slow jam, Motown lift, or quiet bedroom. **Address one person.**

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Slow jam: longer holds, still short lines. Motown: tighter bounce, call in the chorus.

## POV

You. One body, one night, one unfinished sentence.

## Line density

Chorus: fewer words, open vowels (`you`, `stay`, `slow`). Verses: close details, not a crowd.

## Rhyme

Soft perfect rhymes on chorus. Verses may lean into repetition of `you`.

## Imagery bank

Late room, phone face-down, heat, silk or cotton, hallway, slow clock, mouth, a song in the next apartment.

## Chorus job

Plea, invitation, or staying. Title on the hold. Address one person — not the nation.

## Refuse

- Crowd chant / anthem we as default
- Spite-punk diction
- Rap-density verse unless the brief is a hybrid (name the override)

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Don't hang up**

```
[Verse]
Your streetlight in my window
makes a slow gold on the sheet
I almost said I'm fine
then I heard you breathe

[Chorus]
Don't hang up
I'm right here in the quiet
Don't hang up
let the night decide it
```
