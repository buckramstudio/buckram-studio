# Palette — EDM / dance

Sparse lyrics. **Build** and **drop** are jobs. Words get out of the way.

## Form

Short verse or spoken tag – build – drop (chant) – break – build – drop. Do not pack a novel into the drop.

## POV

You / we. Floor, not diary.

## Line density

Very sparse. 3–6 words on drop lines. Verses: one image, then stop.

## Rhyme

Chant > couplet. Repeat the title. Perfect rhyme optional.

## Imagery bank

Lights, floor, 4am, hands, sweat, strobe, last song, heartbeat kick.

## Chorus job

The drop line is the hook. Title on the hit. Pre-drop / build may be one rising sentence split into two lines.

## Refuse

- Verse-novel
- Folk witness paragraphs
- Explaining the feeling during the drop

## Mini example (original)
