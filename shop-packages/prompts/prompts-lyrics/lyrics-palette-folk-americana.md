# Palette — Folk / Americana

Witness verses. Refrain is **not** a pop chorus.

## Form

Verses that see. A short refrain that the room can join without a key-change explosion. Optional last-verse turn. No EDM drop. No rap density.

## POV

I / we as witness. Collective “we” is a town or a crew, not a brand.

## Line density

Verses may be a hair longer if the image is documentary. Refrain: short, repeatable.

## Rhyme

Ballad rhyme OK (ABAB / ABCB). Refrain can repeat a line more than it rhymes.

## Imagery bank

Mill, kitchen table, weather, two-lane, porch radio, hands, harvest, a name on a mailbox, river ice.

## Chorus job

Refrain = the thing the witness cannot stop saying. Not a pop hook stack.

## Refuse

- Pop chorus with a dance pre-chorus
- Rap verse density
- EDM drop
- Pretend-old-timey diction soup (`thee` / `o'er`) unless the brief asks

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Mailbox name**

```
[Verse]
We sat the storm out in the kitchen
the radio named counties like kin
Your hands around a chipped blue mug
like that could keep the river in

[Refrain]
The mailbox still says both our names
rain can't wash a letter off
```
