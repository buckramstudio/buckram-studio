# Palette — Latin

Latin pop / reggaeton. Body + night. English, Spanish, or mix — keep lines short either way.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Reggaeton: hook early, dembow space. Latin pop: cleaner chorus lift. Optional short chant tag.

## POV

You. Close dance. Present tense.

## Line density

Chorus: 6–8 words, open vowels. Verses: night + body details, not a travel brochure.

## Rhyme

Punchy chorus landings. Spanglish: put the title in the language that holds.

## Imagery bank

Club, street, summer, close dance, salt, neon, midnight taxi, a mouth, a repeated color.

## Chorus job

Body-and-night promise. Title you can say on the 1. Do not write a stiff English hymn meter.

## Refuse

- Hymn meter / church-choir default
- Folk witness paragraphs
- Generic “fiesta” with no close image

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Don't step back**

```
[Verse]
Street heat still on your wrist
the taxi keeps its idle
You laugh like the night
is a door we already paid

[Chorus]
Don't step back
stay where the bass is
Don't step back
this is how the night says
```
