# Palette — Jazz / torch

Space. Hold the vowel. One lost you. Last call.

## Form

Verse–verse or verse–chorus. Room for a hold. No EDM drop. No rap density. Optional spoken last line.

## POV

I; one lost you. Adult, not teen-pop.

## Line density

Fewer words than pop. Let a line end early. Chorus (if any) is a title you can hold.

## Rhyme

Soft. Internal echo OK. Do not bounce like funk unless the brief is swinging party jazz (name the override).

## Imagery bank

Low light, glass, rain, last call, piano, coat, a street after the club, smoke that isn’t a cliché if you make it a room fact.

## Chorus job

The hold. Title is the private sentence you’d only say after midnight.

## Refuse

- EDM structure
- Rap density
- Perky pop pre-chorus

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Last call glass**

```
[Verse]

[Chorus]
Last call glass

Last call glass

```
