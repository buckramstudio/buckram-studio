# Palette — Gospel

Testimony. Call–response. Light as a door, not a vibe word.

## Form

Verse testimony – refrain/response – verse – refrain – bridge as prayer or turn – refrain. Leave a line the congregation could answer.

## POV

I / we. We is the room. You may be the addressee of the prayer (not a romantic you unless the brief is that hybrid).

## Line density

Call line short; response shorter. Verses: one scene of the testimony, not a sermon transcript.

## Rhyme

Strong refrain landings. Call–response can repeat instead of rhyming.

## Imagery bank

Door, river, light, congregation, kitchen table grace, night that broke, a name lifted, hands.

## Chorus job

Refrain the room can answer. Title is the testimony in one breath.

## Refuse

- Irony / spite-punk as default
- Romantic pop chorus with no testimony
- Mocking sacred diction

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Open the door**

```
[Verse]
I brought the night in on my shoes
I thought the kitchen would stay dark
Somebody had already put the kettle on

[Chorus]
Open the door
we're coming in with what we are
Open the door
the light don't ask how far
```
