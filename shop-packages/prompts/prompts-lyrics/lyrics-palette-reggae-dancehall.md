# Palette — Reggae / dancehall

Offbeat space. Riddim air. Heat. Do not metal-density the verse.

## Form

Verse – chorus – verse – chorus. Optional toasting break with even shorter lines. Leave space; the unsaid sits on the offbeat.

## POV

I / you. Invitation, warning, or boast — one.

## Line density

Short. Do not fill every beat with words. Dancehall tags can be shorter still.

## Rhyme

Relaxed end-rhyme. Repeat the title. Internal bounce OK; walls of rhyme are hip-hop, not this default.

## Imagery bank

Yard, heat, sound system, night, smoke, sea-salt (if coastal), a name called across the yard.

## Chorus job

Singable warning or invitation. Title on a held, easy vowel.

## Refuse

- Metal density
- Pop pre-chorus soup
- Fake patois as a costume (write clear, musical English unless the writer’s brief is in a voice they own)

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Easy, the night**

```
[Verse]
Yard light yellow on the leaves
your laugh comes in late
Sound system knows our names
better than the gate

[Chorus]
Easy, the night
don't rush what the heat does
Easy, the night
let the system tell us
```
