# Palette — Musical theatre

Book verse. Want-chorus. Bridge = **decision**. Present-tense want.

## Form

Verse sets the room and the problem. Chorus is the want, named. Bridge: they choose. Optional short reprise of the want.

## POV

I. Present tense. The character wants something you can stage.

## Line density

Verses can be a bit talkier (still cut 14-word mush). Chorus: title-sized want. Rhyme can be tighter than indie diary.

## Rhyme

Showtune rhyme OK (clever, but not crossword). Chorus landing must sing.

## Imagery bank

Room, door, city, the thing they can’t say, a prop they hold, daylight vs night, a name they almost call.

## Chorus job

The want. Title is the want in a singable sentence. Not a passive diary.

## Refuse

- Passive diary with no want
- Pop dance drop as default
- Explaining the whole plot in the chorus

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Open anyway**

```
[Verse]
This apartment knows my pacing
the door has heard me twice
I packed the speech in my pocket
then I packed it twice

[Chorus]
Open anyway
I'm done with almost going
Open anyway
let the hallway know me
```
