# Palette — Grunge

Slouch verse. Bitter chorus. Triumph is a trap.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Chorus lifts a little then curdles. No hair-metal key-change win.

## POV

I. Tired, not theatrical. You may be a roommate, a town, a habit.

## Line density

Verses can mumble toward 10 words. Chorus still catchier — bitter catchy, not pep.

## Rhyme

Loose in verses. Chorus landing can be a dull thud rhyme (fine). Avoid sparkle couplets.

## Imagery bank

Damp room, TV snow, thrift coat, parking lot, ashtray, ceiling stain, a job shirt, rain that doesn't clean.

## Chorus job

Bitter summary. Title is the shrug that hurts.

## Refuse

- Hair-metal triumph
- Polished pop pre-chorus
- Inspirational bridge

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Ceiling stain**

```
[Verse]
The TV sells a life
with better weather in it
Your coat on my chair
still dripping

[Chorus]
Ceiling stain
maps a country I don't want
Ceiling stain
I sleep under the hunt
```
