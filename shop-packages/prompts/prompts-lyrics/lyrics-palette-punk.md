# Palette — Punk

Short lines. Spite. No gloss.

## Form

Tight: verse–chorus–verse–chorus. Bridge optional and short. No pretty pre-chorus.

## POV

I / you. You did the thing. I am not sorry in the pretty way.

## Line density

Very short. 4–8 words. If a line needs a comma, it is two lines.

## Rhyme

Hard hits. Perfect or none — no soft singer-songwriter slant as a costume.

## Imagery bank

Cheap room, spit, punched clock, locked door, flyer on a pole, split lip, last bus, a name you won't say nice.

## Chorus job

Spite you can chant. Title is the insult or the refusal.

## Refuse

- Gloss, reverb-poetry, long moral bridge
- Power-ballad climb
- Explaining the politics in verse 3 like a pamphlet (put it in one image)

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Don't wait up**

```
[Verse]
Your key still works
I changed the cheap lock
Clock says work
I say not

[Chorus]
Don't wait up
I'm not your stop
Don't wait up
the light stays off
```
