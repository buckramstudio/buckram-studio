# Palette — K-pop

Hook stacks, short English mix, chant tags. Do not name real groups.

## Form

Verse – pre-chorus – chorus – verse – pre-chorus – chorus – bridge – chorus (with a short chant / post tag). Hooks stack: title line + 2–4 word chant.

## POV

We / you. Group as “we.” Never real act names.

## Line density

Chorus and tags: very short. English mix: a few punch words, not a paragraph. Korean/other language: keep the same stress rule if you write it — short, chantable.

## Rhyme

Punchy end-rhymes on the English tags. Internal echo OK in pre-chorus. Chorus landings perfect.

## Imagery bank

Stage light, countdown, hands up, neon hallway, earpiece, last encore, midnight practice room, one color motif.

## Chorus job

Title + a chant you can clap. Post-chorus may be even dumber (that is correct).

## Refuse

- Long verse essays
- No chant / no stack (that’s singer-songwriter)
- Real group or idol names
- A folk-ballad bridge that kills the hook

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Count me in**

```
[Verse]
Hall light buzzing like a clock
We lace the same white shoes
Mirror says not yet — then now

[Pre-Chorus]
One breath
then we go

[Chorus]
Count me in
hands up, count me in
Say it twice
count me in

[Post-Chorus]
In, in
count me in
```
