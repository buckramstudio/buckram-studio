# Brief decoder

Job: turn a writer brief into **one** palette. Use this index even when palette modules are off. If the matching `lyrics-palette-*.md` module is enabled, use that file’s depth after you pick the row.

## Light

1. Match the brief to one row below.
2. Unknown brief → **nearest** row. Name the override in **one line**. Do not blend two palettes 50/50.
3. “Next summer’s top hit” / “chart smash” / “radio hit” → **Pop** (dance-pop / hit form). Not fortune-telling.

Must-not-break: one palette; one promise; do not stack three rows.

## Tie-breakers

- Dark + guitar + understatement → Blues, not Rock.
- Named town + moral title → Country, not Folk.
- Sparse words + drop → EDM, not Pop.
- Group chant + short English mix → K-pop, not Anthem.
- Witness + refrain → Folk, not Singer-songwriter (singer-songwriter is one room, one you).
