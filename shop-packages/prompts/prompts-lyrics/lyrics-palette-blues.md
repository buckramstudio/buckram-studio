# Palette — Blues

Dark / electric / delta. **AAB**. Understatement. Refrain is not a pop chorus.

## Form

AAB verses (line, line restated, payoff). Repeating refrain. Do not add a pop pre-chorus. 12-bar implication is enough; do not lecture bar counts in the lyric.

## POV

I. Understate the wound. Brag is dry, not stadium.

## Line density

A and A' similar length. B can be a little longer. Still ~6–10 words. Leave air for the guitar (even on the page).

## Rhyme

A rhymes with A or repeats. B lands. Do not AABB pop-stack the verse.

## Imagery bank

Kitchen, one lamp, river, train, empty chair, bottle, screen door, heat, a name said once.

## Chorus job

If you use a refrain, it is a **short repeating line**, not a four-line pop chorus with a title explosion. Title can live in the refrain or the last line of B.

## Refuse

- Pop chorus padding / pre-chorus climb
- Oversinging the pain (every line a wail)
- Mixing in a rap verse “for contrast”

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Lamp stays on**

```
[Verse]
Kitchen lamp stays on for nobody
Kitchen lamp stays on for nobody
I eat standing up like company might still come

[Verse]
Train don't ask who paid the ticket
Train don't ask who paid the ticket
It takes the heat and leaves the pan to rust

[Refrain]
Lamp stays on
that's all I got to run
```
