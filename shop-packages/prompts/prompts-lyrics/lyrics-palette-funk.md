# Palette — Funk

Groove refrain. Talk-sing OK. The downbeat is a character.

## Form

Short verse – refrain – verse – refrain. Optional breakdown with even fewer words. Bridge should not become a ballad essay.

## POV

We / you. Invitation. Sweat is communal.

## Line density

Short. Leave holes for the groove. Talk-sing lines can be a little speechier if they still snap.

## Rhyme

Pocket over poetry. Repeat the title. Internal bounce OK.

## Imagery bank

Hips, street, sweat, downbeat, cheap lights, bass you can feel, night heat, a grin.

## Chorus job

Refrain the body can answer. Title is the instruction or the boast.

## Refuse

- Ballad essay verses
- Emo confession as default
- Metal density

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Give the downbeat**

```
[Verse]
Street cooks in the open lids
your laugh is a snare I know
We don't need a plan
we need the floor to go

[Chorus]
Give the downbeat
put your weight in it
Give the downbeat
that's the whole minute
```
