# Palette — Hyperpop

Glitch hook. Bright ugly candy. Max volume feelings in tiny lines.

## Form

Hook early. Verse can be chopped. Chorus may stutter the title. Bridge can glitch (repeat a fragment). Tasteful restraint is the failure mode.

## POV

I / you. Crush, spite, glee — pick a loud one.

## Line density

Tiny lines, stacked. Internal echoes. Still mouthable; don't write keyboard smash as a lyric.

## Rhyme

Sing-song, nursery-mean, or chopped repeats. Perfect rhyme can be cartoon on purpose.

## Imagery bank

Phone, plastic, crush, candy color, max volume, notification, glitter that hurts, a smile with teeth.

## Chorus job

Glitch the title. Repeat until it sticks. Do not suddenly go folk-quiet unless the brief asks a crash.

## Refuse

- Tasteful restraint as default
- Long coherent ballad verses
- “Cinematic emotional” with no candy/glitch

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Don't leave it on read**

```
[Verse]
Your bubble sits green
I throw glitter at the glass
It sticks to my teeth

[Chorus]
Don't leave it on read
on read on read
Don't leave it on read
I mean it I mean it
```
