# Palette — Hip-hop

Verse density + **short** hook. The hook is not another verse.

## Form

Verse – hook – verse – hook – optional bridge/break – hook. Verses carry internal rhythm. Hook: 2–4 short lines, repeatable.

## POV

I / we. Address the room. Objects and clocks, not abstract essays.

## Line density

Verses denser (still mouthable). Hook: sparse. If the hook is as dense as the verse, it failed.

## Rhyme

Multisyllable and internals in verses. Hook: simple, stamped, title-forward.

## Imagery bank

Block, clock, named object, pocket, hallway light, receipt, skyline from a cheap window — **original** images, no real-artist name-checks.

## Chorus job

Short hook. Title is the stamp. Do not explain the verse.

## Refuse

- Chorus that is another verse
- Pop pre-chorus glitter unless the brief is a sung-rap hybrid (then name the override)
- Real artist / label name-drops

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Receipt in the pocket**

```
[Verse]
Hall light counts my steps in two
pocket full of paper I don't need
Clock on the stove still set for someone
I eat late and call it peace

[Hook]
Receipt in the pocket
proof I paid to leave
Receipt in the pocket
don't ask me to keep
```
