# Palette — Singer-songwriter

One room. One you. Close. Quiet chorus that still catches.

## Form

Verse – chorus – verse – chorus – quiet bridge – chorus. No stadium chant. No drop.

## POV

I; one person as you. Do not address a crowd.

## Line density

Conversational, still 6–10 words. Chorus: the line you’d text and delete.

## Rhyme

Slant welcome. Perfect on the chorus title. Do not couplet-machine.

## Imagery bank

Kettle, street at 2am, one photo, coat hook, half a song on the fridge, window, a chair still warm.

## Chorus job

Quiet catch. Title is the private sentence.

## Refuse

- Stadium chant / anthem we
- EDM structure
- Three metaphors per line

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Chair still warm**

```
[Verse]
I hang your coat like a guest
who might still change their mind
The window ticks with rain
I don't draw the blind

[Chorus]
Chair still warm
I talk like you're in it
Chair still warm
that's my whole minute
```
