# Palette — Pop

Cluster: pop, dance-pop, teen pop, **summer hit**. One lyric job.

## Form

V–C–V–C–B–C. Optional short pre-chorus. Do not invent a through-composed art-song for a hit brief.

## POV

I / you. One addressee. “We” only if the brief is a dance-floor gang shout.

## Line density

Chorus: shorter than verses. ~6–8 words on chorus lines. Verses: ~8–10, more nouns.

## Rhyme

Perfect rhyme on chorus landings. Verses may slant. Do not hide the title on a weak beat.

## Imagery bank

Night heat, cheap lights, phone face-down, dance floor, open window, salt on skin, last train, a color that repeats.

## Chorus job

The promise you can shout. Title on the hold. Slightly incomplete — verses bring the night’s evidence.

## Refuse

- Chart fortune-telling (“#1 in July”)
- Stacking three subgenres (folk verse + rap bridge + metal chorus)
- Dense clever chorus
- Abstract “feel the vibe” with no object

## Mini example (original)

Title: **Leave the porch light**

```
[Verse]
Your jacket on the chair still
smells like rain and parking lot
I cue the same three songs
like that will walk you back

[Chorus]
Leave the porch light
I don't need a reason why
Leave the porch light
I'm not done saying hi
```
