# Stakes and escalation

What failure costs — personal, not only spectacular.

Kidlit scale (friendship/family without adult cynicism): , ; content treatment: .

## Light

Job: make failure cost something the reader cares about *for this person now*.

Contract: goal is Want; stakes are what they lose if they miss it — and those costs escalate as the story progresses.

Must-not-break:
- Stakes ≠ goal (consequence of missing ≠ the Want itself)
- Emotional stakes deepen spectacle
- Scale ≠ intensity (quiet talk can out-stake a chase)
- Reveal early enough that failure means something
- Escalate as a promise, not one dump-reveal
- As the character changes, what they stand to lose should change

## Normal

**Definitions**
- **Stakes** = what characters stand to **lose if they fail** (emotional cost of outcome)
- **Goal** ≠ stakes: goal is Want; stakes are consequence of missing it
- Plot stakes: bomb levels the city. Emotional stakes: person you love dies thinking you never cared.
- Both useful; emotional stakes go deeper

**Raise without going bigger**
- Deepen: threaten identity, belonging, trust, self-worth, hope, relationships
- Stack personal consequences; layered costs (success here = failure there)
- Force value betrayal to win; choose between two valued things
- Target the wound: abandonment wound → loneliness / found-family loss
- Connect external conflict to internal wound so outcome threatens something emotionally specific

**Common failures**

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Waiting too long to clarify failure’s meaning | Early First Act personal stakes visible |
| Confusing goals with stakes | Ask “what bad if they don’t?” |
| Spectacle without consequence | Invoice a personal cost |
| Only positive stakes | Name the loss, not only the prize |
| Stakes that never drive choice | Force a trade-off on page |

## Hard

**Timing (reveal & evolve)**
- Early First Act: personal stakes visible
- Inciting: what can threaten those stakes
- First Plot Point: clarify cost of failure
- Midpoint: raise, reverse, or redefine stakes
- Stakes escalate as a promise across the book
- As character changes, what they stand to lose should change

**Query / heartbeat angle**
- Emotional heartbeat = why these events are devastating *for this person now*
- Intersection of wound + Want (and gap between Want and Need)
- External / internal / relational stake layers; heartbeat usually at the join

**Diagnostics**
- What does this character care about most?
- What loss would wreck them emotionally?
- What if *success* still costs that thing?
