# Plant / payoff / foreshadowing (operational)

Setup that earns later turns. Definitions in ; this note is the working ledger.

## Light

Job: make later turns reclassify what the reader already saw.

Contract: surprising **and** inevitable — no vital fact only at the reveal.

Must-not-break:
- Plant has a present-scene job (camouflage by usefulness)
- Payoff reclassifies; does not invent the missing fact
- Don’t hang unused promise-guns (Chekhov economy) — sideshadow OK
- Red herrings need resolvable wrongness
- Audit climax tools before lock

## Normal

**Device map**

| Device | Job | Fail |
|--------|-----|------|
| **Plant** | Neutral-looking detail later load-bearing | Only appears at reveal |
| **Payoff** | Reclassifies the plant | Needs new off-page fact |
| **Foreshadow** | Tone / omen / thematic hint | Spoils by announcing |
| **Chekhov economy** | Don’t hang unused promise-guns | Over-pruning atmospheric life |
| **Red herring** | Fair mislead with resolvable wrongness | Cheap diversion, no explanation |

**Plant ledger (draft pass)**
For each major turn / twist / climax tool:
1. What must the reader have already seen?
2. Where planted? (chapter + disguise)
3. When can a sharp reader solve / anticipate?
4. What wrong interpretation is available?
5. Payoff beat: reclassify without dumping missing data

**Placement craft**
- Plant in charged scenes
- Early linchpin as casual detail; meaning flips late
- Pinch Points foreshadow without full truth
- Partial answers that raise new questions

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Rifle never fires | Cut plant or add payoff |
| Payoff needs new lore | Plant lore earlier in disguise |
| Neon foreshadow | Show detail; trust reader |
| Every detail is a gun | Allow sideshadow |
| Twist cheats fair play | Plant ledger + clue audit |

## Hard

**Chekhov intent vs drift**
- Original (conservation): every element necessary; remove irrelevancies — rifle on the wall must fire
- Modern drift: “plant early, payoff later” for twists — related but not identical
- Pushback: Hemingway valued inconsequential detail; Phillips argues single-role details make stories predictable; Chekhov left unfired guns thematically (*Cherry Orchard*)
- **Sideshadowing** (Morson, via narrative-devices note): scenes that stay inconsequential raise verisimilitude — not every detail is a gun

**Foreshadow ≠ adjacent devices**
- Not red herring (misleads), not flashforward (scene out of time), not Chekhov (economy of introduced elements)
- Builds suspense; can make later “bizarre” events feel earned

**Fair-play coupling**
- Mystery/thriller hybrids: plant ledger + Knox clue rules 
- Coincidence that “rescues” without plant = deus cousin
