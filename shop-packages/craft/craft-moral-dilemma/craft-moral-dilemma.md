# Moral dilemma dramatization

Ethical forks under pressure — dramatized, not sermonized.

## Light

Job: force a choice that costs a value the character wants to keep.

Contract: dilemma ≠ hard obstacle; both options bite.

Must-not-break:
- Two goods, two harms, or two identities — not “hard but obviously right”
- Make both options concrete (person, deadline, resource)
- Force a turn in scene (decision or refusal that changes the board)
- Invoice the cost in the sequel
- Prove theme via outcomes, not speechifying 

## Normal

**Dilemma ≠ obstacle**
- Obstacle: clear right action, hard execution
- **Dilemma:** choosing costs a value the character wants to keep
- If the “right” answer is obvious to reader and easy for hero, not a dilemma yet

**Stage it**
1. Concrete options (not abstract essays)
2. Attach each option to invested relationship or self-image
3. Turn in scene
4. Sequel invoices cost (guilt, exile, win-that-feels-like-loss)
5. Mid/late book: escalate so old compromise no longer works

**Theme without preaching**
- Want/Need externalizes Lie/Truth: wrenching choice between two concrete goods proves abstract argument
- Let characters disagree about the theme; events decide
- Supporting cast can embody the other horn 
- Antihero dilemmas still need tether and bill 

Prompt seeds: loyalty-vs-truth; temptation-to-reject-theme; forgiveness that costs justice .

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Speechifying in place of choice | Put the fork in action |
| False dilemma (easy third path) | Block easy path on-page |
| No cost after “hard choice” | Pay in relationship / identity |
| Theme sticker on chase plot | Align dilemma with Lie/Need |

## Hard

**Lie/Truth binding at the fork**
- Midpoint: Truth appears as antidote; character tries to hold Lie + Truth — false compromise often here
- Third Act / Climactic Moment: proves relationship to Truth in a way that changes outer conflict — that proof *is* theme
- Arc types: Positive embraces Truth; Negative rejects; Flat models Truth to change others — same staging craft, different invoice

**Escalation of ethical cost**
- Early dilemma can be smaller; later must retire the old workaround 
- Sequel Dilemma (Swain) is structural cousin — moral dilemma is thematic content inside that psychology 

**Edge cases**
- Tragedy: “right” choice still destroys — catharsis craft in 
- Antihero: illegible ethics still need a readable fork and bill
- Shelf ethics (war, grimdark, inspirational) stay in genre engines; reuse this staging
