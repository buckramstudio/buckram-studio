# Pacing

AI tends to resolve conflicts in the same scene they appear. Guardrails for build, complication, and tempo. md` (sentence pulse), , .

## Light

Job: keep unresolved pressure alive across scenes — complicate more than you resolve.

Contract: every scene makes things worse or different; tempo breathes (tension inhale / release exhale).

Must-not-break:
- Protect threads that must stay messy
- Prefer complication over solution when a problem arises
- Yes-but / No-and > pure success/failure
- Tell phase temperature (early simmer ≠ climax converge)
- Plant seeds; pay off late
- Vary tempo — breath scenes and speed-ups

## Normal

**Core problem (AI / rush drafts)**
- Helpful training → solve problems immediately
- Betrayal → confrontation → emotional talk → moved on, all in one scene
- Events without momentum: introductions + resolutions stacked; no slow burn

**Protect unresolved threads**
Before a scene, name what must stay messy:
- Tension between A and B is **not** resolved; they keep circling
- Mystery deepens, not answered
- Scene is about growing suspicion, not confrontation

**Complicate, don’t resolve**
- Every scene makes things **worse** or **different**, not better/resolved
- Partial success that creates a new issue; success always has a cost or catch

**Yes, but / No, and**
- **Yes, but** — works, but something goes wrong or new surfaces
- **No, and** — fails, and something else gets worse
- Pure success/failure rare

**Vary tempo**
- Breath scenes: slow, character-focused, no plot advancement
- Speed-up: short sentences, quick location cuts
- Uncomfortable conversation: long; don’t rush to the point
- After action → quiet; after calm → ramp

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Same-scene introduce+resolve | Protect one open thread |
| Every scene same baseline | Phase temperature note |
| No breathers | Insert quiet with micro-tension |
| Instant solutions | Add complication / cost |

## Hard

**Story temperature (arcs, not scenes)**
- AI has no Act 1/2/3 awareness; every scene starts from the same baseline
- Early — conflicts emerge, not confronted; simmer
- Approaching midpoint — tensions surface; alliances tested
- Building to climax — everything converges
- Update a persistent phase note as the story moves

**Plant seeds, pay off late**
- Minor detail that could matter later; offhand mention; slightly out-of-place environment detail
- Later: “Remember X from chapter 1? It matters now.”

**Throw rocks — force growth with stress**
- Characters resist the hard unknown; make manageable → **untenable** so they must move
- Emotion amplifiers (pain, pressure, scrutiny, exhaustion, danger, hunger, burnout…): add strain until volatility — rash mistakes **or** courage past fear
- Growth payoffs of stress: self-awareness; standing up under overload; caution after false flags; honing skills under peril
- Amplifiers also listed under One Stop thesaurus 

**Self-test**
In last few scenes: how many conflicts were introduced **and** resolved in the same scene? If most — protect one thread next; let characters carry it without discussing it.
