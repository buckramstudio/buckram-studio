# MacGuffin craft

Object, goal, secret, or event that **motivates pursuit** — while the audience’s real investment is conflict, character, and cost. Distinct from Chekhov’s gun (economy of detail) and from clue/prop that must mean something at reveal.

## Light

Job: give characters something to chase so conflict moves — without making the object’s lore the story.

Contract: necessary to plot motion and motivation; audience may care more about people than the thing.

Must-not-break:
- Introduce early enough to drive pursuit
- Attach human cost (who lies / dies / breaks trust for it)
- Don’t let lore lectures outshine pursuit
- Pure MacGuffin: identity often interchangeable; Lucas-leaning: aura OK if meaning still lives in what people *do*
- Multiple MacGuffins risk plot-coupon fatigue
- End beats on consequence of having / losing it

## Normal

**Definition (Hitchcock / MacPhail)**
- Term from screenwriter Angus MacPhail; popularized by Hitchcock
- Hitchcock paraphrase: the thing the spies are after, but the audience doesn’t care
- Necessary to plot motion and character motivation; often interchangeable in kind
- May fade, return at climax, or matter less once human stakes dominate
- Precursors often cited: Holy Grail, Golden Fleece — desire-object that starts the journey

**Two audience contracts**

| Mode | Audience care | Craft job |
|------|---------------|-----------|
| Pure / Hitchcock | Low — chase & people matter | Keep object vague enough; no lore outshining pursuit |
| Lucas-leaning | Higher — object has aura / consequence | Meaning still in what people do for it |

**MacGuffin vs neighbors**

| Device | Job |
|--------|-----|
| MacGuffin | Excuse for conflict; identity secondary |
| Chekhov gun | Introduced element must earn keep / fire later |
| Plant / payoff | Neutral detail later reclassified |
| Clue | Fair information toward puzzle solution |
| Red herring | Fair mislead with resolvable wrongness |
| Plot coupon (derisive) | Collect tokens → ending; shopping list, not drama |

A MacGuffin *can* become a Chekhov object if the story later needs its specific nature — then you drift toward meaningful artifact.

**Prose ops**
- Want + opposition early; delay full explanation of the object
- Mystery/thriller: dataset, sample, exploit — keep human cost attached
- Worksheet adjacency: 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Lore dump about the object | Cut to pursuit / moral invoice |
| Audience forgets why anyone cares | Refresh cost of having / not having |
| Powers rewrite climax mid-scene | Plant rules earlier or keep powers irrelevant |
| Quest tokens, no character change | Re-center Want/Need; object is excuse |

## Hard

**Hitchcock vs Lucas (named debate)**
- Hitchcock: audience indifference to the *thing* is a feature — energy on suspense between people
- Lucas-leaning reception: the object can carry mythic weight (plans / Force-adjacent relics) — still fails if character cost is absent
- Diagnostic: if swapping the object’s identity would collapse theme, you’re not in pure-MacGuffin mode — plant rules and pay fairly

**Fair-play boundaries**
- Mystery: if the “MacGuffin” is also the solution key, it owes clue logic  — can’t be pure vagueness at the reveal
- Coincidence that delivers the object without pursuit cost = deus cousin 
- Chekhov economy still applies to side details hanging around the chase
