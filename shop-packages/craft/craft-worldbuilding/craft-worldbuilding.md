# Worldbuilding craft

Depth over width; story-serving setting; hollow iceberg.

## Light

Job: build only what the story needs — then *hint* the rest so readers invent depth.

Contract: setting, culture, and systems support arcs; they don’t decorate them.

Must-not-break:
- Hollow iceberg / steel-bottomed tip: fake the mass; do the tip well
- Expand what you have before adding something new (Sanderson Third Law)
- Small concrete expert detail > page of general description
- Characters take expertise for granted — no tourist lectures
- Expo *timing* lives in 

## Normal

**Hollow iceberg (vs full iceberg myth)**
- Classic advice: build vast hidden lore; show tip. Pros often **fake** the mass
- Worldbuilder’s disease: endless cuisine/maps before drafting — fine as hobby, paralyzing as pro path
- One horse-knowing line beats a horse essay

**Sanderson Third Law — expand, don’t add**
Three moves:
1. **Extrapolate** — “what happens when…” through society, war, gender, economics, faith (scoped to story length)
2. **Interconnect** — powers/cultures/themes feel of a piece
3. **Streamline** — combine; new culture’s reaction to *existing* magic; schism vs brand-new religion

Deep > wide. First/Second Laws remain in .

**Serve story checklist**
- Cultural elements matter to *these* characters
- Physical elements impact plot/setting now
- Setting/culture/magic support arcs
- Balance wonder with sensory grounding for invented worlds

## Hard

**Third Law stress tests**
| Temptation | Expand-don’t-add move |
|------------|----------------------|
| New magic for Act 3 problem | Extrapolate edge case of existing system |
| New culture as costume parade | Interconnect via trade/war/faith with existing peoples |
| Ten unique magic systems | Streamline: one system, ten local uses |
| Encyclopedia chapter mid-chase | Illusion of depth via competence detail; move dump to hunger beat |

**Laws interaction (fantasy)**
- First Law (capability from understanding) + Second (limits > powers) live in — Third is the anti-bloat law that keeps those two playable
- Soft magic at climax still fails Aristotle/Sanderson-ish causality  even if the iceberg is gorgeous

**Research ↔ iceberg**
- Story-critical facts get Round 3 depth ; decorative lore stays hollow tip
- Secondary-world: research real analogues for texture, then obey *your* rules
