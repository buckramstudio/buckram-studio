## Light

Job: use dream/vision only when waking plot changes — subconscious pressure, omen, trauma cost, or genre engine.

Contract: if the waking plot is unchanged without the dream, cut it.

Must-not-break:
- Prefer brief (paragraphs to ~1 page) unless dream *is* the plot engine
- Dream logic = emotional/symbolic coherence — not lava-lamp chaos
- One or two recurring images that mutate
- Signal in/out; waking beat is characterization
- Don’t rug-pull pages then “and then she woke up” as the only twist
- Magical-realism blur OK only if the contract is trained early

## Normal

**Valid jobs**
- Reveal subconscious fear / desire the character won’t say aloud
- Compress theme / Lie–Need pressure into image
- Soft foreshadow (omen, not spoiler neon)
- Trauma recurrence as cost (not reenactment porn)
- Genre engine: prophetic / shared / monitored dreams — rules on-page

**Length and dream logic**
- Atmospheric short; longer only if load-bearing
- Sensory hyper-real or floaty distance — stay consistent within one dream
- Stream-of-consciousness scraps OK in short bursts

**Signaling in / out**
- White space, tense/distance shift, or altered rhythm — avoid italics-for-every-dream default
- Waking: gasp, denial, residue action that changes the day
- Contain the dream; pay the cost in waking life

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Mood-only flex | Attach waking consequence |
| Exposition dump in dream | Move facts to dramatized waking scene |
| “It was all a dream” chapter cheat | Cut or make the dream’s cost real |
| Multi-page surreal tour | Compress to one emotional through-line |
| Symbol billboard every object | Trust one motif |

## Hard

**Continuity cheat vs earned ambiguity**
- Retroactive “it was a dream” to undo major plot (TV *Dallas* Bobby-death season) is the notorious weak form — prose inherits the same reader rage
- Ambiguous dream/reality (*Wizard of Oz* reception lineage) can work when the status is left intentionally unresolved and thematically load-bearing — not as a save for broken continuity

**Subjective dream vs “dreamed world” camera**
- Film often shows dreams as an objectively observed space the character walks through; real dreaming is usually subjective
- Prose can choose either: tight FID inside the dreamer, or a secondary “world” with its own geography — pick and train the contract
- Resonances between dream imagery and diegetic reality carry the meaning (anxiety, desire) without breaking narrator control

**When to break the “keep it short” rule**
- Shared / monitored / prophetic dream systems in SFF — length earns itself if rules and waking stakes escalate (`genre-*` ownership)
- Still: Midpoint / Climactic Moment rarely belong *only* inside an unearned dream erase
