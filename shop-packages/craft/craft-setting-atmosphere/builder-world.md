# Builder: world

Checklists for constructing story-serving settings (depth over width). Magic → . Scene-scale place → .

## Light

Job: build only the iceberg tip the story needs — stop when lore doesn’t pressure Want now.

Contract: cultural/physical elements matter to *these* characters and impact plot; wonder balanced with sensory grounding.

Must-not-break:
- Serve-story gate before more lore
- One concrete expert detail beats a page of general description
- Characters take expertise for granted (no tourist “let’s do magic/physics”)
- Expand-don’t-add before inventing a second system
- If story could happen almost anywhere with a paint swap → use , not a full bible
- Load-bearing facts capped (≈3 max per domain) with the beat that needs them

## Normal

**Serve-story gate (before more lore)**
- [ ] Cultural elements matter to *these* characters
- [ ] Physical elements impact plot/setting **now**
- [ ] Setting / culture / (magic) support arcs — not decorate them
- [ ] Wonder balanced with sensory grounding
- [ ] One concrete expert detail beats a page of general description

If mostly “no,” stop adding cuisine lore; draft the scene that needs the tip of the iceberg.

**Expand-don’t-add (Third Law) worksheet**

Pick **one** existing element. Fill:

| Move | Questions | Your answers |

**Setting-as-character quick test**

- Recurring place, or road-trip never returns?
- Could the story happen almost anywhere with a paint swap?
- What *fundamentally* changes if the place changes?

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Worldbuilder’s disease | Serve-story gate; draft tip scene |
| Kitchen-sink systems | Third Law worksheet on one element |
| Generic backdrop | Stage cards, not full bible |

## Hard

**When full bible vs stage-only**
- Multi-location Act Two / series / secondary-world → bible stubs + location catalog ( if present)
- Single contemporary apartment drama → stage cards + minimal stubs

**Advanced diagnostics**

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Tourist exposition | Expertise not taken for granted | Cut explainers; keep expert detail |
| Lore without pressure | No beat column filled | Attach facts to Want |
| Second magic impulse | Width addiction | Expand ramifications first  |

**Series continuity:** history scar + hard locks migrate into series bible .
