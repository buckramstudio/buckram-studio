# Crowd / social-space set-piece

Parties, balls, markets, rallies, weddings, galas, stadiums, street festivals — many bodies in one social space as a **battle of attention, status, and exits**. Fight geography: . Cast staging: . Spectacle dial: . Meal-as-engine (table lock): .

## Light

Job: turn a crowded social space into a mini-story with public stakes and one private Want.

Contract: the crowd is pressure weather (surveillance, rumor, applause, crush, anonymity) — not a guest roll-call.

Must-not-break:
- Name the moment (“The Toast,” “The Walk-In”) — not “the party”
- One POV camera; crowd filtered through noise, faces, heat
- Status moves on a clock (toasts, songs, seating, kickoff)
- Opposition has a goal *in this room*
- Mid-book gala must not dwarf climax (ceiling)
- Aftermath: rumor residue / alliance shift / cannot-unsee

## Normal

**Social geography (prose camera)**

Track like a fight; weapons are status and sightlines:

1. **Entries / exits** — who can leave without cost; blocked doors; VIP rope
2. **Zones** — dance floor vs wallflowers, kitchen vs parlor, pitch vs stands
3. **Sightlines & overhearing** — who sees whom; rumor as projectile
4. **Density** — crush vs sparse awkward space (both can trap)
5. **Ritual script** — toast, anthem, first dance, kickoff — manners as timer
6. **Cast choreography** — who *must* be present for the turn

**Design checklist**
1. Name the moment
2. POV limitation
3. One private Want loaded into the public ordeal
4. Status moves on a clock
5. Opposition in the room
6. Aftermath / sequel cost
7. Ceiling vs climax

**Modes (genre lean, not ownership)**

| Mode | Crowd does | Watch for |
|------|------------|-----------|
| Status theater (ball, gala) | Display / marriage market / patronage | Costume inventory without turn |
| Humiliation / comedy | Public risk, misread signals | Cruelty without character invoice |
| Performance / game | Stage or scoreboard as set-piece | Unreadable without audio/visual — translate to prose |
| Mob / protest / riot | Collective will + panic | Faceless mass; pick faces + POV cost |
| Market / festival | Information hunt in noise | Tourism montage  |

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Roll-call of guests | Cut to conflict pair; widen on turns |
| Atmosphere only | Add Want + status reallocation |
| Whiplash between faces | Stronger POV tether; fewer named walk-ons |
| Spectacle without intimacy | Invoice private cost in the crush |
| Info dump in the room | Plant/pay across the room  |
| Same beat as food file | Plate/lock-in is the engine → |

## Hard

**Film term → prose social space**
- Film “set piece”: sequence needing complex logistics / built set — justified expense → high-payoff moment audiences remember
- Novel transplant: memorable *and* load-bearing — quiet books can use ballroom confrontation or wedding toast as the set-piece
- May or may not be plot-integral: Bond-style cold-open spectacle vs dramatic film set-pieces at major plot points (pay-off / resolution / transition)
- Hitchcock “crescendoes” / “bumps”: roughly a few big peaks per story — schedule crowd peaks so mid-book does not steal climax image

**Ceiling vs intimacy (don’t confuse dials)**
- Raising public scale ≠ raising stakes — intimate irreversible cost can outrank the crush (, )
- Amp checks from set-piece craft still apply: mini-story through-line, named theme of the moment, every speaking character’s goal, antagonist present in the room

**When the table owns the engine**
- If locked proximity + manners + plate business drive the turn → food set-piece lane, even inside a party room
