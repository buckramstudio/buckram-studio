# Antagonist craft

Opposition that creates plot *and* presses Lie/Want. md` and .

## Light

Job: make opposition consistent, motivated, and thematically relevant — not “mean because bad.”

Contract: every forward plot needs antagonistic force; not every story needs a villain.

Must-not-break:
- Antagonistic force ≠ optional villain label
- Antag Want independent of “ruin the hero”
- Watertight motivation / why they believe they’re right
- Opposition chains toward confrontation (not random side quests)
- Best opponents make protag briefly think: *What if they’re right?*
- If antag vanished, Want would succeed immediately — structural test

## Normal

**Antagonist vs villain**
- **Antagonistic force** = whatever consistently creates obstacles to the protagonist’s goal → conflict → plot
- Need not be a person (weather, system, illness, asteroid, thematically unified “bad luck”)
- **Villain** = morally reprehensible actor — optional; degree varies
- Roles can split: upright antagonist, off-spine villain, protagonist-as-villain

**Start with the antagonist (organic conflict)**
Brainstorm:
1. Who / what is the antagonistic force?
2. What do they Want? (not merely “stop the hero”)
3. Why — watertight motivation; Ghost; why they believe they’re right?
4. What’s their plan — and how does it interfere with the protag’s plan?
5. Thematic significance of that goal vs protag’s values

**Worthy opponent**
- Hero of their own story: clear Want, plan, justification
- Shared traits / same dilemma, different approach (Truby: contrast powerful *because of* similarities)
- Shared or mirrored goal → reason they keep colliding
- Tempting reasoning — bogus argument → shallow temptation
- Competence: seamless obstacle chain toward final confrontation

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Mean because bad | Independent Want + justification |
| Manufactured blockers after cool hero moves | Plot from antag agenda first |
| Vanished Act-Two ghost | Consistent force through major beats |
| Inner wound disconnected from opposition | Unify outer and inner opponent |

## Hard

**Force types / stake layers** (esp. epic)
1. **Global** — world-ending / impersonal threat
2. **International** — nation vs nation; personify the driving opponent
3. **National** — within the polity
4. **Public** — daily circle: work, family, friends who oppose goals
5. **Personal / relational** — closest relationship characters
- Personified antagonist vs abstract force (both can coexist)
- Contagonist (Dramatica-ish): nearer subplot opposition / “devil on shoulder” vs distant Big Bad

**Pressure on Lie / Want / Need**
- Common Positive-Change: antag as **avatar of the Lie** (or its worst negation); obstacles force confrontation with Lie’s limits → Truth
- Alternate: Truth-aligned antagonist (often relationship) who “breaks” the protag into seeing Lie is unsustainable
- Flat Arc: Truth-holding protag vs Lie-ridden world/force
- Negative arcs: roles invert; antag may embody Truth the protag rejects
- Inner antagonist first: name the demon; then choose exterior force that symbolizes and catalyzes it
- Climax resonance: defeating exterior opponent ↔ defeating (or surrendering to) the inner foe

**Practical checks**
- Opposition consistent through major structural beats?
- Each major obstacle chain from the same force/agenda?
- Pinch Points / Midpoint show the force’s power?
