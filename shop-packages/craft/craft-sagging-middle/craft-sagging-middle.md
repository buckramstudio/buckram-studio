# Sagging middle (Act Two)

Second act dies when pursuit replaces transformation. Pinch Points + try/fail that cost internally.

## Light

Job: keep Act Two transforming the protagonist — not just chasing the goal for a hundred pages.

Contract: every middle stretch either pushes change, resists it, or makes avoidance cost something.

Must-not-break:
- Pursuit alone sustains ~30 pages, not a hundred
- Bigger bombs without internal cost = noise
- Midpoint is a mirror/hinge, not only a twist
- Try/fail must make the *next* attempt psychologically harder
- Subplots earn space only if they pressure the same wound

## Normal

**Diagnosis tells**
- Wander / talk-in-rooms / prepare-for-things-that-never-happen
- Two middle chapters end in essentially the same situation
- Reader could skip three middle chapters and still follow
- External “raise the stakes” without internal cost after ~ch.12

**Transformation engine**
- Enter Act Two as one person; exit fundamentally different
- Scene test: how is the protagonist *internally* different at scene end?
- Fail test → cut, combine, or rebuild around an internal shift
- If they can stroll into climax unchanged from ch.4, middle ran on fumes

**Midpoint as mirror / hinge**
- Glimpse who they must become, or who they’ve pretended not to be
- Strategies that worked in first half of Act Two stop working
- Before midpoint: retreat to old self still possible
- After: retreat means destruction
- Genre flavors: hunter becomes hunted; walls drop; truth can no longer be outrun

**Try–fail that doesn’t bore**
- Logistical fail (“door locked → window”) = delay costume, not cycle
- Prefer **“no, and…”** over plain **“no”** — alerts villain / burns resource / closes option
- Good fails: lose ally → more alone; expose weakness → more vulnerable; force forbidden methods → irreversible compromise
- Victory can escalate too (new obligation; rescue creates debt)
- Vary obstacle *type*, not only size
- Path narrows until climax choice is transform or be destroyed

**Subplots**
Deep weave: . Dual engines: .
- Earn space only if they pressure the *same* internal wound from a new angle
- Unrelated theme thread = channel-surf clutter
- Romance fear-of-commitment must force commitment choices elsewhere, or cut

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Same situation chapter after chapter | Change internal position or cut |
| Louder rival, same argument | Vary obstacle type; add cost |
| Midpoint only plot twist | Add mirror: who they must become |
| Skip-three-chapters still clear | Rebuild around internal shift |

## Hard

**Pinch Points (~37% / ~62%)**
- Remind of antagonistic force’s power to stymie the goal; foreshadow Midpoint / 3PP / Climax
- Can be antagonist-POV only (protagonist may not know) — still reorients reader attention
- Best ones create distinctive moments that influence every scene until the next Plot Point

**First Pinch (~37%, between 1PP and Midpoint)**
- After reaction scramble post–First Plot Point
- Character feels the pinch; gets new *clues* about the battle (not full Midpoint truth yet)
- Swings into growing realization → Midpoint Moment of Truth

**Second Pinch (~62%, between Midpoint and 3PP)**
- After Midpoint, character is in action mode and making headway
- Slap with **what’s at stake** / how much they’re willing to pay
- Less about new clues; more about cost
- Fuels renewed push → seeming victory at end of Act Two → 3PP greatest defeat (stakes foreshadowed here)

**Advanced diagnostic**
| Symptom | Likely cause | Pass |
|---------|--------------|------|
| Middle is “busy but empty” | No internal shift per scene | Transformation scene test |
| Escalation feels fake | External only | Tie each fail to wound cost |
| Midpoint limp | No mirror / no strategy death | Rewrite hinge |
| Act Two ends soft | Weak 2nd Pinch / missing cost slap | Restake before 3PP |
