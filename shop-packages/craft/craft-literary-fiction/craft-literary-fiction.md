# Literary / general fiction

Engine: language, interiority, and theme — more than plot machinery alone.

## Light

Job: aim the page at consciousness, style, and thematic resonance without pretending plot is optional noise.

Contract: character-driven / human-condition focus (market + critical positioning) — techniques travel into genre shelves too.

Must-not-break:
- Plot may exist; sentence-level craft and interiority carry weight
- Theme argued through experience, not thesis stickers 
- FID / psychic distance tools live in — don’t reinvent here
- “Literary” ≠ morally superior; often trade/critical category
- Slow linger allowed if voice earns it — dawdle without losing the thread

## Normal

**Stance (durable definitions)**
- More character-driven than plot-driven; examines human condition; often framed as “serious art” vs market genre categories
- May involve social commentary / political criticism; may prioritize style and layered language (Saricks: elegantly written, lyrical, layered)
- Rafferty: literary fiction may dawdle on stray beauties even at risk of losing its way — pacing permission with a cost
- Boundaries blur: literary techniques inside romance/SF/etc.; Austen “literary romance,” Atwood speculative, slipstream between

## Hard

**Category debates (decision-changing)**
- James Gunn: commercial mainstream (bestsellers) vs literary mainstream (academic-critical community: professors, prestige review organs, writers who take them seriously); high sales sometimes read as “selling out”
- Genre communities often call literary “mainstream” / mundane — Stableford/Gunn notes on SF indifference-to-science critique cut both ways
- Updike resistance: “literary fiction” as limiting label; all written works are “literary” in the trivial sense — craft takeaway: don’t let the shelf label dictate voice jobs you aren’t doing
- High culture lineage (Arnold “best that has been thought and said”) vs mass culture — useful historically; dangerous as moral ranking for working writers

**Prize / canon pressure**
- Nobel / Booker ecosystems shape what counts as “outstanding literary fiction”; judging-by-committee contested (Chaudhuri critique of annual “best book”)
- Classic/canon lists ≠ synonym for living literary fiction practice — use for conversation, not paint-by-numbers

**When to break “linger” permission**
- If reader loses the dramatic question for pages with no micro-tension , dawdle has failed Rafferty’s risk
- Hybrid shelves: keep category promises (clues, HEA, novum) while running literary sentence/interiority jobs
