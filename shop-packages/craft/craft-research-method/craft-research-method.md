# Research method for fiction

Just-enough research for verisimilitude — not academic completeness.

## Light

Job: get enough true texture to write the scene without turning research into the book.

Contract: fiction research = **evocation**; know more than you show (iceberg).

Must-not-break:
- Reader-pause test: would a typical reader stop and question this?
- Stop when you can write the scene without looking things up — not when every question is answered
- Two accurate details beat a manual dump
- Placeholders (`TK` / `[CHECK:]`) beat perfection paralysis
- Don’t use other novels/films as “fact”

## Normal

**Goal**
- Evocation for the page, not textbook representation
- Iceberg: surplus knowledge off-page; on-page = what POV notices under pressure

**Phased method**

| Round | When | Depth |
|-------|------|-------|
| **1 Orientation** | Before / early draft | Period, tech, politics, social norms — enough not to be wildly wrong |
| **2 Texture** | During draft | Lived-in detail for scenes on the page; light; no info-dumps |
| **3 Story-critical** | When plot hinges on a fact | Weather-on-Tuesday depth *only* if the storm is load-bearing |
| **Verification** | Late draft / continuity | Catch drift, contradictions, early placeholders |

Emma Darwin two-step: free “reading round” that shapes ideas → revisit the same material once you know what you actually need.

**Stop rules**
- Time-box research before a draft start date; allow mid-draft targeted lookups
- Balance consumption ↔ creation: after enough for a place/event/person, write a scene about it

**Note → scene pipeline**
1. **Capture** — short notes with source (book/page/URL/date)
2. **Tag by scene need** — which chapter/scene/decision needs this?
3. **Translate to sensory/choice** — what would *this POV* notice under pressure?
4. **Inject one or two specifics**; leave the rest in the iceberg
5. **Mark uncertainty** and keep drafting 
6. **Verification pass** — resolve TKs; continuity against bible ( / )

**Organization**
- Be able to **refind** the source; phone scraps → structured notes later
- Prefer primary / specialist sources for procedures (locks, medicine, law) over movie logic
- Genre reading sets reader expectations  — it is not world research

| Trap | Symptom | Fix |
|------|---------|-----|
| Research as procrastination | Hours of trivia, zero pages | Round 1 only; then draft; park holes |
| Tangent hoarding | Notes never touch plot/character | If it doesn’t serve story, drop |
| Info-dump pride | “I researched this so it stays” | Iceberg; appendix/author note if you must share |
| Fiction-as-source | Other novels/films as fact | Trace to nonfiction; avoid third-hand flatness + plagiarism risk |
| Perfection paralysis | Can’t write until 100% accurate | Placeholders; verification later |

## Hard

**Story-critical vs texture (decision rule)**
- If wrongness would break the plot hinge (tide times, poison dose, court procedure) → Round 3 depth before that scene ships
- If wrongness is only “a specialist would sneer” and no hinge depends on it → texture-level + verification note; don’t stall draft
- Hollow-iceberg failure : researched lore never touches character Want — research must change a choice or a sensory beat

**Velocity coupling**
- Drafting-velocity rule: placeholders for research when beat sheet is ready  — research method supplies the TK grammar, not an excuse to stop
- Snowflake “composting” (informal research/daydream) precedes design docs  — don’t confuse compost binge with Round 3

**Source hierarchy (ops)**
| Need | Prefer | Avoid as sole authority |
|------|--------|-------------------------|
| Procedures (law/med/locks) | Specialist / primary / practitioner | Movies, other novels |
| Place/climate/demographics | Factbook / climate / stats feeds  | Travel-blog vibes only |
| Consistency Q&A | StackExchange worldbuilding as *prompt*, then verify | Treating forum answers as canon |
| Lived texture | Memoirs, oral history, period newspapers | Aggregator listicles |

**When “enough” is a thematic choice**
- Historical / biofic / hard-SF contracts raise the pause-test bar — still iceberg on the page
- Magical / secondary-world: research real analogues for texture, then obey *your* rules ( Sanderson pointer via worldbuilding)
