# Curiosity and information-gap craft

Hunger to know — as a toolkit. Macro tension: .

## Light

Job: open specific questions and time their answers so pages pull forward.

Contract: every open question is a promise — payoff or trust dies.

Must-not-break:
- Specify the question (vague dread ≠ gap)
- Partial answers that raise new questions = sweet spot
- Answer too fast → deflates; starve too long → lost, not intrigued
- Create hunger before explanation
- Chapter ends: open question / shift / omen — full resolution is an exit ramp

## Normal

**Gap types**

| Gap | Reader itch | Craft note |
|-----|-------------|------------|
| **Mystery fact** | What is true? | Fair plants if puzzle-promised |
| **Motive** | Why did they? | Delay full why; show costly behavior |
| **Outcome** | Will it work? | Clock + care first |
| **Dramatic irony** | They don’t know — we do | Dread > surprise for sustained pull |
| **Self-gap** | Character blind to self | Pay off with recognition cost |
| **Ellipsis** | What happened off-page? | Aftermath must invoice |

**Valve control**
- Raise a **specific** question → make them wait
- Information as valve: strategic disclosure = informed uncertainty
- Exposition timing: hunger before explanation 

**Scene / chapter ops**
- After each scene: what question is the reader asking? None → weak forward pull
- Prologues must create questions, not dump answers 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Vague “something’s wrong” forever | Specify the question |
| Infodump kills curiosity | Withhold + dramatize need-to-know |
| Orphan questions at end | Pay off or cut the plant |
| Irony without care | Invest before the bomb-under-table |

## Hard

**Dread vs surprise (choose on purpose)**
- Dramatic irony / dread: reader knows more → anticipation; Hitchcock bomb-under-table for sustained suspense
- Surprise: reader and character learn together → must be earned by prior clues, not cheat resets
- Layer: irony that friend is betrayer + later surprise that betrayal was protective = redefine relationship
- Genre lean (tendency): thriller/horror/tragedy often irony; mystery/comedy/twist psych often surprise

**Micro-tension under the gap (Maass)**
- Moment-by-moment apprehension about the *next few seconds*, not only the big plot question
- Quiet scenes die without micro-friction even when a macro mystery is open
- Conflicting emotions / subtext / incongruent detail keep the valve from going slack between reveals

**Contract diagnostics**
| Symptom | Cause | Move |
|---------|-------|------|
| Skimming mid-book | Macro Q open, micro dead | Add moment-level friction |
| Rage-quit at twist | Surprise without plants | Rebuild fair ledger |
| Numb to dread | Irony without care or endless max tension | Invest; rhythm release |
