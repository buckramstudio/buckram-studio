# Frame / nested narrative

Outer story that contains one or more inner tales — sandwich, nesting dolls, or recursive frames.

## Light

Job: give the telling itself a dramatic reason so the inner tale(s) arrive under pressure.

Contract: the outer layer is not throat-clear — finishing (or interrupting) the inner tale changes the outer world.

Must-not-break:
- Reason to tell (crisis, request, discovery, trial, fire-circle, found papers)
- Stakes in the present for teller and/or listeners
- Return beats that shift belief, clue, or relationship
- Clear entry/exit signals per nest
- One unmarked layer open at a time

Not the same as: single flashback interrupt  or equal braid .

## Normal

**What counts**
- Introductory/main narrative sets the stage for a more emphasized second narrative or a set of shorter stories
- Leads readers from a first story into one or more stories within it
- May inform readers about aspects of the secondary narrative that would otherwise be hard to understand
- To be a frame narrative, the outer story must act primarily as an *occasion for the telling*

**Oregon State reader questions (always ask)**
1. Why is the story being told?
2. What appeals does the story make to its implied audience of listeners?
3. How do the main story’s themes relate to the themes of the frame?

**Architectures**

| Type | Shape | Use when |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Single-story frame** | Outer bookends (+ optional interrupts) | One main inner novel with a teller |
| **Anthology / set frame** | Outer hosts many tales | Theme braid; *Nights* / Canterbury / Decameron energy |
| **Chinese box / multi-nest** | Story in story in story (*Frankenstein*, *Heart of Darkness*) | Rare; clarity cost rises fast |
| **Document / false-document frame** | Found MS, letters, archive, “Historical Notes” | Cross-link |
| **Dream vision** | Narrator sleeps, dreams, awakes to tell | Medieval convention; modern fantasy use contested |

**Nesting-doll transitions**
1. **Trigger** — tangible hinge (journal, “tell me,” tape starts)
2. **Entry signal** — tense/voice/format shift the reader can learn once
3. **Inner job** — one clear task per nest (reveal, reframe, deepen motive)
4. **Exit signal** — snap outer shut (reaction, new question, contradiction)
5. **One doll open at a time** — don’t stack three unmarked layers mid-scene

**Design checklist**
1. Outer conflict *now* — what fails if the telling stops?
2. What does each inner tale change in the outer?
3. Plants that pay in the *other* layer 
4. Distinct voice / knowledge limits for the teller 
5. Convergence — frame and core collide (revelation, choice, or ironic echo)

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Outer vanishes after page 3 | Keep intermittent return beats with cost |
| Reader lost on layer | Consistent signals; fewer nests |
| Inner tales are expo dumps | Give each a mini turn  |
| Frame never pays off | Design collision / reclassification climax |
| Dual-timeline cosplay | Commit to equal braid or shrink past to interrupt |

## Hard

**Named jobs of the outer layer**
| Job | Effect | Classic cue |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| **Cast doubt on narrator** | Distance author from teller; mark tall-tale risk | Fly-fisherman Mulliner; *Amadeus* as Salieri’s story |
| **Procatalepsis** | Put reader resistance into a listener | Grandson in *Princess Bride*; town gossip in *Ethan Frome* |
| **False documents** | Explain how the inner story was “found” | Jekyll letters; *Handmaid’s Tale* Historical Notes |
| **Mise en abyme** | Inner tale mirrors/encapsulates frame aspect | Nested *Nights* sets; Ovid nesting |
| **Recurrent framing image** | Bookend return under changed circumstances | Same place/ritual seen anew (framing device ≈ reprise) |

**Dream-vision edge (Tolkien)**
- Medieval dream frames often marked fiction/revelation; Bunyan used prison dream as divine warrant
- Tolkien (*On Fairy-Stories*) complained dream frames can signal unwillingness to treat the marvels seriously — if you use one, know you may undercut fantasy’s claim
- Oz film dream-frame vs book’s “real” Oz: frame choice changes validity of the marvel

**Anthology-frame politics**
- Pilgrim/teller identity colors the tale and the *next* teller’s response (Chaucer Miller → Reeve) — frame is social combat, not wallpaper
- Scheherazade: restlessness of embedded heroes serves survival *and* must not fully license the husband’s wandering/kill cycle — theme handshake between layers

**Chinese-box diagnostic**
- *Frankenstein*: Walton letters → Victor → Creature → brief family observation — each layer needs a Want; drop a layer if it only dumps lore
- If the memory/telling is longer than the outer present but the outer present is still the greater interest (Odyssey/Phaeacians pattern), keep outer stakes alive or the frame dies
