# Supporting roles craft

Mentor, sidekick, foil, and other load-bearing secondaries — as jobs, not Jung sticker packs.

## Light

Job: give every named secondary a pressure function the protagonist can’t get elsewhere.

Contract: role = what they make the lead face — plus private Want.

Must-not-break:
- Answer: what do they force that no one else can?
- Own conflicting goals + mini-arc vs theme
- Mentor must not solve the climax
- Sidekick ≠ village idiot; don’t hide fair-play thoughts
- Foil shares enough DNA that comparison hurts

## Normal

**Role = pressure function**

| Role | Load-bearing job | Rot pattern |
|------|------------------|-------------|
| **Mentor** | Skills / worldview / permission — often failed or costly path | Info NPC; immortal correctness |
| **Sidekick / confidant** | Access, banter, sanity check, intel lead can’t get alone | Comic only; no private GMC |
| **Foil** | Living alternate self / method / ethics | Trait opposite, no shared problem |
| **Rival** | Same Want, conflicting claim | Petty annoyance without stakes |
| **Love interest** | Intimacy gradient + choice cost | Trophy / healer appliance |
| **Institutional block** | System pressure with a human face | Faceless bureaucracy forever |

**Mentor specifics**
- Wise **or** failed/false sage
- Create dependence then force independence (or betray / die / leave with a bill)
- Dramatize at least one mid-book break as student becomes master

**Sidekick / Watson problem**
- Don’t hide sidekick thoughts if they hold fair-play info
- Useful specialty, not village idiot
- Banter moves status, intel, or loyalty 

**Foil craft**
- Shares enough DNA that comparison hurts; differs on thematic axis
- In triangles, each pole reveals a different possible self
- Resolution proves growth, not coin-flip convenience

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Role badge without private Want | Write their GMC |
| Mentor solves climax | Protégé acts; earned causality |
| Sidekick vanishes when tone darkens | Keep function under pressure |
| Foils with no A↔B relationships | Build bonds between poles |

## Hard

**Knox sidekick constraint (cross-genre lesson)**
- Even outside mystery: if a confidant holds solution-critical info, hiding their thoughts cheats the reader’s experiential fairness
- Intelligence slightly-below-reader (Knox) is a *puzzle* rule — general fiction can have brilliant sidekicks; still don’t use them as hidden author vaults

**Mentor as false sage / Shadow teacher**
- Wise-old-figure archetype can be trap  — betrayal or revealed cost reframes earlier lessons
- Antagonist craft overlap: institutional block / rival may be the true opposing will  — don’t split pressure across five mild helpers

**Cast economy diagnostic**
| Symptom | Cause | Move |
|---------|-------|------|
| Climax solved by advice speech | Mentor overreach | Force protégé choice under fire |
| Banter chapter changes nothing | Sidekick without Want | Give conflicting goal or cut |
| “Opposite” roommate with no shared problem | Fake foil | Share the thematic problem |
