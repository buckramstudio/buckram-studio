# Motivation–Reaction Units (MRUs)

Small-scale prose cause-and-effect inside Scenes and Sequels (Dwight Swain / Ingermanson).

## Light

Job: keep on-page cause and effect in physiological order so the scene feels real.

Contract: Motivation (external) → Reaction (internal, ordered) — wrong order breaks the illusion even if facts are “correct.”

Must-not-break:
- Motivation is outside the POV (camera-recordable)
- Reaction follows Motivation; don’t whip-saw same paragraph
- Inside Reaction: Feeling → Reflex → Rational action/speech (omit freely; keep remaining order)
- Chain MRUs until Scene/Sequel ends
- Tool for clarity — if forced order hurts emotion, adjust

## Normal

**Core model**
- Everything splits into **Motivation** (cause) and **Reaction** (effect)
- Fluff that isn’t an MRU is a cut candidate
- Large-scale Goal–Conflict–Disaster / Reaction–Dilemma–Decision lives *above* MRUs 

**Motivation (external / objective)**
- Outside POV: sight/sound/smell/touch/taste events a camera could record
- Present in objective terms, usually its own paragraph
- Example: *The tiger dropped out of the tree and sprang toward Jack.*

**Reaction (internal / subjective) — order matters**
Physiological time-scale (fast → slow):
1. **Feeling** — instantaneous visceral response
2. **Reflex / instinct** — automatic body move before thought
3. **Rational action + speech** — deliberate move and/or dialogue

Rules:
- Omit one or two parts; keep whatever remains in that order
- Feeling before reflex before rational speech/action
- Reaction in its own paragraph(s) after Motivation

Example Reaction: adrenaline → jerk rifle → sight and fire → “Die, you bastard!”

**Paragraphing and pace**

- Paragraph breaks can mark Motivation ↔ Reaction switches
- Short paragraphs accelerate; longer blocks slow
- Fuzzy/out-of-focus prose often = shuffled MRUs

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Dialogue before visceral hit | Move feeling/reflex earlier |
| Same-paragraph stimulus/response whip-saw | Split paragraphs |
| Endless internal without external Motivation | Put a camera event on the page |
| MRU paint-by-numbers kills voice | Keep order; vary length and omit parts |

## Hard

**Relation to Scene/Sequel**
- Scenes are continuous strings of stimulus/response units
- Disaster at Scene end is still a Motivation for the Sequel’s Reaction
- Sequel Dilemma is cognitive — still often preceded by Feeling; don’t skip visceral cost after real Disaster

**Edit diagnostic (symptom → cause)**
| Symptom | Likely cause | Pass |
|---------|--------------|------|
| “I don’t believe the panic” | Speech before Feeling | Reorder Reaction |
| Fight reads like stage directions | Motivations stacked; no Feeling | Insert visceral beat |
| Melodrama | Feeling without Motivation | Show the tiger |
| Over-explained calm | Rationalizing before Reflex | Cut or delay analysis |

**When to loosen**
- Lyric / literary passages may stretch or bury MRU edges — restore order when clarity or immersion fails
- Omniscient or distant POV can summarize Reactions; close POV should usually dramatize Feeling→action
- Humor timing sometimes inverts expectation; still plant Motivation before the joke lands
