## Light

Job: keep erotic prose embodied, specific, and free of cliché fog that kills heat.

Must-not-break:

## Hard

**Style as category signal**
- Strip test : remove sex/sexual-journey scenes → remaining text should not still be a complete novel — style bans serve that spine (specificity of desire), not moral purity
- Romance / erotic-romance readers expect HEA/HFN; erotica may not — euphemism fog + “sacred union” language often mis-signals romance packaging
- Literary “erotic literature” (arouse ± satire/critique) is broader than commercial novel erotica — these bans target commercial heat prose

**Consent / aftercare as style, not sermon**
- Consent in positioning, staying, asking, guiding — not interrupting for legal-contract speeches unless character-specific
- Aftercare: specific touch, practical care, banter, silence, space — not narrated why-aftercare-matters
- Power-imbalance and CNC/noncon *fantasy* frames need diegetic negotiation or clear fantasy contract  so style doesn’t gaslight in-world reality

**Dark-romance / race edges**
- Daddy/dom aesthetic, humiliation, possession-as-ownership, punishment-for-pleasure — only when established and addressed; otherwise reads as unexamined trope dump
- Food comparisons for skin, “exotic,” racialized animal/jungle metaphors — cut; contrast OK only when grounded in a specific character’s perception and not the scene’s primary fetish focus
