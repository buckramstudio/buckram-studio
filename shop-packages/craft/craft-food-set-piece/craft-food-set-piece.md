# Food set-piece craft

Meals, kitchens, and taste as **story engines** — not menu wallpaper. Shelf engines: . Sensory filters: . Crowd without plate engine: .

## Light

Job: use locked proximity + manners + body business so a meal changes relationship, status, or information.

Contract: recipe dump ≠ scene; dish/service must advance pressure.

Must-not-break:
- Private agenda per load-bearing seat
- Surface topic vs subsurface fight
- At least one food action advances pressure (refuse, spill, undercook, perfect plate, poison)
- POV expertise filter (cook vs guest taste different worlds)
- Aftermath: who they can still eat with
- Mid-book feast must not outshine climax (ceiling)

## Normal

**Why food scenes work**
- Locked proximity — hard to leave; silence is visible
- Script of manners — pass the salt while agendas fight
- Body business — chew, spill, refuse, serve, burn
- Power map — who carves, who cooks, who is served last
- Public judgment — review, feast, competition, investor dinner

**Set-piece jobs (pick a primary)**

| Job | Engine |
|-----|--------|
| Reveal / fracture | One sentence / one plate that reclassifies the room |
| Status war | Seating, toasts, who interrupts |
| Service / kitchen ordeal | Ticket clock, heat, hierarchy |
| Covenant / found-family table | Shared food as bond under cost |
| Memory / inheritance | Taste as Ghost drip — still needs a present turn |
| Ally talk with vibration | Tension + food discomfort + conflicting agendas |

**Design checklist**
1. Private agenda per load-bearing seat
2. Surface vs subsurface fight
3. Physical food channel advances pressure
4. POV expertise filter
5. Course / interrupt rhythm (service, plates cleared, kitchen crash)
6. Aftermath taste / status reallocation
7. Ceiling vs climax

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Talking heads over pasta | Food-as-pressure + agendas |
| Menu prose, no turn | Name the moment; force status shift |
| All taste = “sexy” metaphor | Culture, labor, memory, danger |
| Exotic buffet tourism | Ground cooks/eaters; research |
| Equal camera on twelve guests | Cut to conflict pair |
| Kitchen magic erases labor | Burns, hours, hierarchy on invoice |

## Hard

**Food in literature (durable frame)**
- Food scenes carry culture, class, gender labor, hospitality ethics, and appetite-as-desire — not only sensory garnish
- Banquet / famine / poisoned cup lineages: the plate is often a moral and political instrument, not scenery

**Crowd vs table engine**
- Many bodies in a party room without plate pressure → 
- Table lock (can’t leave, manners timer, serve/refuse) → stay in this note even inside a larger gala

**Culinary-shelf coupling**
- When the book’s engine is restaurant/competition/food-world stakes, each set-piece should invoice labor hierarchy and public judgment  — cozy “found family table” still needs a present turn, not only warmth
