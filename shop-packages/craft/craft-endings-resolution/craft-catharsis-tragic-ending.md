# Catharsis and tragic endings

How loss, pity/fear, and recognition land — without mistaking tears for craft.

## Light

Job: make a hard ending feel necessary and clarifying, not merely sad.

Contract: tragedy (craft sense) = outcome feels necessary from character + situation; recognition and reversal align.

Must-not-break:
- Climactic Moment still ends the pursuit — tragedy doesn’t excuse a muddled stop
- Tools of ruin/survival must be planted; deus ex mercy or doom both cheat
- Pity without fear is soap; fear without pity is spectacle — braid both toward an invested person
- Genre promise binds (HEA/HFN ≠ tragic bait-and-switch)
- Prefer clarification over “did they cry?”

## Normal

**Catharsis (working definitions)**
Aristotle: tragedy works through pity and fear toward *catharsis* of such emotions — phrase famously disputed.

| Reading | Writer implication |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Purgation / release** | Build pressure → allow discharge |
| **Clarification** (often more useful) | Ending makes human limitation / choice **intelligible**; emotion follows understanding |

**Tragic ending vs sad event**
- **Sad:** bad thing happens
- **Tragic (craft):** recognition (*anagnorisis*) and reversal (*peripeteia*) align; agency and flaw/limitation participate — not only random cruelty
- Protagonist may win outer goal and lose soul-cost, or relinquish the goal because Truth finally costs less than the Lie

**Earn the fall**
- Cost visible in Resolution: who remains, what can’t be repaired, what Truth was bought
- Open/ambiguous endings can be tragic if the **tracked** moral/plot question still answers; frustration ≠ catharsis

**Epilogue**
- Short scarred new normal can seal clarification
- Long “everyone’s future” or epilogue that *fixes* the tragedy undoes it
- Series: book-level tragedy only if series contract allows; don’t reboot consequence for free

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Misery pile without recognition | Add anagnorisis — character or reader sees the pattern |
| Random death for shock | Tie death to choice / established threat |
| Tears then joke cancel | Hold silence; comic valve later  |
| Genre bait-and-switch | Align ending type with early promise |
| Author punishment fantasy | Check theme argument  |

## Hard

**Anagnorisis + peripeteia (ops)**
- Best tragic turns: recognition *causes* or coincides with reversal — seeing the truth changes the board
- Reader-only recognition (character stays blind) can still clarify if the tracked question answers — different emotional temperature than shared recognition
- Negative arc / rejection of Truth  is a tragic cousin: clarity for reader, ruin for lead

**Aristotle probability bar (shared with causality craft)**
- Solutions/outcomes should follow necessarily or probably from character + prior incidents — random cruelty fails the tragic contract even if sad 
- Deus ex mercy *or* doom both cheat the fall 

**Pity/fear braid diagnostic**
| Imbalance | Feel | Repair |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Pity only | Sentimentality | Raise agency / complicity |
| Fear only | Horror spectacle | Restore personhood / investment |
| Neither | Flat misery | Rebuild Want + recognition |
