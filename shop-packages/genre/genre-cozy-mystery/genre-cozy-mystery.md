# Cozy mystery

Engine: fair-play puzzle + comfort. Reader wants to revisit the town as much as solve the crime.

Sibling of general mystery fair-play rules . Contrasts hardboiled/noir .

## Light

Job: restore a small community’s order through amateur intellect — without turning the book into gore or procedural grit.

Contract: sex and violence mostly off-stage; amateur sleuth in a socially intimate community; murderer usually a community member hiding in plain sight; closure restores order.

Must-not-break:
- Off-page / low-gore violence; mild language; romance sweet or closed-door
- Sleuth access via day job / social role — not badge-first
- Motive: greed, jealousy, revenge, reputation — human, often old wounds
- Fair-play clues before the reveal 
- Series DNA: ensemble, place rituals, theme (shop/pet/craft) matter

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Late-20th-c reinvention of Golden Age whodunit energy; term “cozy” in use by the 1960s, wider recognition later
- Minimal violence/sex/social-spectacle; solution by intellect/intuition more than police procedure
- Closed community; honorable characters; order restored
- Murder methods often “quieter” (poison, fall); wounds rarely used as forensic spectacle clues
- Boundaries stay vague — thematic hobby/job is characteristic, not mandatory law

**Promise vs hardboiled**

| Cozy | Hardboiled / noir |
|------|-------------------|
| Puzzle + comfort | Atmosphere + moral damage |
| Off-page violence | Violence / grit central |
| Order restored | Partial justice / compromised win |
| Amateur + social intuition | Pro (or antihero) vs corrupt system |

**Sleuth & ensemble**
- Day job grants access (baker, librarian, bookseller, innkeeper, caterer, reporter…)
- Contact on the force is usually spouse/friend/family — not ex-partner-from-the-squad as default
- Personal stake makes “meddling” believable (shop, friend accused, clear-your-name, festival)
- Sidekick/confidant + authority friction + optional love interest (keep evidence ethics clean)
- Culinary / craft / pet cozies: theme is series glue

**Suspect grid & fair play**
- Several suspects with Motive / Means / Opportunity + a personal secret (shady ≠ killer)
- True clues visible before reveal; linchpin early as casual detail
- Gossip as clue vehicle: half-truths; truth in the noise
- Reveal reclassifies what was shown

**Beat spine (typical commercial cozy length ~60–75k)**
1. Hook — sleuth daily life + setting charm
2. Body / entanglement
3. Authority warns “don’t meddle”
4. Interviews + Clue #1 / Herring #1
5. Subplot spark (shop, festival, family, romance)
6. Suspect carousel → false-lead “win”
7. Midpoint twist (new body, alibi collapse, stake spike)
8. Pushback → re-examine Act I detail → danger close
9. Gathering + reveal (culprit slips in character)
10. Afterglow + Book 2 seed

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Comfort essays stall the puzzle | Alternate charm with questions/reversals |
| Serial-killer spectacle | Community member + human motive |
| Quirky caricature | Punch up, not down |
| Police do all the work | Keep amateur intellect load-bearing |

## Hard

**Comfort × fair-play dial**
- Comfort beats after darker turns — but Knox/Van Dine pressure still applies if you sold a puzzle
- If continuous dread/clock dominates comfort → retag toward / domestic suspense
- If gore, moral damage, and system rot dominate → leave cozy for 

**Series recurrence problem**
- Long-running joke risk: sleuth as walking body magnet — lean into comedy *or* vary entanglement so “accident” stays believable
- Stock company of eccentrics: detective as sanity center — don’t let comic relief erase stakes for the victim

**When to break small-town insulation**
- Insular community can be a workplace, festival circuit, or island — size is social, not acreage
- Historic/hobby themes (herbalist, antiques) OK if fair-play and off-stage violence hold
- Boundaries of the subgenre are vague — signal heat/gore level in comps and opening promise
