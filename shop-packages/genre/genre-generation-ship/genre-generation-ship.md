# Generation ship

Engine: closed society under multi-generational voyage pressure — the ship is world; original passengers die en route; descendants inherit a mission they did not choose.

## Light

Job: braid biosphere, society, and meaning stress so the hull is a polity, not a hotel with metal walls.

Contract: centuries of travel; successive generations; scarcity and knowledge drift as friends of the engine.

Must-not-break:
- Ship as world — corridors = geography; maintenance = economy
- Intermediate generations as means — ethical bind on page
- At least two pressure systems braided (biosphere, genetics, society, psychology, engineering, destination risk)
- Origin reveal / Mission heresy / Arrival crisis as available engines
- Avoid infinite perfect recycling
- Children didn’t volunteer — theme vacuum if ignored

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Interstellar ark / world ship; sub-types by scale/speed (Hein et al.): sprinter, slow boat, colony ship, world ship
- Early concepts: Goddard, Tsiolkovsky “Noah’s Ark,” Bernal “globes”
- Classic SF: culture drift, forgotten Earth, tribalization of decks (*Orphans of the Sky*, Aldiss *Non-Stop*)

**Pressure systems (braid at least two)**

- Biosphere: closed loop; cascading ecological failure
- Genetics/population: minimum viable population debates
- Society: mutiny, caste, religion of the Mission, amnesia about origin
- Psychology/purpose: intermediate generations as means (Levy)
- Engineering: self-repair, radiation, deceleration
- Destination risk: was the target hospitable? probe data lied?

**Conventions & obligatory beats**

- Knowledge loss / mythologized Earth or Captain
- Generation conflict: believers in Arrival vs Ship-as-forever-home
- Map-of-the-world-that-is-a-ship; system failure Tuesday triage; Mission heresy; origin reveal; arrival crisis; ethical confrontation; culture vs colonization checklist

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Starship-as-hotel | Scarcity + polity + labor |
| Amnesia plot, no institutional logic | Who benefits from forgetting? |
| Handwaved eternal recycling | Cascade failures |
| Arrival as sudden epilogue | Voyage cost across middle |
| Ignoring non-volunteer children | Theme the ethical bind |

## Hard

**Feasibility literature as craft pressure**

- Population and closed-ecosystem debates raise reader realism expectations — invent consistently even if you don’t cite papers
- Hard SF strain: mass budgets, delta-v, shielding as plot; soft SF strain: kinship, ritual, language drift

**When to break “must arrive”**
- Refuse-to-stop / Ship-as-home endings are valid if generation conflict earned them
- Series: ship can host murder/romance/war if voyage pressure remains
