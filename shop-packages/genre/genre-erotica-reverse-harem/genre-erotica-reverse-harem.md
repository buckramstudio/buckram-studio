# Reverse harem / why-choose (erotica ↔ erotic romance)

Engine: **one protagonist builds lasting romantic/sexual bonds with multiple partners and does not “pick one”** — group resolution is the point. Market-dominant home is **romance / erotic romance**; pure erotica versions exist but readers often expect HEA-shaped landings.

## Light

Job: build parallel bonds that refuse the “pick one” ultimatum — differentiated partners, group landing (or honest erotica repositioning).

Contract: protagonist agency; page-time fairness; LI–LI matrix matters; do not bait RH romance into secret monogamy finales.

Must-not-break:
- Agency — protagonist chooses the many; not passively collected
- Differentiation — each partner distinct voice/emotional job (clone problem)
- Relationship matrix — LI–LI dynamics and group history
- Page-time fairness
- Group HEA/HFN if sold as RH romance — all intended endgame partners in
- Multi-lateral consent in group intimacy

## Normal

**Labels**
- **Reverse harem (RH):** historically “one woman, multiple men” ratio framing
- **Why-choose:** centers structural refusal of “pick one”; gender ratio can vary
- Craft problem is the same: **parallel bonds + group HEA**

**Shelf home**

| If… | Then… |
|-----|-------|
| Optimistic multi-partner ending required | Romance / erotic romance path |
| Sexual constellation without permanence | Erotica ménage/poly  |
| Sex load-bearing for each bond + group HEA | Erotic romance RH |

**Reader promises**
1. Agency
2. Differentiation
3. Relationship matrix
4. Page-time fairness
5. Group HEA/HFN (romance path)

**Consent / power craft**
- No partner volunteered by the others
- Pack/mate-bond / harem-politics still need emotional consent — destiny ≠ finished relationship
- Bodyguard cluster, captive “harem,” bully RH — negotiated fantasy vs dark lane ; HEA still required if sold as romance

**Structures that fit**
- Staggered bond escalation
- Black moment that threatens the *polycule*, not only one dyad
- Series: one group across books, or revolving focus with continuity 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Clone love interests | Voice tags, distinct conflicts, LI–LI matrix |
| Secret forever-monogamy twist | Wrong shelf — don’t |
| Orgy math without emotion | Purpose test per scene |
| Neglecting partner #3–4 | Rotate POV or on-page care |
| Erotica open ending sold as RH romance | Reposition or write the group landing |

## Hard

**Why-choose vs ménage branding**
- Why-choose centers the structural promise; ménage may be closed triad without RH page-time expectations — comps must match
- Pack/PNR RH stacks compulsion craft from without skipping consent

**When to break “equal page time”**
- Temporary focus arcs OK if neglected partners still receive care beats and endgame inclusion — permanent silent drop fails the lane
