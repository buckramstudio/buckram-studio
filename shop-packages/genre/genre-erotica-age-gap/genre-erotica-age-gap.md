# Age-gap erotica (adult–adult only)

Engine: **experience, life-stage, and power asymmetry between consenting adults** as erotic and emotional fuel — not a “mentor the young” illegal frame.

**Hard constraint for this craft note:** all sexual characters are **adults**. This collection does not provide guidance for sexual content involving minors or adult–minor frames.

## Light

Job: make adult–adult experience/power asymmetry the fuel — both partners agentive adults; gap is craft, not child-coding.

Contract: asymmetry visible (knowledge, money, status, sexual experience); growth specific; marketing honest about the gap.

Must-not-break:
- All sexual characters are adults — no craft guidance for minors or adult–minor frames
- Younger adult not child-coded (adult jobs, social world, sexual agency)
- Asymmetry legible — who has capital, vocabulary, housing, secrets
- Mentorship + sex: separate teaching competence from sexual access
- Ability to refuse without destitution — or honest dark framing , not “cute” coercion
- HEA only if erotic-romance structure; else position as erotica

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Gap may be modest (late 20s / early 40s) or wide; craft issue is **asymmetric knowledge, money, status, or sexual experience**, not the number alone
- Works as erotica (sexual becoming / renegotiating desire) or erotic romance (HEA through that asymmetry)
- Overlaps: boss/employee, professor/adult student, celebrity/civilian — see workplace file when institution is the engine
- “Daddy”/pet aesthetics are diction/power traps unless negotiated and adult-framed 

**Reader promises**
1. Adult agency on both sides
2. Asymmetry is visible
3. Growth is specific — innocence→knowledge *or* jaded→reawakened
4. Honest marketing — age-gap shoppers want the gap

**Consent / power craft**
- Clearer on-page want under imbalance; enthusiastic participation
- Aftercare / social aftermath: family judgment, workplace policy, friend-group fallout are plot tissue

**Structures that fit**
- Initiation with experienced partner → younger adult claims equal desire vocabulary
- Midlife reopening with younger partner → older adult’s Lie about being “done” cracks
- Reunion gap (knew each other when both already adults; years apart)
- Secret relationship → exposure midpoint changes power

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Child-coding the younger adult | Adult world + adult sexual agency |
| Mentor grooming aesthetics | Separate pedagogy from sex; or dark-lane honesty |
| Gap as only trait | Specific Want/Need beyond birth years |
| Sugar-script without economics | Money as power acknowledged on page |
| Romance fluff expectations unmet | Signal heat + power; match HEA if erotic romance |

## Hard

**Routing edges**
- Institutional power (job, visa, housing) dominating the erotic engine → stack or prefer 
- Social forbiddenness primary → 
- Coercion without exit sold as fluffy age-gap → wrong lane; dark honesty or rewrite agency

**When to break “experienced teaches novice” default**
- Midlife/younger-partner arcs where the younger adult holds erotic or emotional competence invert the cliché — earn it as character, not twist gag
- Never “break” the adults-only constraint
