# Small-town / community romance

Engine: ensemble town pressure + central love story — gossip, history, and overlapping relationships force the couple’s choices.

## Light

Job: make the place a cast with power, not a postcard behind a generic couple.

Contract: central love story + HEA/HFN ; community density is the pressure system; series-by-town common.

Must-not-break:
- Town as witness — private courtship has public cost
- Secondary cast with Wants (not cute NPCs)
- Belonging test (stay/leave, fit/misfit)
- Black Moment involves community/family loyalty, not only personal misunderstanding
- HEA renegotiates how they live *here* (or leave together)
- Heat lane named and kept

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Dominant CR sub-shelf (Hallmark-adjacent to steamy indie); often series-by-town with rotating couples
- Outsider/insider, returnee, or forever-local meets newcomer are common spines
- Rural / coastal / main-street suburb — **density of overlapping relationships** is the engine, not farm aesthetic alone
- Cozy-leaning vs high-heat variants share town-as-pressure

**Obligatory pressures**
- History landmine — shared past, rumor, or old feud
- Festival / crisis set-piece that traps the couple in public
- Map recurring locations (diner, harbor, ranch, church hall)
- Ensemble without stealing the central couple
- Series hooks: next couple seeded without spoiling this HEA
- If suspense enters, decide RS dual-engine vs soft danger wallpaper 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Postcard setting; remove town and plot unchanged | Give place jobs that block/aid Want |
| Nosy neighbors as only forever obstacle | Escalate history / belonging / livelihood stakes |
| Instant community acceptance | Earn belonging; show costs |
| Series bible drift | Lock geography / ages / who-knows-what |

## Hard

**Romance contract still primary**
- RWA-style baseline: central love story + emotionally satisfying optimistic ending — town color cannot replace couple Black Moment / earned landing
- Women’s-fiction bleed: if career/family/community journey outranks couple climax, reposition 

**Ensemble / series edges**
- Contagonist townie (temptation of “safe” local match) vs outsider growth — distinct functions 
- Place bible durability: signature sensory triad + social rules that change speech 
- Gossip as information economy: who knows what when = fair mystery-adjacent craft without stealing romance endgame

**When to break small-town comfort**
- Dark past / crime under Main Street OK if romance endgame still paid and content signaling honest
- Transplant to city “found community” only if overlapping-relationship density remains — zip code alone isn’t the engine
