# Urban fantasy

Engine: **familiar city + secret (or newly visible) supernatural** — friction between rent, phones, and the uncanny.

## Light

Job: make the mundane half of life load-bearing so magic has something to bruise.

Contract: contemporary (or near-contemporary) urban grain + fantastic underworld; often masquerade/secrecy; adventure/mystery-forward (distinct from paranormal romance’s HEA-first promise).

Must-not-break:
- City specificity (not any-city nightclub)
- Code-switch: mundane obligation vs supernatural crisis same day
- Masquerade (or “magic just arrived”) has why/how if plot keeps poking it
- Magic via action + consequence; costs/limits
- Double life cost — relationships, exhaustion, identity
- Don’t sell UF when the promise is PNR heat/HEA

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Metropolitan grain: subways, warehouses, nightclubs, rent, bureaucracy — not secondary-world quest maps
- Classic lineage: occult detective / crime-noir with monsters + hidden supernatural elite
- Distinct from high/epic secondary-world stakes → 
- City as tool: tone, plot pressure, sometimes character

**The masquerade (or veil)**
- Why it matters: UF wants “our world” + ancient magic; secrecy (or new-magic premise) papers over history contradiction and supplies double-life conflict
- Why hide: outside threat; higher authority non-interference; or “magic is new”
- How: cleanup org / memory wipe; designed forgetting; isolation auras; or honest handwavium (fails if family-lying *is* the drama)

**Obligatory pressures**
- Code-switch → threshold/first sight → investigation ladder → masquerade stress test → neutral ground → double life cost
- Series pattern: monster-of-the-week + long-running faction/found-family arc

**Working checklist**
- “Standard UF” triad: contemporary city + hidden underworld + protagonist straddling both
- Texture contrast: fluorescent offices against ritual/blood/relic
- Smartphones aren’t prop-only if tech/arcane clash matters
- Sanderson continuum still applies 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Mundane life underwritten | Rent, job, family scenes with weight |
| Masquerade angst, no why/how | Establish enforcement or new-magic premise |
| Faction taxonomy dump | Case-driven reveal |
| Generic city backdrop | Specific place-pressure |

## Hard

**Masquerade scrutiny edges**
- Big public magic fights still “nobody notices” breaks trust unless cleanup/perception rules are paid
- Handwavium OK only when plot doesn’t stake drama on keeping the secret from loved ones
- Magic-just-arrived skips ancient-masquerade problem — different tradition, different obligatory history

**Shelf collisions**
- PNR: same toys, relationship-first promise and HEA/HFN owed if romance-primary
- When dread leads over case/adventure → lean / occult
- Secondary-world city with no Earth grain → not UF; check epic/low

**When to break secrecy**
- Public-magic futures exist — then extrapolate Tuesday (law, media, tourism) or the novum is costume
- Series can escalate veil failure if cost and aftermath stay load-bearing
