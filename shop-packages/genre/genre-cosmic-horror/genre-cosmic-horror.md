# Cosmic / weird horror

Engine: human scale fails against the incomprehensible. Dread from realization and forbidden knowledge — not jump-scare or gore alone.

## Light

Job: wound the reader with *realization* — the world was always vast/bleak — more than with a monster reveal.

Contract: unknowable/indifferent cosmos; knowledge itself as stake; protagonists intelligent yet powerless; endings refuse tidy closure.

Must-not-break:
- Fear of the unknown/unknowable; human morality irrelevant at cosmic scale
- Entities indifferent, not cartoon-evil
- “Rules” show how little they protect
- Atmosphere and implication > taxonomic monologue
- Any “win” temporary or catastrophic
- Keep cosmic engine; discard period bigotry when working the tradition

## Normal

**Genre contract**
- Horror of realization more than intrusion-of-monster alone
- Sanity, identity, or faith as stakes
- Lingering implication > full description
- Weird fiction = broader mode (numinous + nontraditional monsters); cosmicism = philosophical spear-tip

**Cosmic vs weird vs gothic**

| Mode | Primary pressure |
|------|------------------|
| Gothic | Buried secret, place, lineage  |
| Weird | Slippery macabre; awe + dread; nontraditional monsters |
| Cosmic | Insignificance / denied primacy / shattered natural law |

**Five horror elements (Lovecraft’s own frame)**
1. Underlying horror / abnormality
2. General effects / bearings of that horror
3. Mode of manifestation (what is observed)
4. Types of fear-reaction in characters
5. Specific effects under these conditions

Process hint (“Notes on Writing Weird Fiction”): chronological synopsis → narrative-order synopsis → rapid atmospheric draft → revise for tone → polish. Mood before action.

**Working checklist**
- Second-hand documents (letters, journals, reports) for distance/unreliability
- Ban empty “indescribable” — specific sensory wrongness, then withhold taxonomy
- Denied primacy: older intelligences precede us
- Focus questions, not answers; leave threads

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Monster Wikipedia at climax | Implication; cut taxonomy |
| Human-scale villain + tentacle cosplay | Indifference / vastness as pressure |
| Protagonist defeats cosmos cleanly | Pyrrhic or temporary at best |
| Gore substitutes for existential pressure | Knowledge wound; scale |
| Mythos name-drop without philosophical stake | Engine first; names optional |

## Hard

**New Weird & contemporary practice**
- New Weird (VanderMeer / Miéville lineage): mood-first, often urban/political — tentacles not required
- Modern practice: keep cosmic engine, reframe tradition (LaValle, Ruff, et al. as engagement models, not pastiche)

**Cross-lane dials**
- Local rite + community complicity without vast indifference → 
- Flesh as cosmic medium → 
- Ancestral house excavation without cosmic thesis → 

**When to break powerless protagonists**
- Temporary human-scale wins OK if cosmos remains unsolved and cost sticks
- “Defeating” a local avatar ≠ defeating the cosmos — keep scale honest
