# Historical mystery

Engine: period constraint + fair-play (or procedural) investigation; the past’s tech, law, and manners shape what can be known and how; dual pleasure of puzzle and time-travel texture.

## Light

Job: make period limits shape the clue economy — no magic DNA unless speculative AH.

Contract: mystery obligations inside historical fiction research/voice; sleuth may be amateur, early professional, physician, scribe, nun, magistrate…

Must-not-break:
- Detection methods fit the era
- Social access gates who may ask questions (class/gender/race)
- Fair play if billed as puzzle; signal if noir-historical instead
- No tourist exposition (“as you know, Bob, in 1888…”)
- Victim humanity — not puzzle toys

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Hybrid: + 
- Anachronism risk high: forensic knowledge, modern speech frameworks, CSI methods must be earned or avoided

**Working checklist**

- Clue economy limited by period science
- Research: law, forensics history, slang, geography of city/court
- Reveal that uses period knowledge the reader was taught
- Justice ending negotiated with historical power realities

**Obligatory pressures**
- Period investigation limits (no phones, forensics ceiling, class access)
- Fair-play or procedural clue trail
- Social rule as obstacle to questioning
- Anachronism trap avoided
- Reveal using taught period knowledge
- Justice ending vs historical power

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Modern detective in costumes | Rebuild method from period tools |
| Period drowning mystery | Keep turns sharp; close cupboards |
| Solution from unknowable facts | Teach the reader the needed period fact |
| Tourist dialogue | Characters don’t explain their ordinary world |

## Hard

**Contract dial: puzzle vs noir-historical**

- Fair-play billed → Knox/Van Dine spirit still binds 
- Cost-over-puzzle / rigged institutions → noir-historical tools
- Early institutional detection → lean procedural realism without modern forensics magic

**Parent craft synthesis**

- Mattress research: open cupboards only for *this choice*
- Voice = period content of thought, not forsooth soup
- Social gates are not flavor — they are investigation obstacles

**When to break period science ceiling**
- Speculative alternate history / SF hybrid must signal the break
- Cozy community restoration endings must still respect who historically held power — or own the fantasy
