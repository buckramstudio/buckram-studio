# Romantic suspense

Engine: dual engines — intrigue/crime/threat *and* courtship — each raising the other’s stakes until mystery resolves **and** the relationship solidifies (HEA/HFN required).

## Light

Job: braid threat and courtship so stripping either engine fails the book.

Contract: danger has teeth; couple lands optimistically. Not thriller-with-a-crush and not romance-with-a-burglar-subplot.

Must-not-break:
- Dual strip test — both engines load-bearing
- Problem + Proximity + Pacing (Three Ps)
- Don’t pause for a clinch mid-chase — intimacy in the come-down
- HEA/HFN — don’t kill romance contract for thriller “realism”
- Both leads contribute competence — no helpless wallpaper
- Fair-enough clues for mystery-lean vs thriller-lean

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Classic pattern: one lead endangered; other often protector (LEO/military/bodyguard) — modern books diversify competence
- Primarily contemporary; historical romantic suspense exists
- More plot-driven than pure CR; language may run leaner
- Relationship decisions raise suspense; suspense events change the relationship

**Three Ps**

1. **Problem** — meaty romantic conflict + suspense conflict with real stakes; banter can’t replace either
2. **Proximity** — leads on the page together; show attractive *action*, not trait lists
3. **Pacing** — ticking clocks compress romance time — post-adrenaline breathers for intimacy

Plot practice: timeline romance arc and suspense arc separately, then braid.

**Reader promises**

- Danger forces honesty — fear strips pretenses
- Competence fantasy with mutual save
- Concrete near-misses and costs — soft threat fails

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Danger as wallpaper | Dual strip test; braid decisions |
| Smooch during pursuit | Move intimacy to aftermath |
| Unearned instalove on compressed clock | Shared trauma + noticed traits + proximity |
| Soft threat | Near-misses with cost |
| HEA omitted for grit | Earn HFN or leave shelf |
| One competent lead | Both skills matter to survival |

## Hard

**Mystery-lean vs thriller-lean**

- Puzzle fairness dominates → borrow mystery clue economy
- Clock/audience-knowledge dread dominates → thriller tools
- Domestic betrayal cage → hybrid
