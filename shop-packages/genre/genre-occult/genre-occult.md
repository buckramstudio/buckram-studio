# Occult / demonic horror

Engine: ritual, forbidden practice, and entities that answer. Faith, will, and knowledge as weapons — and as liabilities.

## Light

Job: make cosmology rules load-bearing — summoning, binding, curse, exorcism play fair.

Contract: hidden knowledge/practice drives plot; stakes include soul, free will, loved ones’ bodies, community salvation — not only pulse.

Must-not-break:
- Establish cosmology early enough for confrontations
- Ritual conditions create clock (earned, not random blood moon)
- Grimoire logic consistent — partial reading = partial disaster
- Aftermath cost — what cannot be un-summoned?
- Faith traditions can be ally, obstacle, or ambiguous — avoid sermon-as-plot unless that *is* the market
- Research living religions; don’t treat sacred trauma as costume

## Normal

**Genre contract**
- Occult = “hidden”; antagonist may be demon, spirit, witch-power, or human cult using those frames
- Distinct when demonology/grimoire logic/church–magic opposition is load-bearing
- Overlaps folk horror’s communal rites and possession-as-body-theft — different engines

**Possession & intrusion patterns**
- **Invasion:** entity rides/owns a body; identity war ( tools)
- **Bargain:** power bought; payment due
- **Contamination:** object, place, or text as vector
- **Exorcism ordeal:** ritual as set-piece — preparation, failure, cost, possible pyrrhic win
- **Skeptic → believer** (or believer whose doctrine fails against the real thing)
- Ambiguity option: dual-readable signs if promising gothic doubt; if promising confirmed occult, pay that promise

**Working checklist**
- Clergy, occultist, or folklorist as competent opposition — competence without plot armor
- Specific sensory wrongness over “evil energy”
- Language texture ≠ fake profundity dump
- Dread ladder from parent: terror > horror > revulsion 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Demon = serial killer + red contacts | Cosmology + indifference/agenda beyond gore |
| Exorcism win, zero setup | Teach rules before ordeal |
| Scripture quote climax | Dramatized ordeal + cost |
| Cultists as cartoon idiots | Internal ethic; costly belief |
| Tourism of closed practices | Invent rooted cult or deep homework |

## Hard

**Ethics & research**
- Living religions and closed practices: research; prefer invented cults inspired by patterns when unsure
- Possession tropes have real-world harm histories — fiction can explore without endorsing abuse-as-exorcism

**Dial toward neighbors**
- Community consensus + landscape/ritual without summoned entity as literal engine → 
- Ancestral house excavation / lineage secret dominates → 
- Ambiguous doubt primary → gothic tools; confirmed answering entities → stay occult

**When to break confirmed-entity promise**
- Dual-readable ending only if seeded as gothic doubt from the start
- Pyrrhic exorcism (entity gone, soul/community scarred) often truer than clean win
