# Paranormal romance (PNR)

Engine: supernatural rules that complicate courtship — immortality, mating bonds, secrecy, predator nature — while central love + HEA/HFN still win.

## Light

Job: make supernatural conditions pressure trust, power, belonging, and secrecy without demoting the romance landing.

Contract: central love story + optimistic couple ending; fantastic elements are load-bearing, not wallpaper.

Must-not-break:
- Strip test: remove paranormal → romance should break
- Mate bond / destiny supplies attraction, not finished trust
- Power imbalance needs counterweight (skill, knowledge, courage, unique need)
- Masquerade/secrecy has rules that hold when plot pokes them
- HEA/HFN for category PNR
- Position honestly vs urban fantasy (case/quest-first)

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Fantastic/SF-adjacent woven into romance: vampires, shifters, witches, fae, demons/angels, psychics, ghosts; wiki historically also parks time travel/aliens under paranormal umbrella
- Two jobs: sell the love **and** make supernatural conditions affect trust/power/belonging/secrecy
- Attention may stay couple-forward or build full systems — still PNR if love remains emotional core
- Distinct from **urban fantasy**: UF promises case/adventure/masquerade friction; PNR promises relationship landing

Creature lanes (tension flavor): vampire (hunger/immortality), shifter (control/pack/mate), witch (consent/free will under magic), fae (bargain/alien morality), angel–demon (cosmic forbidden).

**Mate bond / fated mates**

- Bond = recognition/attraction; **not** finished relationship
- Conflict from rejected bond, enemies fated, delayed recognition, broken prior bond, pack/court politics
- Destiny *and* earned emotional consent — body knows; heart/trust catch up
- Instalove justified by bond alone = failure mode

**World vs romance balance**

- Rules that matter to the couple first (sunlight, moon, blood cost, bargain law)
- Human-world relation: masquerade, integrated, or separate realm — each shapes secrecy/prejudice
- Reveal world through romantic pressure, not faction taxonomy dumps
- Heat often warm→steamy+; supernatural should change intimacy (senses, instinct, magical feedback)
- Series-native: pack/coven/brotherhood (new couple + overarc) or one couple + escalating threats

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Supernatural wallpaper on CR | Strip test fails → thicken paranormal cost |
| Mate bond skips yearning | Bond attracts; trust/choice still arcs |
| Masquerade angst with no rules | Define why/how secrecy holds |
| Passive protected object | Agency + competence for less-powered lead |
| Worldbuilding drowns couple climax | Keep romance load-bearing for category PNR |
| Wrong shelf (UF vs PNR vs romantasy) | Position by primary promise |

## Hard

**PNR ↔ UF ↔ romantasy triage**

- If case/quest can resolve with romance thinned → UF lean 
- If secondary-world co-bills magic *and* romance → romantasy map 
- If alien/tech novum is primary speculative pressure → SFR 
- Series HEA per book still expected on romance-shelf PNR even when villain overarc continues

**Power-asymmetric intimacy**

- Predator/prey, immortal/mortal, bonded/unbonded: diegetic negotiation of consent under instinct/magic
- Supernatural feedback in sex scenes is craft opportunity — senses, heat cycles, magical consequence — not only set dressing
- When to break “masquerade forever”: only if exposure cost is the couple’s Black Moment fuel, not a forgotten rule
