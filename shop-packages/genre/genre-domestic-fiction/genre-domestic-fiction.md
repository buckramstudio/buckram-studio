# Domestic fiction

Engine: household and kinship stakes — marriage, parenting, eldercare, inheritance, home as stage; the intimate sphere carries the plot. Not the same as domestic thriller (threat/suspense engine).

## Light

Job: make the household the pressure geography — rooms, duties, and kinship money as plot.

Contract: intimate-sphere consequence is primary; dread optional; distinct from domestic thriller / intimate suspense billing.

Must-not-break:
- House/apartment enables or blocks intimacy and power
- Kinship roles with conflicting duties (care, money, loyalty, inheritance)
- Small events with large meaning (holiday, diagnosis, move, affair, will)
- Intimacy includes power — don’t soften all conflict into polite misunderstanding
- If suspense dominates, you may have crossed into 
- Avoid furniture catalog without want

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Tradition of novels centered on home life and family relations (19th-c domestic/sentimental “woman’s fiction” lineage; craft today is broader)
- Distinct from **domestic thriller** / intimate suspense: here dread is optional; emotional and social consequence is primary
- Adjacent: WF, family saga (multi-gen scale), contemporary realist
- Historical domestic realism often staged ideal family between disordered poverty and idle upper-class foil (class as craft pressure)

**Working checklist**
- Household geography (rooms, privacy, rent money)
- Kinship duty clash
- Ritual interrupted — holiday, dinner, bedtime, Sunday
- Secret in the intimate sphere revealed or threatened
- Home changed — move, divorce, death, newborn, empty nest
- Repair or rupture that redefines the household contract
- Care work onstage, not backdrop only

**Obligatory pressures**
- Household geography that enables or blocks intimacy/power
- Kinship duty clash
- Ritual interrupted
- Secret in the intimate sphere
- Home changed
- Repair or rupture of household contract

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Soft furniture catalog | Name want + duty clash |
| Care work as backdrop | Put labor in scene beats |
| Thriller bait for quiet novel | Align cover/comps with engine |
| “Women’s problems” stereotype | Specific kinship + class texture |

## Hard

**Domestic fiction vs domestic thriller**

| | Domestic fiction | Domestic thriller |
|--|------------------|-------------------|
| Primary engine | Emotional / social consequence in household | Threat, suspense, revelation clock |
| Dread | Optional | Load-bearing |
| Ending grain | Repair, rupture, redefined contract | Survival / truth cost / danger resolved |

- Marketing mismatch is a known failure — quiet kinship novel sold with thriller covers trains the wrong promise

**Lineage vs modern use**

- Victorian/sentimental domestic realism often ended in marriage (reform the wild male *or* marry the solid one) — modern domestic fiction need not; keep the household stage, update the endgame
- Class sandwich (disordered below / idle above) remains a useful pressure geometry when not cartooned
- Multi-generation scale → consider ; present-day single household → stay here / contemporary realist

**When to break “intimate sphere only”**
- Workplace / public plot OK if it returns pressure to the home contract
- If public thriller machinery takes the spine, rebill
