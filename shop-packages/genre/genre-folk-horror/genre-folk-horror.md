# Folk horror

Engine: rural / isolated community + folklore or ritual pressure; landscape and social complicity scare more than jump-scare monsters.

## Light

Job: make belief-and-belonging the threat — not a generic monster in the woods.

Contract: outsider (or returning local) meets a closed knowledge system; dread > gore; people’s actions often scare more than independent entities.

Must-not-break:
- Landscape as pressure and symbol
- Community consensus as antagonist
- Ritual logic internal and costly
- Complicity is the knife twist
- Research real folklore respectfully; invention feels locally rooted
- Not UK-only — culturally specific folk horrors worldwide

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Elements: countryside isolation, superstition / folk religion / pagan survival themes, sacrifice, dark nature
- Film “unholy trinity” reference points: *Witchfinder General*, *Blood on Satan’s Claw*, *The Wicker Man*
- Literary roots often cited: James / Machen / Blackwood; Jackson *The Lottery* as N. American touchstone
- SE Asian cinema often default-folk — don’t treat the mode as British costume only

**Working checklist**
- Naive outsider vs closed knowledge (or insider who breaks ranks)
- Explain ritual just enough — taxonomy dumps kill dread
- Neighborly smiles with ritual teeth
- Dread ladder from parent horror: terror (anticipation) > horror (confrontation) > revulsion
- Aftermath cost: what cannot be recovered after the rite

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Generic “cult in the woods” | Specific custom, calendar, cost |
| Rural people as only backward props | Interior motives; complicity with stakes |
| Supernatural reveal erases human agency | Keep belief/action load-bearing |
| Tourism of someone else’s sacred practice | Respect research; or invent rooted custom |

## Hard

**Belief vs entity dial**
- Mode often focuses on people’s beliefs and actions more than independent supernatural entities — dial toward occult/demonic when summoned evil is the literal engine 
- If ancestral house excavation dominates without community ritual → check 

**Landscape / community synthesis**
- Fields, woods, stones, weather as pressure — setting craft is not backdrop 
- Isolation can be social (no one will help) even when geography is open
- Sacrifice plots: victim must matter; community “good reasons” are the horror

**When to break rural isolation**
- Urban folk horror possible if closed knowledge community + ritual pressure remain
- Cosmic indifference overlay: only if human complicity still cuts — else retag cosmic horror
