# Psychological thriller

Engine: thrills from mind games, unstable perception, and eroded trust more than spectacle; action is often suppressed; dread lives in what characters believe — and whether the reader should.

## Light

Job: make epistemic danger (what’s real / whom to trust) carry the suspense spine.

Contract: hybrid of thriller structure + psychological fiction — motives, distortion, moral conflict drive suspense; domestic/intimate settings common.

Must-not-break:
- POV limitation is load-bearing
- Inner struggle + outer threat braid
- Twist surprising **and** inevitable
- “Unreliable narrator” ≠ incoherent facts
- Moral conflict/choice matters — character development not garnish

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- “Investigations of the psychologies of the principal characters” (Hutchings via wiki tradition)
- Overlaps: domestic thriller, mystery, psychological horror — dial physical vs epistemic threat
- Devices: unreliable narrator, twists, red herrings, MacGuffins, amnesia / gaslighting patterns

**Working checklist**

- Privilege information carefully (Hitchcock dread vs shock)
- Obsession / pathological relationships over random stalker silhouette
- Isolation — allies removed or suspect
- Endings often restore clarity/safety — earned ambiguity allowed if seeded

**Obligatory pressures**
- Unstable perception planted (gaslight, memory, dual story)
- Trust erosion with someone intimate
- Mind-game turn that reinterprets prior clues
- Isolation of allies
- Revelation that reorders reality
- Aftermath dread — safety not fully restored

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Unreliability as chaos | Keep a fair fact spine |
| Twist anthology | One psychological through-line |
| Prestige relabel of gore-horror | Dial epistemic threat up |
| Gaslight without fair clues | Plant readable tells when mystery-adjacent |

## Hard

**Domestic vs psych dial**

- Anchor in home/marriage/neighborhood → lean 
- Mind/perception anywhere with thriller clock → stay here
- *Gone Girl*-type overlaps both — bill the intimate cage if household is the prison

**Horror crossover**

- Residual dread / uncanny without tidy taxonomic payoff → horror sibling tools
- If supernatural explanation becomes the engine, retag; if mind remains the threat stage, stay

**When to break tidy restoration**
- Ambiguous endings OK when seeded; unearned fog reads as unfinished draft
- Howcatchem / known culprit still needs psychological cost spine
