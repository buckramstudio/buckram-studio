# Forbidden / taboo-as-market-label erotica

Engine: **desire that violates a social, professional, or communal boundary among consenting adults** — the forbiddenness is the heat source *and* the plot pressure.

**Scope:** adult–adult fiction only. Craft here covers **market “taboo”** (power imbalance, secrecy, role conflict), **not** illegal sexual content. Do not use this note as guidance for sexual content involving minors or other illegal acts.

## Light

Job: make a real social/professional barrier the heat — forbiddenness has witnesses and cost among consenting adults.

Contract: both adults choose; secrecy has logistics; payoff matches erotica vs erotic-romance shelf.

Must-not-break:
- Barrier is real — if nobody would care, raise social cost
- Both adults choose — forbidden ≠ one adult deciding for a non-agent
- Adults only — no illegal-content craft guidance (including minors)
- Role power needs visible ability to refuse — or honest dark framing
- Secrecy logistics: near-discovery, reputation, family, HR, community
- Don’t confuse negotiated kink taboo play with social-taboo plot 

## Normal

**What “taboo” means in craft (not SEO)**
Useful adult-market clusters (social forbidden, not a menu of illegal acts):
- Professional ethics conflict (therapist/client vibes, coach/athlete adults, clergy/parishioner — research red lines)
- Family-adjacent **adult** role conflict sold as trope (step-relationships among adults, etc.) — platform policies vary; craft still needs adult agency + consequence
- Betrayal structures (affair) where the erotic engine is secrecy/guilt
- Class / enemy / feud barriers

**Not in scope:** sexual content involving minors; non-consent as a “genre to learn.” Contested noncon/dubcon → high-level risks only in .

**Reader promises**
1. The barrier is real
2. Both adults choose
3. Secrecy has logistics
4. Payoff matches shelf

**Structures that fit**
1. Secret meetings escalate → exposure midpoint → sexual/ethical reckoning
2. “We shouldn’t” resistance → negotiated exception → identity change
3. Community surveillance as external antagonist
4. Temporary permission window (tour, wartime leave, conference) with aftermath cost

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Taboo sticker, no social cost | Add witnesses, rules, reputation stakes |
| Illegal-content fishing for clicks | Out of scope — do not craft here |
| Cardboard obstacles | Specific institutions/people who can punish |
| HEA that erases harm without reckoning | Earn repair or recategorize ending |
| Platform-blind tagging | Research storefront rules; content notes |

## Hard

**Taboo vs dark vs literary**
- Social forbidden without coercion aesthetics ≠ dark erotica — don’t borrow darkness tags for mild secrecy
- Literary transgression may center critique over commercial heat pacing 
- Platform policies change faster than craft — content notes and storefront research are part of professional practice

**When to break “HEA after barrier falls”**
- Erotica may end in exposure without couple permanence — market honestly
- Affair plots: third party’s humanity affects theme; cardboard spouse is a failure mode
