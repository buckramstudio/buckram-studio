# Dystopian fiction

Engine: **a society worse than ours by design** — oppression, dehumanization, or engineered “perfection” that costs humanity; warning via exaggeration of a present trend.

## Light

Job: make a specific oppressive social logic the hook — not generic “evil government.”

Contract: ongoing harmful social order (not mere collapse); protagonist who sees differently; rebellion/refusal costs; aftermath rarely tidy HEA.

Must-not-break:
- Distinctive mechanism of control (castes, language, fertility, surveillance…)
- Ordinary dystopian day before rebellion — show what will be lost
- Decision to rebel/refuse is load-bearing
- Suffering for that choice
- Everyday textures of control (slogans, rituals, banned books, regulated love)
- Don’t confuse grim aesthetic with dystopia (need social logic)

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Wretched/dehumanized/fearful society — not merely “things went wrong”
- Distinct from **post-apocalyptic** (collapse/survival) though hybrids exist; dystopia implies *ongoing* order that harms
- Classic toolkit: propaganda, police state, censorship, enforced conformity, loss of individuality
- Function: exaggerate a contemporary fear into a cautionary world; anti-utopia = utopia attempt with fatal flaw

**Conventions**
- World is the hook — particular oppressive logic must feel specific
- Relatable present → speculative extreme
- Hierarchy contrast is scenic and thematic
- Antagonist may be the system more than one villain

**Obligatory moments (commercial / Story Grid-adjacent)**
- Ordinary dystopian day → crack in the lens → decision to rebel/refuse → suffering for that choice → confrontation with source of oppression → aftermath cost
- Freshness = unexpected *how*, not skipping jobs

**Near-future craft**
- Opening: pre-crisis normality *or* creeping unease — test which earns care
- Stakes on every page: what might be lost *now*
- Specificity: shops, jobs, smells — alternate worlds die from vagueness
- Avoid lecture-pause: ideology through lived constraint

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| World tour, no desire | Protagonist want under the regime |
| Generic oppression | Distinctive mechanism |
| Sermon dumps | Lived constraint scenes |
| Rebellion without personal cost | Harm to status/loved ones |
| Grim wallpaper only | Social logic + everyday control |
