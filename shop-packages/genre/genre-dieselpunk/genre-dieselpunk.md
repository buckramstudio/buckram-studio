# Dieselpunk

Engine: **interwar-to-midcentury diesel / Deco retrofuture** — grease, radio, airpower, and pulp modernity; optimistic “decodence” *or* war-machine pessimism, depending on school.

## Light

Job: fill the iconological gap between steam-Victorian and info-age dystopia — era tech as vibe *and* lever.

Contract: retrofuturist SF / AH roughly WWI through 1950s; Art Deco / raygun gothic signals; pulp + noir grammar allowed; AH freer than strict history but period logic still constrains.

Must-not-break:
- Era technology changes what plots can do (diesel, radio, rocketry, industrial war machines)
- Pick a school — Deco optimism or war pessimism — so tone and comps match
- Not Victorian hiss/steam cosplay ; not cyberspace chrome 
- Grease and fuel smell over brass polish
- Ordinary automobile is rarely the point — charismatic transport (airships, tanks, experimental craft) carries iconology

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Retrofuturistic SF / AH combining diesel-era tech aesthetics with speculative machines
- Term coined 2001 by Lewis Pollak (*Children of the Sun* RPG); applied retroactively to pulp/Deco-future art
- Visual mother lode: Lang *Metropolis*, Art Deco, Bauhaus, raygun gothic, serial adventure
- SFE: contrarian alternate histories of that era; airships and charismatic transport; fills gap between steampunk and cyberpunk
- Westerfeld craft border: WWI as optimism→pessimism hinge; Weird WWII often reads dieselpunk

**Core contract**

1. Era technology is the vibe *and* the lever
2. Deco modernity — streamlined forms, world’s-fair futures, jazz-age energy *or* their corruption into fascism/total war
3. Pulp + noir grammar — serial adventure archetypes, hardboiled shadows, weird-menace allowed
4. AH freer than strict history — but period logic still constrains manners and tech path

**Obligatory pressures**

- Era ordinary under the chosen school’s rules
- Machine spectacle (air race, convoy, radio intercept, experimental weapon)
- Modernity’s knife edge: progress promise vs war/fascism/industrial horror
- Mission under industrial weather (fog, blackout, ration, propaganda)
- Costed landing: victory that smells of diesel and compromise

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Steampunk with darker brass | Shift energy signal + design language to Deco/diesel |
| Costume Deco, no lever | Make radio/airpower/war machine change the goal |
| School mush | Commit Ottensian or Piecraftian grain |
| Cyberpunk neon pasted on 1930s | Drop cyberspace; keep industrial modernity |

## Hard

**Two schools (Ottensian / Piecraftian)**

| School | Grain | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **Ottensian** | Utopian Deco futures; Jazz Age enthusiasm prolonged | World’s Fair optimism; war delayed or transformed |
| **Piecraftian** | World-war aesthetics; culture stalled by endless conflict | Survival over style evolution; diesel as necessity |

Dividing line commonly drawn at WWII’s start — pick a school so tone and comps match. “Decodence” = Deco + decadence: style enthusiasm as worldview.

**Steampunk vs dieselpunk**

| | Steampunk | Dieselpunk |
|--|-----------|------------|
| Soft era | Victorian / Edwardian industrial | Interwar → ~1950s |
| Energy signal | Steam / clockwork | Diesel / petrol / early electric radio age |
| Mood default | Invention wonder (+ social cost) | Deco optimism **or** war pessimism |
| Design | Brass, gears, ornament | Streamline, Deco, Bauhaus, pulp |

**When to break “AH freer than history”**
- Strict historical with light pulp OK if marketed historical/adventure — dieselpunk promise expects speculative machines or clear alt fork
- Noir ambience without novum → route
