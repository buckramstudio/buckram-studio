# Contemporary romance (CR)

Engine: present-day love under recognizable modern pressure — career, family, phones, money, vulnerability — without supernatural or period scaffolding.

## Light

Job: make modern-life pressure press intimacy without dukes, vampires, or Season calendars doing the work for free.

Contract: realism world runs roughly like ours; growth + confession must feel chosen. Parent HEA/HFN + heat: .

Must-not-break:
- Present-day setting readers mean as “today”
- Internal obstacle primacy — external beats trigger internal ones
- Full lives outside the love interest
- Specific premise (profession, place, culture, tone) — “two people in a city” is not enough
- Heat lane named and kept
- Don’t bend realism so far you leave for paranormal/fantasy/romantasy

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Dates fastest of romance shelves — tech/slang/mores age
- Largest modern commercial slice; voice + specific premise must carry
- Umbrella flavors: romcom, small-town, workplace, sports, holiday, NA-leaning — shoppers differ

**Reader promises**

1. Realism contract — tech, work, social rules recognizable
2. Modern-life pressure on intimacy — jobs, family via distance, reputation, mental-health expectations, what capitalism lets you sacrifice for love
3. Internal obstacle primacy — vulnerability fear, past hurt, mismatched goals
4. Earned emotional landing — no mate bond/Season force

**Conventions that work**

- Voice as atmosphere — first person / dual POV; banter; said vs thought gap
- Specific place texture — neighborhood, workplace rituals, local social web
- Tech as romance tool — texting chemistry, digital vs face-to-face gear-shift; soft-pedal app names that age overnight
- Friends, work, hobbies exist; romance enhances, doesn’t fill a vacuum

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Generic couple in generic city | Specific premise + lived-in details |
| Love interest = entire world | Independent friend/work arcs |
| Conflict = one honest chat | Meaty internal stakes requiring change |
| Therapy-speak / dating slang freeze | Character-true voice; soft platforms |
| WF journey sold as CR | Check whether couple climax is load-bearing |
| Heat mismatch | Match lane end-to-end |

- Midpoint/climax answer couple question → stay CR
- Life-obstacle / self-fulfillment climax → lean WF even with a love thread
