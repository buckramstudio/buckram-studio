# M/M erotica

Engine: male–male desire as the sexual (and often emotional) center — not “het erotica with genders swapped.” Community reading conventions, body logistics, and voice matter.

## Light

Job: deliver character-specific M/M desire with complete erotic beats at the advertised heat — pairing letters do not auto-make erotica.

Contract: apply erotica strip/HEA tests ; permanence optional unless erotic romance.

Must-not-break:
- Complete erotic beats at erotica heat — foreshortened “fade in the middle” frustrates high-heat M/M readers
- Character-specific desire; roles (top/bottom/vers or neither) are character data, not mandatory casting
- Emotional interiority in body; sex advances plot/relationship
- Respectful clarity — consistent plain terms over euphemism gymnastics at graphic heat
- Closeted/outing stakes are power — never spicy garnish without cost
- Avoid “predatory bisexual” / corrective tropes

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Pairing ≠ genre: M/M can be steamy romance, erotic romance, or erotica — HEA/sex-spine tests still apply
- M/M **erotica**: sexual self-discovery / dynamic negotiation primary; couple permanence optional
- M/M **erotic romance**: HEA/HFN + sex load-bearing 
- Submarkets (contemporary, historical, paranormal, omegaverse, etc.) stack with setting files

**Reader promises**

1. Complete erotic beats at erotica heat
2. Character-specific desire
3. Emotional interiority in body (deep POV)
4. Respectful clarity at graphic heat

**Consent / power craft**

- First times, size/prep logistics, pacing = realism + care — not medical manuals
- Kink M/M still needs negotiation craft 

**Beyond gender-swap**
- Two male bodies: choreography, refractory realities, who notices what — research comps in heat lane
- Straight-gaze failure: men only as het-heroine wish-fulfillment props
- Found-family / queer social world often carries non-heat plot
- Historical M/M: period danger + anachronistic safety — see 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Het template paste | Read M/M comps; specific community/voice |
| Interchangeable “hot guys” | Distinct Want/Need + sexual vocabulary each |
| Only trauma/coming-out as plot | Desire journey can be joy-forward; trauma needs purpose |
| Heat bait-and-switch | Signal heat in blurb/cover language |
| Erotica without change | Aftermath + self-knowledge beat |

## Hard

**Romance vs erotica shelf inside M/M**

- Same pairing letters span three contracts — apply strip test + HEA test before comps
- KU/M/M romance readers often expect HFN/HEA even at high heat; erotica positioning must be honest

**Logistics as character, not checklist**

- Prep/pacing/refractory detail should reveal care, power, or character voice — dump of anatomy facts without POV is weak
- When to break “plain terms preferred”: only if euphemism is *voice* for a specific character and clarity still holds for acts that matter
