# Regency romance

Engine: Austen-adjacent manners market — Season, reputation, marriage mart, and wit as courtship weapons; period constraint is the romance obstacle course.

## Light

Job: make social rules *bite* — access, reputation, and marriage math are the courtship engine.

Contract: English Regency (and often wider Georgian) as commercial brand; anachronistic girlboss speech breaks immersion faster than a slightly wrong glove button. Parent HR: .

Must-not-break:
- Reputation risk (alone, touching, rumor, ruin)
- Access problem — chaperones, calling hours; privacy *is* plot
- Marriage market math — fortune, entail, settlements vs love
- Wit duel advances intimacy or war
- Scandal/revelation Black Moment with social consequences
- HEA renegotiates place in society

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Almack’s-adjacent, country house, London Season
- Heyer as foundational commercial template; Austen as tonal ancestor (wit, moral testing)
- Sub-tones: traditional closed-door, sensual HR, mystery-hybrid, diverse Regency (research specificity)

**Obligatory pressures**
- Reputation risk
- Access / chaperone problem
- Marriage market math
- Public set-piece — ball, musicale, promenade, races
- Wit duel
- Scandal Black Moment
- HEA with social renegotiation (special license, country exile, ton acceptance…)

**Working checklist**

- Big things right: titles, inheritance basics, travel time, Season calendar
- Language: selective period flavor; ban modern slang/therapy-speak
- Class and servants as people with Wants, not furniture
- Mystery/spy hybrid keeps co-engine obligations
- Diverse Regency: dramatize period constraint + interior truth — don’t paste modern identity politics speech

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Ballroom tourism | Rules force a choice |
| Duke/virgin checklist | Character under manners pressure |
| Instant social revolution ending | Cost the renegotiation |
| Generic historical labeled Regency | Season/reputation math specific |
