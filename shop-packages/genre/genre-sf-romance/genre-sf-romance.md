# Science fiction / futuristic romance

Engine: novum complicates intimacy — other worlds, futures, alien biology, or tech regimes change loyalty, body, law, or distance — while romance HEA/HFN remains the emotional payoff.

## Light

Job: make the speculative element create romantic obstacles, revelations, or intimacies impossible in contemporary realism.

Contract: category SFR keeps couple landing load-bearing; heat lane still a cover/blurb promise.

Must-not-break:
- Strip test: remove the impossible element → romance should not survive intact
- Culture-clash / fish-out-of-water usually load-bearing
- Consent under alien hierarchy, military command, or symbiotic bonds needs diegetic negotiation
- Don’t strand leads in incompatible times/worlds without a landing
- Tech rigor optional; consequences and travel/comms constraints required
- Tragedy ending → don’t sell as SFR romance shelf

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Courtship on other worlds, futures, ships, colonies, or under transformative tech
- Krentz observation (early futuristics / *Sweet Starfire*): often **“historical romance logic in space”** — road-trip / culture-clash plots with speculative scenery
- Sensuality ranges chaste→very sexy; heat lane is still a contract
- May blend fantasy furniture; steampunk/AH hybrids at edges 
- Distinct from SF-with-subplot-romance: SFR keeps couple landing load-bearing

**What the novum must do**

At least one of:
- Create romantic **obstacles** (species law, vacuum distance, wartime deployment, caste tech)
- Force **emotional revelation** (biology/heat cycles, telepathy, longevity mismatch)
- Enable intimacy/conflict **impossible** in contemporary realism

**Conventions**

- Culture-clash common (planetbound × spacer, human × alien, colony × core)
- Adventure + competence under speculative threat often shares pages with yearning
- Series worlds common; psychic/marriage-agency futures (Castle/Krentz lineage) are a known lane
- Hard-SF readers shopping “romance” still need HEA if you claim the romance shelf

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Starships as painted backdrop | Novum changes couple choices |
| Infodump physics, starved yearning | Teach tech when it pressures the relationship |
| SF tragedy sold as SFR | Recategorize or earn HEA/HFN |
| Alien mate-bond instalove | Same craft as PNR bonds — attraction ≠ trust |
| Hard-SF coldness, no emotional engine | SFR is mainly romance craft in exotic space |

## Hard

**Historical-romance logic in space (Krentz lineage)**

- Road-trip / forced proximity / culture-clash templates transplant cleanly when world rules enforce the pressure
- “Futuristic” can mean near-future Earth OR deep space — dial novum to match comps, not Wikipedia SF purity

**Consent under speculative asymmetry**

- Longevity mismatch, heat biology, telepathy, military rank: name the power axis and give counterweight
- When to break “soft science for romance”: only if the book’s pleasure is hard constraints — then own hard-SF reader expectations without dropping HEA
