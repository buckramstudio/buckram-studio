# Thriller / suspense

Engine: pacing + escalating stakes; reader feels the clock. Mood = apprehension, anticipation, anxiety — if it doesn’t thrill, it isn’t doing the job.

## Light

Job: keep the reader in continuous apprehension while stakes escalate on a clock.

Contract: villain-/obstacle-driven intensity; devices may include red herrings, twists, cliffhangers, cover-ups — common ground is **emotion intensity**, not setting.

Must-not-break:
- Escalating stakes every act (not flat chase loops)
- Clock present (literal deadline or figurative)
- Protagonist reactive early → proactive late
- Twist surprising **and** inevitable in hindsight
- Suspense ≠ only shock (bomb under the table > surprise explosion)

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Ordinary citizen or hard professional in danger; mistaken identity / wrongful accusation (Hitchcock pattern) common
- Subgenres share intensity: legal, spy, medical, political, tech, romantic thriller
- Spy / espionage as distinct emphasis (tradecraft, betrayal, institutions): 
- Deep siblings: crime intensity → / 
- Hacker stories: default ; neon-noir corp dystopia → 

**Suspense mechanics**

- Anxiety from uncertainty **or** from knowing the danger and waiting for *when*
- Hitchcock hierarchy: audience knows more than characters → powerless dread
- Rewatch can still tense via anticipation / desire vs knowledge
- Adjunct foreshadowing: 

**Working checklist**

- Short chapters; cliffhanger endings
- Ticking clock
- Red herrings vs fair clues when puzzle-heavy (borrow mystery fairness)
- Psychological mode: mind games / epistemic danger as much as physical 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Shock without dread | Plant danger the reader sees coming |
| Flat intensity | Escalate constraints, not only volume |
| Twist from nowhere | Seed plants; inevitability on reread |
| Wrong shelf | Cozy comfort → ; dread-first → |

## Hard

**Information privilege dial**

- Audience-ahead (Hitchcock bomb) vs character-ahead vs equal ignorance — pick deliberately per beat
- Psych thriller leans epistemic threat (what’s real?) over spectacle; domestic anchors threat in household 
- When puzzle fairness dominates the pleasure, retag toward while keeping thriller pace

**Subgenre routing**

- Process/rules as arena → 
- Body/hospital/public-health clock → 
- Hidden system / paranoia pattern → 
- Kinetic set-piece survival → 
- Home/marriage/family cage → 

**When to break short-chapter fever**
- Longer recovery beats can heighten the next hit if the clock still ticks 
- Aftermath residue matters when crossing into horror dread ladder
