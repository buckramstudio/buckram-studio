# Cyberpunk

Engine: **high tech / low life** — advanced cybernetics, AI, and dataspheres juxtaposed with street-level precarity; megacorps (not nations) as power; identity as editable body/credential.

## Light

Job: make tech create the trap — implants/networks/surveillance with tradeoffs — from a POV below the towers.

Contract: near-future street SF; corporate/networked power; unstable identity; bittersweet survival more common than Chosen One saves Earth.

Must-not-break:
- Tech creates conflict (access ↔ tracking; upgrade ↔ debt)
- Power is corporate/networked — governments weak, bought, or theater
- POV from below (data thief, street medic, gig-edge)
- Identity unstable — memory, credentials, flesh editable
- Neon without cynicism/moral ledger is décor
- Orientalist “mood board” city pastiche is a known failure

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Bethke coined the word (1983); Dozois popularized; Gibson *Neuromancer* (1984) locked the template
- **Cyber:** networks, cyberware, drugs/bio-change, VR / matrix
- **Punk:** streetwise, alienated, successive disillusion peelings
- Atmosphere often film-noir / hardboiled; density of information as *style*
- Street finds its own uses for things — tech against inventors’ intent

**Core contract (five)**
1. Tech creates the trap
2. Power is corporate/networked
3. POV from below
4. Identity is unstable
5. Bittersweet survival common

**Obligatory pressures**
- Street ordinary → tech bargain → corp/network reveals teeth → run/jack/heist under pressure → identity crack → costed aftermath
- Freshness = unexpected *how* on these jobs, not neon tourism

**Working checklist**
- Megacity as ecosystem (vertical class: towers vs ducts)
- Information as primary currency — hack can equal chase set-piece
- Body as class site: chrome, wetware, black-clinic mods
- Antagonist often too large for clean defeat — procedural corp beats cartoon CEO alone

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Neon tourism, no trap | Tradeoffs on every upgrade/jack |
| POV from the boardroom | Drop to street / edge |
| System fully “fixed” | Small win / pyrrhic escape |
| Techno-Orientalist pastiche | Specific people and place |
| Hacker thrill, no dystopian aesthetic | Route |

## Hard

**Neon noir (not costume noir)**
- Moral weather from hardboiled/noir: rigged world, compromised code, knowledge that closes options
- Light is expensive; shadow is free — neon that fails to illuminate corners
- Without a novum that pressures Tuesday, noir is pastiche
