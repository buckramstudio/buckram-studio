# Historical fiction craft

Past lived as present. Research supports drama; drama isn’t a museum tour.

## Light

Job: make period constraints create present-tense pressure — characters experience *today*, not “history.”

Contract: only set the story in the past if the period uniquely enables the plot/pressure; research is mattress, not avalanche (Janet Ellis metaphor).

Must-not-break:
- Emotion era-agnostic; **how** it’s carried is period-specific
- Closed cupboards: know the inventory; open only when the scene needs it
- No forsooth / phonetic dialect soup
- Anachronism audit: object/tech, attitude, vocabulary, tour-guide dumps
- Pressure first — unresolved want anchors; period becomes secondary

## Normal

**Core stance**

- Failures often sentence-level performance (“bonnet prose”), not missing footnotes
- Ready-made historical plots enable and constrain — invent freely where record is thin
- Writer must know smells, floors, food, death attitudes — reader needn’t get the inventory

**Research vs drama**

- “Need to know”: what must the reader know to understand *this choice*
- Flavor nuggets from archives > lecture paragraphs
- Accuracy protects tension: world behaves; mistakes cost
- Primary sources: letters, diaries, shopping lists, newspapers, maps/routes, visual records

**Anachronism + voice**

- Mostly modern sentence flow + period-appropriate word choices / absences
- Class and labour shape speech more than calendar year
- Voice = content of thought: what they fear, don’t know, assume
- Analogy/metaphor from *their* reference world
- Hint of dialect: one turn of phrase, not a phonetic wall

**Drama from constraint**

- Laws, gender roles, travel speed, medicine, class walls, religious oversight = stakes engines
- Don’t soften into theme-park accessibility; let friction bruise
- Characters react forward — they don’t narrate significance for posterity

| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Feels fake / stiff | Cut performative period diction; restore present pressure |
| Museum tour | Move facts under action; close cupboards |
| Anachronism sting | Audit idiom + tech + attitude vs year |
| Forsooth soup | Modern flow + period concerns/content |
| Why this century? | Ensure period uniquely enables conflict |

## Hard

**Accuracy spectra**

- Intentional anachronism (stylized juxtaposition) is a deliberate aesthetic — not a research accident
- Trust exercise: one badly wrong detail or glaring omission = reader exits
- Invention: when unsure, borrow analogous period practice and bend carefully — immersion + honesty about uncertainty
