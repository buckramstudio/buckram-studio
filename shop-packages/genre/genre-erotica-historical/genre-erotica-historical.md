# Historical erotica

Engine: **period-constrained desire** — privacy, reputation, law, class, contraception, clothing logistics — with sexual journey as spine. Research is load-bearing; anachronistic sexual ethics need intentional framing.

## Light

Job: make period constraints change sex — privacy, reputation, law, and logistics are erotic obstacles/tools.

Contract: research texture that affects intimacy; power map of the era; desire period-possible *or* deliberately anachronistic with a clear contract.

Must-not-break:
- Research that affects sex — servants, thin walls, pregnancy risk, disease, divorce law
- Power map — who can ruin whom; who owns property; who is believed
- All sexual characters adults — beware child-coding “debutante” aesthetics
- Period voice for heat: modern slang shatters; pure euphemism fog also fails
- Strip test — historical tour without sexual journey ≠ erotica
- Choose fantasy modern-consent frame vs period-coercion honesty — don’t muddle marketing

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Same erotica vs erotic romance vs steamy historical romance tests
- Regency/Victorian/Western/ancient — each has different privacy and risk maps
- Queer historical erotica: danger and coded community often plot — see / 

**Reader promises**
1. Research texture that affects sex
2. Power map of the era
3. Desire period-possible *or* deliberate anachronism with clear contract
4. Strip test

**Consent / power craft**
- Marital duty, class coercion, limited exit — enthusiastic modern consent is a fantasy frame; period coercion is darker territory 
- Interracial / colonial settings: avoid fetishizing exoticism 

**Structures that fit**
- Season / marriage-mart pressure cooker
- War/leave affair with reputation bomb
- Servant/employer or class-crossing secrecy (adult; power craft careful)
- Widowed independence → sexual reclaiming

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Modern therapy-speak in 1812 | Period-plausible emotional vocabulary |
| Corset-ripping logistics ignore | Clothes, hygiene, privacy as obstacles/tools |
| Pregnancy never exists | Risk as stakes or intentional protection plot |
| Research dump mid-act | Embody through sensory constraint |
| Sweet histrom cover + dark coercion content | Warning + category honesty |

## Hard

**Anachronism as contract**
- “Modern consent in costume” is valid *if marketed*; silent anachronism reads as research failure
- Queer historical: survival/coded community may dominate non-heat plot without killing sex-spine — balance for erotica readers

**When to break “period-possible desire only”**
- Speculative or alternate-history heat still needs *some* constraint map (law, class, magic) or retag
