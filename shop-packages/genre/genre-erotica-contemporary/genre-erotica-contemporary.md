# Contemporary erotica

Engine: **present-day desire under recognizable modern pressure** — phones, work, apps, reputation, housing — with sexual journey as spine (erotica) or load-bearing path to HEA (erotic romance).

## Light

Job: put present-day logistics on desire — modern pressure triggers internal obstacles; sexual spine still primary for erotica.

Contract: believable secrecy/safer-sex/privacy/time; specific premise; heat density matches shelf.

Must-not-break:
- Strip test decides erotica vs steamy CR vs erotic romance 
- Believable modern logistics for secrecy, safer sex, privacy, time
- Internal obstacle primacy — fear, shame, mismatched goals — triggered by modern externals
- Specific premise — “two people in a city” fails
- Tech/slang soft-pedal brand names (dates fast)
- Adults only; digital consent stakes when relevant

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Same “today” realism contract as contemporary romance — structure tests decide shelf
- Umbrella for workplace, billionaire-lite, small-town heat, NA-leaning adult — specialize via stacked files when needed

**Reader promises**
1. Believable modern logistics
2. Internal obstacle primacy
3. Heat density matching shelf — not 80% office plot in erotica
4. Specific premise / angle

**Consent / power craft**
- Digital consent: recordings, group chats, revenge-porn risk as stakes when relevant
- App-mediated meet-cute still needs character
- Workplace contemporary → when institution is the erotic engine

**Structures that fit**
- Pressure-cooker weekend / travel
- Neighbors / forced proximity modern
- Exes with unfinished sexual business
- Kinship-of-choice houseshare / poly house 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Generic city couple | Specific subculture/job/place |
| Steamy CR mislabeled erotica | Check sex-spine + HEA |
| Tech that ages overnight | Evergreen verbs; minimize brand |
| Only texting, no body | Embodied scenes required for erotica |
| Friends/work vacuum | Full lives  |

## Hard

**Umbrella vs specialist**
- “Contemporary” alone is weak comps — stack workplace, age-gap, poly, short-form when that engine is primary
- NA-leaning adult vs older CR age texture changes stakes (housing, career) without changing adults-only rule

**When to break “evergreen tech”**
- If the plot *is* about a specific platform culture, name the pressure in durable terms (surveillance, virality, ghosting) even if brand names stay soft
