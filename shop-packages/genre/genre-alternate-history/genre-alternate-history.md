# Alternate history

Engine: **point of divergence (POD) + ramifications** — one (or few) historical changes, then a world that follows cause-and-effect from that fork.

## Light

Job: fork recorded history at a clear POD, then dramatize personal cost inside the remade world.

Contract: divergence before the author’s present; change that would alter subsequent history; examination of consequences — not secret history (hidden events that don’t change the public timeline).

Must-not-break:
- Spell the Point of Departure clearly early
- Causal chain, not costume teleport (institutions change when the fork requires)
- Personal story inside the remade world — don’t only tour the map
- Root the pivot in real history — buffs punish handwaves
- Dual timeline awareness: real vs alt constraints (geography, logistics, ideology inertia)
- Ending answers *why this fork matters* emotionally, not only who won the war

## Normal

**Three required elements**

1. **Point of divergence** before the author’s present (from recorded history)
2. A change that would alter subsequent history
3. Examination of consequences / ramifications

Not AH: futures written before their date arrived (period SF), or **secret history** (hidden events that *don’t* change the public timeline). Related but distinct: academic counterfactual historiography.

**POD craft**

- Prefer a **specific, small pivot** with large ripples over vague “things were different”
- Root the pivot in real history
- Spell the Point of Departure clearly early (Shimmin: first chapter, more than once)
- Build a **causal chain**: Divergence → 3–5 explained downstream events → long-term outcomes
- Keep a dual timeline: real vs alt, with constraints that don’t flex unless the POD truly changes them
- Allow happenstance — not every outcome is a master plan

**Divergence dossier (practice)**

Short working doc: what changed, why it could, who benefits, who loses; then 10-node causal map. Consistency sweep: what would ordinary people notice first? What would bureaucrats do?

**Story engines**

- Personal story inside the remade world
- Escalating consequences: unrest, opportunity, ideology clash born of the fork
- Balance familiarity and estrangement
- Optional SF overlay (post-1950s common): time travel creating the POD, sidewise jumps
- “Heinleining” / layering: drip alt facts through action and assumed knowledge (, )

**Obligatory pressures**

- Anchor beat: “this is our history until…”
- Divergence made felt (street, law, flag, missing tech, altered language of power)
- First personal cost of the alt world
- Ramification reveal midbook
- Choice under alt constraints
- Thematic closing of the what-if

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| POD buried late | Announce early; repeat via lived detail |
| Butterfly ignored | Change institutions, not only costumes |
| Butterfly overdone | Rebuild causal chain; cut random flips |
| Research theater | Character desire under alt constraints |
| Revenge fantasy | Internal plausibility + personal cost |

## Hard

**AH vs neighbors**

| Mode | Test |
|------|------|
| Alternate history | Public timeline changed by POD + ramifications |
| Secret history | Hidden events; public timeline matches ours |
| Historical fiction | No fork — fidelity to recorded outcomes (invention in gaps) |
| Period SF / future-past | Written before date arrived — not a fork from known history |
| Dystopian AH | Fork + oppressive order weaponized — also |

**SF overlay & exposition**

- Time travel / sidewise jumps OK when mechanism serves the fork — don’t let gadget tour replace ramifications
- Recursive “AH within AH” possible; keep one readable causal spine
- Layering craft: assumed knowledge in dialogue/action beats textbook asides

**When to break “small pivot”**
- Large spectacular forks OK if the causal chain is still earned and personal story remains
- Multiple PODs risk noise — justify or simplify
