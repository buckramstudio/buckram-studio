# First contact

Engine: encounter with the alien other — detection, communication, ethics, and consequence when sapient species meet; the novum is the otherness and the interface, not only the spaceship.

## Light

Job: make the interface (meeting, artifact, message) carry ethics and misread risk — hats aren’t enough.

Contract: first meeting between humans and extraterrestrials (or any two planetary sapients); physical, artifact, or technosignature.

Must-not-break:
- Design alien cognition/culture before the meeting scene
- Detection → protocol fight → interface attempt → misread → aftermath politics
- Avoid universal translators unless the story is *about* what they hide
- Estrangement: make human defaults weird under alien eyes
- Who speaks for Earth? Nations, religions, scientists vs military
- If invasion: give the other strategy/motive beyond evil hunger (unless horror contract)

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Popularized as invasion allegory via Wells; Leinster titled the mode “First Contact” (1945)
- Star Trek **Prime Directive**: non-interference ethic (often dramatically broken)
- Themes: infiltration anxiety, wonder, futility of understanding (Lem), linguistics (*Arrival*), dark-forest game theory (*Three-Body*)

**Contact modes**

| Mode | Engine focus |
|------|----------------|
| **Invasion / infiltration** | War, mimicry, Cold War allegory |
| **Artifact / BDO / probe** | Archaeology of the other; *Rama*, monoliths |
| **Message / SETI** | Decoding, politics of announcement (*Contact*) |
| **Linguistic / cognitive** | Can we mean together? |
| **Failed contact** | Mutual unintelligibility as thesis (Lem) |
| **Uplift / tourism / cargo** | Power imbalance; colonialism mirror |

**Ethics cluster**
- Non-interference vs “progressors” (Strugatsky) — export of utopia/revolution problematized
- Contamination: microbes, memes, markets, messiahs
- Optimism vs horror/futility

**Obligatory pressures**
- Detection; protocol fight; interface attempt; misread; scale shock; aftermath politics; personal mirror relationship

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Humans in rubber suits; monoculture planets | Design cognition/culture |
| Instant fluent English without cost | Make language the plot or pay for translator |
| Contact as single landing then ignored | Aftermath politics |
| Prime Directive as unexamined sermon | Break or defend with thematic notice |
| Dark forest as only cynicism | Character under the thesis |

## Hard

**Failed contact as thesis**

- Lem-lineage unintelligibility: mastery may mean refusing neat communion — still need human-scale scenes under that thesis
- Linguistic contact (*Arrival* lineage): Sapir–Whorf stakes require concrete scenes of meaning-making, not jargon fog

**When to break non-interference**
- Dramatic Prime Directive breach should cost — cultural collapse, arms race, or personal guilt — or the ethic was toilet paper
- Soft SF kinship/taboo vs hard SF light-lag/biology: pick which constraints are load-bearing (, )
