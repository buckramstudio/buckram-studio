# Magical realism

Engine: one (or few) impossibilities inside a fully intact ordinary world, narrated without apology. Seam craft: magic enters without tearing the real.

## Light

Job: narrate the extraordinary in the same register as weather and breakfast — brick-faced, no apology, consequences over mechanics.

Contract: recognizable social/historical reality; almost no explanation owed; wonder-how splits the seam.

Must-not-break:
- Pick **one** departure from the real (restraint is the discipline)
- Introduce early; flat statement; no “amazingly/incredibly”
- Surround with concrete streets/money/grievances
- Track consequences (marriage, harvest, funeral), not how the rain started
- Ban characters theorizing the system
- Don’t confuse with portal fantasy or soft-magic secondary world

## Normal

**Magical realism vs fantasy**

| | Magical realism | Fantasy |
|---|-----------------|---------|
| World | Ours (recognizable social/historical reality) | Invented or systematically altered |
| Magic debt | Almost no explanation owed | System / cost / consistency often owed |
| Instinct to resist | Justifying / “how does it work?” | Deus ex / undefined soft magic poorly solving climax |

**Tone: brick-faced / journalistic**

- García Márquez: grandmother told supernatural stories with complete naturalness / “brick face”
- Stranger the event → flatter the delivering sentence (Kafka: insect, then anxiety about missing the train)
- Author must believe the telling; doubt leaks as wink or apology

**Ground the impossible + function**

- Magic presses against specific social/historical/cultural texture
- Precise sensory realism; one well-placed impossibility > dozen flourishes
- Prefer magic that externalizes emotion, metaphors political/social reality, or is organic to folklore/place
- Allegory caution: story must entertain first 

**Time and voice habits**
- Cyclical / nonlinear time common; omniscient or village-elder cadence; optional long flowing sentences
- Starter protocol: one departure → early flat statement → concrete world → consequences only → narrator unbothered

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Magic as literary decoration | Make it pressure plot/theme |
| Characters theorize the system | Cut; restore brick face |
| Vague dreamworld, weak realism | Verifiable humidity, dust, grievances |
| Kitchen-sink marvels | One impossibility |
| Confused with portal / soft secondary world | Stay in our world; no system tour |

## Hard

**Seam diagnostics**

- If readers keep asking “how does the magic work?” you may have drifted into fantasy debt — either answer (leave MR) or flatten the telling further
- Single concrete journalistic fact can “legitimize” surrounding marvel — craft habit, not a license to explain

**When to break “one impossibility”**
- Family/lineage sagas may accumulate marvels if each remains untheorized and socially grounded (*One Hundred Years* lineage) — still not a magic-system wiki
- Low fantasy intrusion shock vs MR acceptance: choose deliberately
