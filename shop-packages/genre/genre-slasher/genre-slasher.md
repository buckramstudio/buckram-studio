# Slasher / survival horror

Engine: predatory pursuit. Body count vs survivor agency. The threat is (or was) human — walks among us.

## Light

Job: stage stalk-and-murder geography so survivor agency and character cost matter more than kill creativity alone.

Contract: human or once-human killer; isolation; escalating set-piece deaths; final confrontation with survivor(s).

Must-not-break:
- Killer stalks a set of people; blades/improvised tools common
- Antagonist bound (or failing) human morality — not alien conscience-void
- Isolation (camp, suburb, campus, house) locks
- Survivor earns win through attention and will, not random luck alone
- Ensemble characterization enough that deaths cost
- Catalyst often past trauma / anniversary / “sins of the past”

## Normal

**Genre contract**

- Film-born formula novels adapt; franchise gravity can make villain the brand — novels usually keep one book’s survivor arc central unless series-built

**Formula spine**

1. Wrongful past / trauma seed
2. Commemoration or trigger reactivates killer
3. Group gathers in vulnerable space
4. Warnings ignored; isolation locks
5. Escalating kills (set-piece deaths with geography)
6. Final confrontation — survivor(s) face the killer alone
7. Aftermath sting optional

**Final girl / survivor craft**

- Last (often young woman) left — resourceful, observant, agency under panic
- Classic “virginal final girl among sexually active peers” is overstated / self-mythologizing; modern work often subverts or self-aware-parodies it
- Avoid pure meat-fodder unless parodying that failing

**Novel vs film**
- Prose “camera” for stalk sequences — distance, sound, what protagonist doesn’t see
- Interiority can deepen killer *or* survivor — pick one primary intimacy
- Gore dosage: page can imply; choose contract (restrained vs gore-forward)
- Meta cycle (*Scream* lineage): works if characters know tropes *and* still fear

| Mode | Differs by |
|------|------------|
| Splatter | Gore spectacle primary |
| Giallo | Stylish mystery + erotic dread |
| Broader survival horror | Environment/monster attrition; may leave human killer |
| Home-invasion thriller | Shorter cast, siege, less body-count carousel |

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Kill creativity, no geography/cost | Map space; characterize before death |
| Killer motivation Wikipedia at end | Seed trauma early; show through action |
| Punishing identity traits as “earned death” | Avoid lazy moralizing |
| Zero survivor agency | Build final-girl competence ladder |

## Hard

**Moralizing death trap**

- Classic “sex = death” schema is a critical myth as much as a rule — if you use it, know whether you’re endorsing, critiquing, or parodying
- Identity-trait punishment (queer coding, race, disability as “deserved kill”) is a known toxic hangover — not mastery

**When to break formula order**
- Meta/self-aware: characters cite tropes — fear must still land or it’s only comedy ( if that is the promise)
- Killer-as-protagonist franchise mode: shift contract; survivor empathy may become secondary — label honestly
