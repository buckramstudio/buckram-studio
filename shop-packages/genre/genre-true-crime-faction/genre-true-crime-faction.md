# True-crime novelization / faction

Engine: a real case (or tightly researched real milieu) scaffolds a narrative; drama shapes truth without inventing harm carelessly; ethics of naming and invention are load-bearing, not afterthought.

## Light

Job: dramatize a real case without laundering invention as fact or turning victims into puzzle props.

Contract: spectrum from documentary true crime → novelized reconstruction → “faction” / nonfiction novel → roman à clef; reader must know how much invention is allowed (author’s note, marketing, voice).

Must-not-break:
- Harm audit for victims, survivors, falsely accused, living families
- Document what is fact / inference / composite / invented dialogue
- Don’t invent confessions or motives presented as fact
- Thematic question beyond “whodunit replay”
- Legal risk awareness (defamation, privacy) — craft note, not legal advice

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- True crime recounts real crimes by real people; often criticized as insensitive — craft answer is perspective + harm care, not thrill alone
- Nonfiction novel / “faction”: real figures and events + fiction storytelling techniques (sometimes invented conversation)
- Capote *In Cold Blood* lineage is the famous English-language touchstone; Hersey *Hiroshima* and others precede/parallel
- Overlaps crime fiction when names change; overlaps CNF when testimony and research dominate

**Craft checklist**
- Harm audit before drafting intimate scenes
- Author’s note as contract: memory limits, name changes, composites, dialogue = best recollection
- Evidence vs rumor made dramatizable without smearing living people
- Avoid trauma-as-entertainment without a moral/analytical stance
- Precision over character-assassination labels 

**Obligatory pressures / moments**
- Case spine anchored to real events
- Investigation or obsession engine for the focal character
- Evidence vs rumor tension
- Harm awareness beat — victims not props
- Revelation that respects known facts or clearly marks invention
- Aftermath — justice incomplete, media circus, living consequences

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Invented confession sold as fact | Mark invention or cut |
| Exploitative victim intimacy | Ask what the scene teaches beyond thrill |
| Conspiracy cosplay smearing real people | Evidence bar + legal vet |
| Heavy fiction, no disclosure | Author’s note + marketing honesty |

## Hard

**Faction accuracy dial (Capote problem)**
- Capote claimed word-for-word truth; skeptics challenged accuracy; he later acknowledged at least one pure-invention scene (graveyard conversation)
- Diagnostic: if the book’s power depends on “this happened,” invented hinges destroy the contract even when prose sings
- Capote ideal (minimize novelist presence) is one school — New Journalism / Gonzo deliberately insert the reporter; disclose which contract you chose

**Ethics ladder for real cases**
- Higher care: specific wrongdoing by identifiable living people; private medical/sexual facts; unsubstantiated “facts”
- Identifiability survives name-change if job + city + relationships still point
- Composites: disclose; protective motive ≠ jazzing the plot 
- True-crime shelf criticism (trash-culture / serial-killer hunger) — answer with system question and harm stance, not denser gore

**When to leave faction for straight fiction**
- Extract theme, invent instance — when confidences or unverifiable interiors would require invention presented as fact
- Roman à clef still owes living-people care if identification is easy
- Court-adjacent allegations: vet before publish; this note is not legal advice
