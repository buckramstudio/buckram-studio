# Epic / high fantasy

Engine: **secondary world + scale** — invented setting with stakes that threaten nations, peoples, or the world’s order; wonder paid for with consistency.

## Light

Job: make invented-world scale feel personal before it feels planetary.

Contract: secondary world (own geography/history/rules) and often civilizational stakes; map, fellowship, and magic consistency expected in commercial epic.

Must-not-break:
- Internal consistency of non-Earth setting
- World-threat made personal (village/family cost before continents)
- Magic taught before climax use ( First Law)
- Multi-POV: distinct voice + local goal feeding world plot
- Price of victory — map scars, innocence, fellowship
- Quasi-medieval default is tradition, not law

## Normal

**High vs epic (useful split)**
- **High:** secondary world (not “magic intrudes on Earth”); term lineage Lloyd Alexander / Frye high mimetic
- **Epic:** *scope* — vast casts, multi-thread plots, world-shaking stakes, often multi-POV
- Overlap common (LotR archetype); intimate high fantasy and epic-feeling low fantasy both exist
- Usually *not* synonymous with sword & sorcery (personal, picaresque)

**Conventions**
- Non-Earth setting with internal consistency
- World-level or civilizational conflict (vs purely local revenge)
- Hero / fellowship trajectory frequent; quest/campaign skeleton
- Map-page expectation in commercial epic; linguistic/naming consistency
- Moral polarity historically strong; modern epics may muddy without becoming full grimdark

**Obligatory pressures**
- World-threat made personal → crossing into the larger map → rule-of-magic demonstration → counsel/alliance → descent/abyss → board-changing set-piece → price of victory

**Working checklist**
- Spine what-if first; grow world from conflict 
- Magic: limits/costs; expand culture ramifications (Third Law)
- Pace: revelation ↔ mystery, battle ↔ breath; micro-tension under epic length
- Reveal lore through action and competence — hollow iceberg

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Worldbuilder’s disease / kitchen-sink magics | Draft spine; expand one system culturally |
| Tourist POV lectures every kingdom | Competent natives; taken-for-granted detail |
| Only planetary stakes | Personal cost on every major beat |
| New power at climax | Seed and limit earlier |
| Flat Dark Lord | Ideology, scarcity, or history |

## Hard

**Scale without sludge**
- Map essentials that shape culture and war — not every hamlet
- Ground the cosmic in emotional specificity — readers endure armies for people
- Series bible discipline: factions, tech/magic limits, naming 

**Moral spectrum vs grimdark**
- Clear good-vs-evil is traditional high; muddy ethics ≠ automatic grimdark
- If rotten systems + moral compromise are the *engine*, route 
- Dark/uncanny secondary worlds without political thesis → check 

**When to break epic furniture**
- Intimate high fantasy: secondary world, smaller stakes — still keep consistency and wonder
- Non-Western / non-medieval secondary worlds count if inventedness + stakes hold
- Epic-feeling low fantasy exists — don’t force a map if intrusion engine is primary
