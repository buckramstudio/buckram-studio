# Low fantasy

Engine: fantastic intrusion into a recognizable (or realist-scoped) world — magic is rare, disruptive, or psychologically ambiguous; “low” names familiarity of the world, not quality.

## Light

Job: make the first impossibility break a grounded mundane frame and change Tuesday — jobs, fear, law, family.

Contract: state which sense of “low” you mean (primary-world intrusion vs gritty low-magic secondary world).

Must-not-break:
- Ground the real with sensory primary world before the break (or the break won’t land)
- Rarity makes magic expensive — treat accordingly
- Denial → bargain → secrecy/exposure → re-integration or rupture
- Mundane stakes still real under magic
- Don’t smuggle full secondary-world tour guides into “low” unless shifting subgenre
- Fantastic elements that never pressure plot = wallpaper

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Classic critical sense: nonrational happenings intrude on the rational/primary world (Boyer/Zahorski; Wolfe)
- Contrasts **high fantasy** set in secondary worlds with their own laws
- French *fantastique* kinship: bizarre enters everyday
- Children’s low/portal staple (Nesbit onward)
- Alternate RPG sense (GURPS): daily life in a magic world with mythic scope dialed down — **say which sense you mean**

**Boundary fog (name it)**

- Portal secondary worlds (Narnia) often classed high/portal, not low — despite starting in primary world
- World-within-world (Potter) argued both ways
- “Gritty secondary world with little magic” is a **different** popular use — tone/magic-density vs primary-world intrusion
- Magical realism: marvelous often *accepted* in character worldview; low fantasy intrusion more often *violates* expected reality

**Conventions & pressures**
- Forms: personified animals/toys, comic physics, secret gifts, supernatural bleed, time slips
- Ambiguity allowed: magic, madness, or metaphor?
- Urban fantasy and much PNR sit on the low-fantasy map
- Obligatory: first impossibility; denial→bargain; secrecy/exposure; mundane stakes; rule drip; re-integration or rupture

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Fantastic never pressures plot | Tie magic to Want/Need |
| Confused with MR acceptance | Intrusion shock vs brick-faced acceptance |
| Kitchen-sink powers in “rare magic” pitch | Scarcity + cost |
| Primary world as cardboard for chosen-one speech | Ground Tuesday before break |

## Hard

**Two meanings of “low” — comps triage**

- Intrusion-primary vs low-magic-epic: different reader promises — label in comps or you bait wrong shelf
- Portal vs low: if the secondary world becomes the main stage, you’ve often left classic low

**When to break rarity**
- Escalating magic density mid-series can migrate toward high/UF — own the dial turn
- Ambiguity ending (never confirm magic): valid if gothic/psychological promise was clear from page one
