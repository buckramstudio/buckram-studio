# Portal fantasy & isekai

Engine: **crossing a threshold into another world’s rules** — adaptation, competence, and transformation under a new physics/society; the portal (or death/summon/rebirth) is the catalytic novum, not the whole story.

## Light

Job: make the threshold change the character’s agency — fish-out-of-water learning curve with consistent far-side rules.

Contract: leave familiar → threshold → unfamiliar world; portal rules = magic system; return/seal/leak are thematic, not forgotten props.

Must-not-break:
- Threshold crossing with sensory shock / irreversibility pressure
- First rule taught the hard way
- Earn wins — don’t omniscient-cheat every problem
- Portal rules consistent even when mysterious
- Portal not forgotten after chapter 3 if crossing was the engine
- Examine summoned-as-weapon / colonial savior ethics when relevant

## Normal

**Genre shape — portal / portal-quest**
- Also: gateway fantasy, crossworld fantasy; Mendlesohn: leave familiar → threshold → unfamiliar world
- Early clear model: *Alice*; classic Anglo-American: Oz, Narnia, Covenant; Barsoom as portal-to-planet
- Fantasy portals often keep magic *on the far side*; SF/horror portals more often let threat leak back

**Portal types**
- Doors, mirrors, wardrobes, tunnels, books, amulets, wormholes, storms, screens
- Natural vs artificial; guarded vs accidental; one-way vs two-way
- Effective craft: **consistent operation and costs**

**Isekai (Japanese market engine)**
- Person from “here” survives “there,” often gaining power/importance lacking before
- Transit: reincarnation, summoning, divine act, tool, game-pull, unexplained
- Common furniture: medieval-like world, magic, monsters; often LitRPG/JRPG grammar
- Cheats; variants: slow-life, villainess reboot, reverse isekai
- Overproduction backlash real — originality pressure high

**Portal fantasy vs isekai (craft)**

| | Classic portal fantasy | Isekai (typical) |
|--|----------------------|------------------|
| Tone | Wonder, quest, moral testing | Competence fantasy, reboot of self, often series grind |
| Power | May stay ordinary | Often explicit cheats / levels |
| Return | Home changed or sealed door | Stay, conquer, or slice-of-life settle |
| Market | Children’s/YA strong historically | Light novel / web novel / anime boom |

**Obligatory pressures**
- Threshold → rule lesson → competence gap → guide/false guide → homesickness vs belonging → portal politics → return or seal

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Tourism plot | Want + cost under new rules |
| Invincible cheat, no bill | Social/moral/System failure modes |
| Database-consumption clone | Invent culture from consequences |
| Portal forgotten | Keep gate politics or seal thematic |
| Colonial savior unexamined | Agenda check; local agency |

## Hard

**Rules as magic system**
- Sanderson limits apply on both sides 
- Newcomer POV is exposition gold — still drip, don’t tour-guide dump
- If using cheats/System: visible costs — 

**Age-band & ethics**
- Kidlit portals: hope and return ethics
- Adult isekai: consent of summon, labor, power-fantasy audit
- Distinct (in JP discourse) from pure time travel and from VRMMORPG-stays-in-game — still craft-adjacent

**When to break return-home**
- Stay/conquer/slice-of-life settle is valid isekai contract — then belonging vs home must still pressure
- SF wormhole portals: leak-back threat changes aftermath obligations vs sealed-wardrobe fantasy
