# Legal thriller

Engine: strategy inside a recognized process. High stakes + rules (or the hiding of broken rules). Not a gunfight — a chess match with liberty, reputation, or life on the line.

## Light

Job: make the legal mechanism load-bearing — swap it for a generic chase and the book collapses.

Contract: intellectual legal battle + thriller suspense (threats, conspiracies, ethical traps); readers expect justice theme, twists, courtroom or motion practice as arena.

Must-not-break:
- Process is publicly recognizable — readers understand the board
- Villains manipulate the system (calculated evil ≠ only physical threat)
- Get in, get out — transcript-length court scenes are dead
- Gloss foreign legal concepts in one or two flowing sentences — don’t lecture
- Protagonist ethics under pressure — not only intellect

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Protagonist usually lawyer / judge / law student / paralegal navigating case + danger
- Moral range visible because everyone must play by the rules or hide that they’re breaking them
- Dual tracks common: courtroom / filing war **and** investigation or conspiracy outside court
- Clocks: filing deadlines, statute limits, execution dates, media cycles, gag orders

**Accuracy vs page-turn**

- Compress real-world delay: emergency motions / injunctions / warrants, **or** seasonal section breaks (Turow *Presumed Innocent* pattern)
- If you bend procedure, seed a one-line reason (biased judge, mistake, pressure)
- Research rabbit holes: keep only what serves story (Swiss-watch syndrome)
- Don’t over-explain; modern audiences know hearsay basics from trial media
- Hot-button issues: let characters disagree realistically; avoid single-sermon plots

**Character intake (Grippando)**

1. What do they want?
2. Why?
3. What are they willing to risk?

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Court as wallpaper | Make next legal move the turn |
| Lecture dump | One-sentence gloss → continue |
| Perfect procedure fantasy | Unjust rulings exist — seed them |
| Chase without file | Wrong genre — retag thriller/action |

## Hard

**System as villain stage**

- Shows people at best (trying to do right) and worst (perjury, hidden/destroyed evidence)
- Strategy beats: next move matters; good and bad both shine under that pressure
- Law vs justice gap → noir-adjacent  when integrity costs more than verdict

**Investigation handoff**

- When the machine of detection dominates before filing → 
- Keep thriller spine: escalate, cliffhang, twist that was plantable
- Cognitive load managed: terms earned, then used

**When to break courtroom climax**
- Motion practice / deposition / media hearing can climax if the board and stakes are clear
- Seasonal time-skips legitimate when dead months would kill pace — signal the contract
