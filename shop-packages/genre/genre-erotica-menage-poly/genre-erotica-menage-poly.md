# Ménage / poly erotica

Engine: **multi-partner desire where ethics, logistics, and group chemistry are the plot** — not “extra body in the bed” decoration.

## Light

Job: make multi-partner ethics and logistics the plot — everyone is a character; consent is multi-lateral.

Contract: jealousy handled (utopian earn or dramatize); group chemistry specific; HEA shape matches shelf.

Must-not-break:
- Everyone is a character — third/fourth has Want/Need/voice
- Consent is multi-lateral — A+B agreeing about C’s body fails
- Jealousy is craftable — ignore (earned security) or work it; don’t oscillate randomly
- “Cheating disguised as poly” is a trust break unless the book is *about* betrayal
- Logistics specific — calendars, nesting, disclosure, safer-sex agreements as tissue
- RH/why-choose group HEA expectations → see 

## Normal

**Genre shape**

| Shape | Typical promise |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Ménage** (often MMF/MFF closed triad) | Finite set; heat + jealousy negotiation; erotica or erotic romance |
| **Poly / open** | Ongoing ethical non-monogamy; rules, metamours, time |
| **Why-choose / reverse harem** | Usually romance-shelf; group HEA — dedicated file |

- Erotica ménage may end without permanent triad; erotic romance ménage usually owes optimistic multi-partner landing

**Reader promises**
1. Everyone is a character
2. Consent is multi-lateral
3. Jealousy is craftable
4. Logistics are sexy when specific

**Consent / power craft**
- Hierarchy (primary/secondary) vs non-hierarchical — name the model; conflict when models clash
- Group scenes: who can pause/stop; nonverbal check-ins when attention is split
- Power imbalances multiply — show who can least afford “no”

**Structures that fit**
1. Couple opens → third enters → renegotiate as triad/polycule
2. Two established partners pursue one → rivalry becomes alliance
3. Poly network exists → new desire threatens rules
4. Serial constellation (erotica): multiple bonds without forever-promise

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Third as sex toy | Interiority + consequence for them |
| Magical no-jealousy | Earn security or dramatize jealousy |
| Math problem orgy | Emotional change per major beat |
| HEA that abandons a partner silently | Group landing or honest open ending |
| RH clone problem | Differentiate — reverse-harem file |

## Hard

**Ménage vs why-choose**
- Closed triad ≠ RH page-time matrix — if readers expect parallel bonds + group HEA, use RH craft
- Ethical poly vs betrayal plot: marketing words matter more than act count

**When to break “everyone ends together”**
- Erotica may refuse permanence; still don’t ghost a partner’s arc mid-climax without thematic intent
- Open/poly ongoing stories may end mid-network — promise the form in positioning
