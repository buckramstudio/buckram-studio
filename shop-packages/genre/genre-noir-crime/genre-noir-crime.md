# Noir / hardboiled / crime fiction

Engine: moral compromise in a rigged world. Solving the case is secondary to what the case costs the protagonist.

## Light

Job: make least-bad choices under a rigged world — case cost > puzzle toy.

Contract: cynicism earned by violence/corruption; antihero or compromised protagonist; city as pressure vessel; endings often partial, messy, or downbeat.

Must-not-break:
- Institutional antagonist named in the spine (not only the corpse)
- Protagonist’s code stated under pressure — then tested until it cracks or costs
- Knowledge that closes options (noir) vs opens doors (mystery)
- Avoid costume noir: rain/fedora without moral weather
- Voice notices the detail that doesn’t fit — reader interprets

## Normal

**Hardboiled vs noir**

Critic Eddie Duggan (via Jay Gertzman on Goodis):

- **Hardboiled** — backdrop of institutionalized social corruption; tough detective moving through it (Hammett, Chandler; *Black Mask*)
- **Noir** — primary focus interior: psychic imbalance, self-hatred, compulsion; wound also inside

**Moral architecture**

- World is rigged; best efforts → better than nothing, worse than justice
- Enough integrity to **see** corruption, not enough power to **fix** it
- Moral ledger: after each major beat, knowledge gained / options lost
- Neo-noir updates infrastructure (surveillance, algorithmic bias, private security) without pasting 1940s silhouettes

**Voice craft**

Hardboiled perception rhythm:

1. Neutral observation
2. Cynical interpretation
3. Fatalistic snap judgment

- Short punches, then a longer coiled metaphor
- Similes that accuse / expose / rank — social texture, not soft nature pictures
- Competent but not omniscient; misses things; compensates with attention and nerve

**Setting & character**

- City / place as antagonist or mirror; ordinary spaces made ominous
- Alternate violence with stillness; pressure > speed
- Courage and cowardice coexist; hierarchy inversion (criminal clearer ethics than institution)
- Gap between law and justice as engine

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Costume noir | Moral weather first |
| Invincible tough guy | Flaw, miss, cost |
| Mystery fairness bait | Signal cost-over-puzzle contract |
| Soft nature simile fog | Accusatory social texture |

## Hard

**Structural test (mystery vs noir)**

- If knowledge **opens** doors → mystery
- If knowledge **closes** options → noir
- Fair play optional; atmosphere and moral ledger not optional

**Sibling routing**

- Lone investigator shelf craft → 
- Unit/protocol machine → 
- Thriller clock spike without abandoning moral cost → 
- Neon-noir systems dystopia → 

**When to break downbeat ending**
- Partial wins OK; unearned sunny justice breaks the rigged-world contract
- Neo-noir may modernize tools; don’t modernize away the imbalance unless that’s the point
