# Picture book craft

Format-first kidlit: ~32 pages, read-aloud performance, words leave room for art. Not “a short story with pictures.”

## Light

Job: use page turns as suspense beats — setup before the turn, payoff after — while text leaves room for art.

Contract: industry default 32 pages; picture-book text feels like **half** a book. Child-led solution; performance for caregivers who re-read hundreds of times.

Must-not-break:
- Format constraints drive craft (page count, spreads, word band)
- ≥3 memorable page turns (strong books often 6+)
- Complement, don’t duplicate art — don’t narrate what art will show
- Clear protagonist + problem + child-led solution
- End emotional journey without tacked-on moral lecture
- Wrong length (2000+) → re-home as early reader / chapter book

## Normal

**Format constraints**

- ~12–14 double-page spreads of story space; front matter eats early pages
- Word-count bands (guidelines): board/0–3 ~0–300; ages ~4–5 ~400–600; ~6–8 ~600–1000
- **500–800** common trade sweet spot; ~40 words/spread at 500 → 2–4 sentences typical

**Page-turn pacing**

Techniques: question hook; anticipation; visual reveal; pattern break; emotional beat; comedy pause; emotional contrast; before/after.
- Revision: mark every break; flag three consecutive “and then” ends
- Read-aloud test: if three spreads pass with no tension pull, revise

**Visual leave-room**

- Text carries: inner thought/feeling, dialogue, hard-to-draw action, plot advance
- Art carries: physical description, setting, expression, visual gag / counterpoint
- Sometimes art contradicts or expands text — true PB partnership (CB usually does not)
- Traditional pub: author usually does **not** hire illustrator first

**Refrain, rhythm, story shape**

- Vary sentence length; refrain builds prediction; escalate or break on third pass
- Rhyme only if meter natural — forced rhyme worse than clean prose
- Age-theme bands: 2–3 routines/feelings; 4–5 friendship/small fears; 6–8 school/social
- Stakes concrete and immediate — intensity without adult cynicism

| Symptom | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Slideshow feel | Plant setup/payoff at turns |
| Too many words | Cut what art shows |
| Flat read-aloud | Rhythm pass; refrain / length mix |
| Adult solves climax | Return agency to child |
| Moral tacked on | Trust story action |
| Wrong shelf length | Re-home as ER / CB |

## Hard

**Performance economics**

- Annoying empty repetition fatigues adult re-performers
- Onomatopoeia = oral drama + page energy

**Ladder routing**

- Concept/rhythm, almost no plot → board book
- Prose chapters + mini-wins → chapter book
- PB art counterpoint is a feature; CB art support is the norm

**When to break 32 pages**
- 24/40/48 possible but cost/waste; debuts stick to 32
- Wordless / sparse may run shorter; gift collections longer — different contracts
