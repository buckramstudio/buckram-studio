# Near-future / mundane SF

Engine: tomorrow’s tech and systems in a recognizable world — extrapolation from present science and society; usually Earth or Solar System; restraint about FTL, galactic empires, and easy aliens so estrangement hits closer to home.

## Light

Job: put one clear novum step into a world the reader could fly to — policy and infrastructure pressure people, not only lab demos.

Contract: near-future (years–decades) and/or mundane-SF discipline dial; thrillers allowed; restraint ≠ boredom.

Must-not-break:
- Settings readers could recognize bent by new tech or crisis
- Novum already “in effect” or one clear step out
- One novum cluster — overload reads like magic
- Recognition twist: familiar brand/street/law with one wrong new piece
- If you break mundane rules (alien signal, time slip), own the hybrid
- Present-day litfic with a renamed app ≠ estrangement

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- **Near-future SF** (broad trade): next years–decades; familiar geopolitics bent by new tech or crisis
- **Mundane SF** (2004 Clarion/Ryman manifesto lineage): discipline against escapist far-fetched furniture; Earth/Solar System; plausible extensions; skepticism of FTL, easy ET, teleport, time travel as defaults
- Overlaps hard SF; precursors: Ballard “next five minutes”; Soviet near-future SF

**Mundane constraints (optional tight mode)**

Avoid-list vibe: FTL, psi, miracle nanobots, aliens, conscious AI as wish, effortless space riches, immortality, mind uploading, teleport, time travel.

Use as a **dial**, not a purity cult — Ryman noted jokey origins; critics question need for a new label vs hard SF.

**Conventions & pressures**

- Topics: genomics, climate, surveillance, robotics, VR, resource politics, migration
- Character drama under policy and infrastructure
- Obligatory: recognition twist; adoption curve; regulation fight; Tuesday cascade; escape temptation (stars vs stay-and-fix); plausible worst/best futures

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Manifesto scolding instead of story | Dramatize constraints |
| Beige “realistic” prose, no want | Character GMC under systems |
| Secret FTL after mundane pitch | Own hybrid or cut cheat |
| Renamed app, no estrangement | Make the novum change Tuesday |
| Ignoring constraint as creative girdle | Invent within limits |

## Hard

**Manifesto vs story**

- Tight mundane mode is a creative girdle — mastery is invention *inside* it, not policing other SF
- Nancy Kress–adjacent note: very strict hard ≈ mundane for some — comps clarify which reader you court

**When to break the avoid list**
- Alien signal / time slip / conscious AI as wish: valid hybrids if chapter-one promise didn’t sell pure mundane
- Pair climate pressure with ; wetware with without losing human-scale scenes
