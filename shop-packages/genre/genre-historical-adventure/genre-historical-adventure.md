# Historical adventure

Engine: travel, empire, survival, or quest inside a researched past; ordeal geography + period constraint; competence under pressure with historical teeth (disease, distance, politics).

## Light

Job: fuse adventure ordeal with historical plausibility — period logistics must bite the journey.

Contract: danger outside ordinary life *and* past-as-present pressure; dual shelf promise (quest pace + research mattress). Update imperial/colonial templates rather than replaying extractive treasure + scenery.

Must-not-break:
- Journey structure with escalating ordeals
- Period logistics: travel time, weapons, money, letters, medicine
- Local people as agents, not scenery — especially cross-cultural contact
- Set-pieces earned by prior constraint
- Compress carefully — don’t invent easy telegraphs in 1420
- Moral cost of adventure (who pays for the prize)

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Adventure (D’Ammassa): events outside ordinary life, danger, often physical action; pace matters
- Historical adventure lineage: Scott, Dumas, Henty/Stevenson children’s historical adventure sophistication (Trease, Sutcliff…)
- Modes: exploration, wartime courier, pirate-adjacent period sea, overland expedition, court intrigue with chase
- Overlaps imperial romance / lost-world — ethics update required
- Distinct from pure war novel when treasure/map/journey pleasure bills ahead of unit/conscience under orders

**Working checklist**

- Core question pressures each ordeal (not set-piece tourism)
- Ally/rival on the road
- Open cupboards only for *this* choice (mattress research)
- Voice = period content of thought, not forsooth soup
- Return changed — home cannot hold the old self

**Obligatory pressures / moments**
- Period journey with logistics that bite
- Competence under empire/frontier pressure
- Ally/rival on the road
- Set-piece ordeal using era tools
- Moral cost of adventure
- Return changed

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Modern quips every century | Rebuild humor from period reference world |
| Extractive treasure + natives | Agents; who already knew |
| History brochure between fights | Facts under action |
| Invincible vs period mortality | Wound, disease, distance |

## Hard

**Adventure vs war vs lost-world routing**

- Mission + unit + conscience → 
- Hidden civilization / pocket world → 
- Sea society immersive → 
- Stay here when period journey + ordeal geography bill first

**Colonial gaze (obligation, not optional flavor)**

- Mass-literacy adventure often empire-colored — revise who holds knowledge, who pays
- Children’s historical adventure can carry sophistication and controversy (terrorism/war themes in modern kidlit adventure) — age-band honesty required

**When to break dual contract**
- If period is costume only → retag adventure
- If ordeal is absent → retag historical
- Ironic failed return still measures change if opening/closing mirror holds
