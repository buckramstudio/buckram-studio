# Sword & sorcery

Engine: **personal-scale adventure with pulp velocity** — sword-wielding (anti)heroes in invented or legendary milieux where magic works; situational conflicts and survival/gain more than world-saving good-vs-evil crusades.

## Light

Job: keep stakes personal and pace fight-forward — weird magic as obstacle, not prophecy memo.

Contract: pulp adventure pulse; antihero/rogue/outsider common; serial adventures plausible because peace is dull; magic with teeth.

Must-not-break:
- Personal stakes crystal clear every episode
- Weird intrusion (sorcerer, monster, cursed ruin) as obstacle
- Fight geography: blade, fatigue, dirty tricks
- Avoid world-ending stakes every novella (breaks S&S serial logic)
- Magic costly — no deus ex rescue
- Don’t reduce the lane to 80s bikini-armor stereotype

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Term popularized by Fritz Leiber (1961) answering Moorcock — distinct from cloak-and-sword historical adventure
- Origin pulse: Robert E. Howard (Kull → Conan); Weird Tales DNA
- Lin Carter / Murphy summaries: action in invented land; personal/mercenary aims vs dark magic
- Lower stakes than epic → serial adventures plausible

**S&S vs epic / high**

| | Sword & sorcery | Epic / high |
|--|-----------------|-------------|
| Scale | Personal, city, kingdom slice | World/war/cosmic |
| Hero | Antihero, rogue, barbarian outsider | Often chosen ones / ensemble |
| Magic | Costly, often villain-coded or dangerous | Varies; can be systemic wonder |
| Moral | Gray, situational | Clearer good vs evil common (not always) |
| Pace | Pulp fight-forward | Campaign and map politics |

**Obligatory pressures**
- Job or grudge hook → weird intrusion → fight geography → bargain with darkness → loot and consequence → road onward

**Working checklist**
- Sense of wonder + weird dread, not only arena stats
- Urban dens, caravan roads, decadent cities as stages
- Female warriors have deep precedent (Jirel; Valeria) — don’t default to damsel furniture
- “Heroic fantasy” sometimes broader/less garish label — overlaps but not identical

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Deus ex magic rescue | Strength/cunning addresses problem; magic costs |
| Conan clone, no voice | Landscape, theme, idiom |
| World-ending every novella | Personal/city stakes; serial logic |
| Gratuitous cruelty as “grit” | Theme + tether |
| Writing down to “pulp” | Polish pace and atmosphere |

## Hard

**Themes under pulp**
- Liberty, tyranny, rise/fall of civilizations, agency (Howard → Moorcock → Delany) available without epic campaign furniture
- Outsider protecting innocents civilization fails (Robin Hood mythic root per Alexandrian summary)
- Literary/“new” S&S may show epic scope through S&S eyes if adventure pulse stays

**When to break personal scale**
- One board-changing kingdom crisis OK if next story can still be a job/grudge — don’t permanently promote to epic cosmology every time
- Dying Earth / other planet/dimension settings still S&S if personal pulp pulse holds
