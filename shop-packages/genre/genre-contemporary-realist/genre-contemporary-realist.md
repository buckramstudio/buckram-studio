# Contemporary realist fiction

Engine: present-day (or near-present) character drama in a recognizable world; low or no speculative furniture; social and psychological pressure from work, love, money, kinship, health, politics-as-lived.

## Light

Job: make ordinary causality carry desire and consequence — no speculative exit ramps.

Contract: events obey familiar physics and social logic; extraordinary events are human, not magical; can be commercial or literary (label follows language density and plot shape).

Must-not-break:
- Realist bill: no late speculative twist that breaks the contract
- Desire + obstacle still need escalation 
- Specificity of now (class, place, labor) without trendy-name clutter that ages overnight
- Avoid diary of moods without turns
- Ending feels true to the dramatized life (not necessarily twisty)
- “Nothing happens” only if quiet novel contract is intentional 

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Realist contract: represent familiar activities and experiences as they are — avoid grandiose/exotic/speculative as the engine
- Contemporary setting: generally post-WWII / present-adjacent lived world (wiki “contemporary literature” period sense) — craft use = recognizable now
- Can be commercial or literary; overlaps domestic fiction, workplace novel, adult coming-of-age, slice-of-life
- Social and psychological pressure from work, love, money, kinship, health, politics-as-lived

**Working checklist**

- Specificity of now without disposable brand-name fog
- Subtext in dialogue and gesture (, )
- Ordinary event with outsized meaning (move, diagnosis, reunion, layoff)
- Interior turn visible in behavior
- Consequence that sticks — no genre reset button

**Obligatory pressures**
- Present-day constraint without speculative exit
- Ordinary event with outsized meaning
- Relationship pressure scene that cannot be solved by plot coupon
- Interior turn visible in behavior
- Consequence that sticks
- Ending true to dramatized life

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Diary of moods | Add turns and external pressure |
| Issue checklist | Route issues through character want |
| Speculative twist | Cut or rebill as slipstream/SF |
| Generic suburbia | Local texture: class, labor, place |

## Hard

**Realism spectrum (diagnostic)**

| Mode | Grain | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Literary realism | Faithful ordinary subject-matter; avoid romantic idealization | Default contemporary realist bill |
| Naturalism | Heredity/environment as inescapable force; often darker | When systems crush choice — don’t soft-pedal |
| Kitchen sink / social realism | Working-class domestic cramped life; social structures on trial | Class texture as pressure, not tourism |
| Commercial realist | Same causality; clearer dramatic question / pace | Route also |

- Realism as technique ≠ socialist realism (state program) — don’t confuse craft label with doctrine
- Dirty/grunge realist traditions (late-century “gritty existences”) still need want and turn — grit alone is wallpaper

**When to break strict mimesis**
- Brief lyric/symbolic heightening OK if causality stays ordinary
- Persistent uncanny → slipstream / magical realism — don’t smuggle under “literary”
