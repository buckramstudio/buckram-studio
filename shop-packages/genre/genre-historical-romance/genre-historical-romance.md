# Historical romance (HR)

Engine: period constraint as courtship pressure — manners, law, class, reputation, travel speed, and marriage-as-contract force couple choices; research is part of the promise.

## Light

Job: make period rules create tension contemporary CR must invent — access, reputation, marriage math.

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Era clusters: Regency/Georgian, Victorian, medieval, Western/American historical, mid-century, growing non-Western shelves
- Heyer lineage: researched past as romance engine
- Mass-market HR historically paperback-heavy; fortunes track format trends

**What the period must do**

- Reputation — private moments cost social survival (esp. women in many eras)
- Chaperones / access — effort to get alone time *is* declaration
- Marriage as social/economic contract — love vs family strategy
- Class/station — resolve disapproval, don’t handwave
- Logistics — letters, carriage days, no phones — delay/misunderstanding structural

**Research & language**

- Telling details: food, fabric, ballroom smell, journey length — iceberg tip
- Plot-relevant facts: prefer multiple sources
- Selective vocabulary anchors; avoid anachronistic slang and modern therapy-speak
- Soften unromantic hygiene realities when they only distract — don’t put cars in the wrong century
- XX and move on while drafting — don’t stall mid-scene on dress fabric

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Ballroom tourism | Make rules force choices |
| Info-dump social history | One period anchor per scene |
| Modern emotional diction | Era-plausible speech for the feeling |
| Anachronism snap | Kill modern idioms; history-check |
| Research stalls draft | Placeholder → finish arc → verify |
| HEA optional | Retag historical fiction |
