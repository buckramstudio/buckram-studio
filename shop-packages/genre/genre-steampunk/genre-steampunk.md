# Steampunk

Engine: **retrofuturist steam-age tech as argument** — anachronistic steam / clockwork inventions reshape society; Victorian (or analogous industrial) aesthetics serve a question about progress, power, and who pays.

## Light

Job: make steam-age tech load-bearing — inventions change power, labor, and plot, not only wardrobe.

Contract: SF / AH (often Victorian–Edwardian or steam secondary world); Verne/Wells lineage of imagined machines; social cost of invention visible; soft temporal stop near WWI in many definitions.

Must-not-break:
- Technology is load-bearing — remove the machines and the plot collapses
- Invention has a social cost (who builds, who rides, who breathes the smoke)
- Scientific / engineering reasoning for wonders (even pulpy) — not unexplained magic
- Period constraint + acceleration — manners/class/empire still pressure
- Brass without the question = costume party
- Empire cosplay without colonized or laboring viewpoints flattens the “punk”

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- SF (often AH) incorporating retrofuturistic tech inspired by 19th-century industrial steam design
- Common frames: alternate Victorian / Edwardian; American frontier under steam; secondary world with steam as primary power
- Anachronistic inventions as the era *might have imagined* them — not mere Neo-Victorian costume drama
- Term coined by K. W. Jeter (1987 *Locus* letter) by analogy with cyberpunk, for Powers / Blaylock / Jeter “gonzo-historical” Victorian fantasies
- SFE: temporal real-world terminus often just before WWI; Dickensian London as seedbed (filth + pistons) behind early visions
- Hybrid-friendly: fantasy, horror, historical, adventure — but the **technological hypothesis** should still pressure plot

**Core contract**

1. Technology is load-bearing
2. Invention has a social cost
3. Scientific / engineering reasoning for wonders
4. Period constraint + acceleration along steam paths

**Obligatory pressures**

- Machine demonstration (tech works or fails — rules earned)
- Social consequence (class, city, or body pays)
- Journey that requires the tech
- Knowledge bargain that shifts power
- Confrontation with industrial force
- Aftermath: world changed by the device’s existence

**Working checklist**
- Visible mechanics as scenic texture serving stakes, not checklist cosplay
- Class verticality rewritten by machines (factory floor vs airship deck)
- Not limited to England: U.S. Civil War AH steam, Meiji/Taishō Japan, etc.
- Later diesel aesthetics → 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Goggles-and-gears, no argument | Name who pays for the invention |
| Magic labeled “aether” | Add mechanistic constraint — or bill as gaslamp |
| Tech tourism | Tie each wonder to character want |
| Empire cosplay | Include laboring / colonized viewpoints |

## Hard

**Steampunk vs gaslamp fantasy**

| | **Steampunk** | **Gaslamp (fantasy)** |
|--|---------------|------------------------|
| Root shelf | Science fiction / AH | Historical fantasy (often gothic-adjacent) |
| Driving question | What if steam-tech advanced / branched this way? | What if magic / supernatural were in that society? |
| Wonders explained? | Yes — science, engineering, mad chemistry | Often no — uncanny simply *is* |
| Engine test | Remove the machines → plot collapses | Remove magic/uncanny → plot collapses; machines may be set dressing |

- Kaja Foglio coined “gaslamp fantasy” (~2006) partly to distance *Girl Genius*-adjacent work from convention steampunk
- Carriger-style paranormal-in-crinoline often reads gaslamp even when airships appear
- Hybrids exist — name the **primary engine** for comps and cover promise

**SFE diagnostics**

- Victorian London as Jonbar-point city: industry + finance + squalor — the modern world being born
- Later lifestyle/couture wave (post-~2006) can occult the present into unobtainable nostalgia — fiction still needs story stakes beyond aesthetic
- Proto works (Moorcock Bastable, Priest *Space Machine*) may not sort with later US gonzo-Victorian cluster — own your lineage

**When to break “no magic”**
- Science fantasy / aether OK if constraints are consistent and you don’t market pure SF steampunk
- Gothic excavation when haunted house / ancestral sin dominates →
