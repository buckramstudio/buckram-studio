# Paranormal erotica

Engine: **supernatural rules that change desire, body, or bonding** — hunger, heat cycles, glamour, immortality — while sexual journey (erotica) or love+sex (erotic romance) stays central. Distinct from urban fantasy’s case/quest-first promise.

## Light

Job: make supernatural rules change intimacy — fangs/instincts are engines, not wallpaper; sex-spine or HEA+sex decides shelf vs UF.

Contract: rules consistency; strip paranormal and erotic engine breaks; bond/compulsion never skips emotional consent.

Must-not-break:
- Supernatural changes intimacy — senses, instinct, magical feedback
- Rules consistency when plot pokes them
- Mate bond / compulsion attracts — does not finish trust or skip consent
- Predator/prey power needs counterweight agency
- Glamour/mind-influence that removes refusal = dark/compulsion frame — label honestly
- UF mystery drowning sex-spine fails erotica positioning

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Vampires, shifters, witches, fae, demons/angels, psychics, ghosts — plus some markets’ alien/time-travel parking under “paranormal”
- **Erotica:** sexual nature/dynamic discovery is the spine
- **Erotic romance / PNR:** HEA + supernatural complicating courtship 

**Mate bonds / biological compulsion**
- Bond can supply attraction; **must not skip emotional consent**
- Show protagonist claiming desire, not only enduring biology
- Rejected bond, delayed recognition, political pack interference = conflict fuel

**Consent / power craft**
- Feeding, venom, heat: aftercare and bodily consequence are story tissue
- Pack/harem politics → often RH-adjacent 

**Reader promises**
1. Supernatural changes intimacy
2. Rules consistency
3. Strip test
4. Heat + world balance

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Mate bond = instalove done | Bond attracts; trust arcs |
| Human as passive snack | Agency + competence |
| UF mystery drowning sex-spine | Cut or sexualize the spine |
| Compulsion unmarked | Content notes + diegetic ethics |
| Creature softened to costume | Keep otherness — or use contemporary |

## Hard

**PNR vs erotica vs UF**
- HEA-focused PNR readers ≠ erotica open endings ≠ UF case readers — position before comps
- Secondary-world magic-first heat → often rather than Earth-masquerade paranormal

**When to break “keep the creature other”**
- Softened “human with quirks” can work for contemporary-paranormal romance hybrids — then don’t promise monster-erotica otherness
