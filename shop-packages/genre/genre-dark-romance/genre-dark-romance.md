# Dark romance

Engine: morally gray / high-heat / taboo-edge courtship that still owes a romance HEA/HFN for most trade readers; darkness pressures the couple.

## Light

Job: keep darkness as structural pressure on the bond without dropping the romance endgame (unless you honestly leave the shelf).

Contract: central love story + optimistic couple landing for standard dark-romance buyers; state tragedy endgames in positioning if you use them.

Must-not-break:
- Meet under imbalance — power, danger, or moral asymmetry visible early
- Attraction despite (or because of) wrongness costs something
- Consent craft under darkness — reader tracks agency 
- Black Moment with moral weight
- HEA/HFN that earns the darkness (not a soft wipe)
- Boundary beat — what the story will / won’t romanticize

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Market clusters: antihero / captive / stalker-adjacent / mafia / bully / morally gray billionaire — tropes vary; **romance contract** is the shelf test
- Reader split is live: many want emotional HEA/HFN; some want tragedy — state endgame in blurb/comps
- Heat often load-bearing; if sex is the category engine and HEA optional → 
- Distinct from romantic suspense (external thriller threat) though hybrids exist

**Working checklist**
- Name the darkness engine (crime world, captivity frame, bully hierarchy…) and keep its rules
- Dual GMC survives the heat 
- Avoid “abuse = romance” without transformation cost
- Trigger / content signaling for marketing honesty
- Comps match heat + endgame

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Dropping HEA while selling “romance” | Earn HFN/HEA or retag dark erotica / literary |
| Darkness as aesthetic only | Make it block Want / press Lie |
| Consent fog as endorsement of harm | Cost on page; clear fantasy/negotiation frame |
| Confused with RS or dark erotica | Check threat engine vs sex-as-spine vs couple endgame |

## Hard

**Romance vs dark erotica boundary**
- Romance/erotic romance: lasting romantic love question + HEA/HFN required
- Erotica: sexual becoming as spine; HEA optional
- Dark romance that abandons optimistic landing while using romance packaging breaks the mass-market romance promise (wiki/RWA-style baseline still the expectation many buyers bring)

**Consent under asymmetry (craft, not sermon)**
- Enthusiastic, ongoing, specific consent — verbal or clearly embodied; silence ≠ yes
- CNC/noncon *fantasy* frames need diegetic negotiation or clear fantasy contract
- Harm meant as harm must read as harm with consequence — not spicy garnish
- Aftercare / changed terms after intensity is story tissue when the darkness is load-bearing

**Edge diagnostics**
- Identity→Essence clash: love asks them to become someone their darkness resists 
- Romantic suspense bleed: if external clock/threat is primary and couple secondary, check 
- When to break HEA: only with honest positioning outside standard dark-romance buyer promise — do not invent a “secret HEA-optional romance” category mid-blurb
