# War fiction / military / wartime historical

Engine: organized violence and its human systems — combat unit, command, home front, occupation, aftermath; loyalty, fear, moral injury, and mission pressure; period accuracy serves lived cost, not only hardware.

## Light

Job: make unit/home-front social world and moral injury under orders the engine — not gear porn.

Contract: anti-war and combatant-brotherhood traditions both exist — tone is a contract, not a virtue contest. Distinct from military SF (speculative tech/doctrine elsewhere).

Must-not-break:
- Unit as social world: ranks, specialties, nicknames, debts
- Sensory combat selective — geography and confusion over video-game omniscience
- Moral questions under fire
- Aftermath: injury, silence, political betrayal of veterans
- Research doctrine/kit/timelines — then dramatize gaps
- Don’t fold into generic adventure without institutional pressure

## Normal

**Modes**

| Mode | Center of gravity |
|------|-------------------|
| War fiction | Battlefield or war-preoccupied civilians; combat + consequence |
| Military (contemporary) | Modern forces, ops realism, bureaucracy, deployment/reintegration |
| Wartime historical | Specific campaign/home front; historical fiction obligations apply |

- Lineage: epic → 19th-c realist battle novels → WWI anti-war boom → WWII theatres → Vietnam memory → later limited wars
- Home front and returnee novels count when war organizes the emotional plot

**Working checklist**

- Home front braid: letters, rationing, propaganda, waiting
- Mission or campaign pressure with doctrine/constraints
- Combat advances character; empty spectacle fails
- Brotherhood / unit bond as emotional spine; command burden as internal conflict

**Obligatory pressures**
- Unit / home-front social world
- Mission or campaign pressure
- Selective combat/bombardment set-piece
- Moral injury choice under orders or chaos
- Letter / leave / news braid
- Aftermath wound, silence, betrayal, or uneasy peace

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Gear porn | Interiority + moral question |
| Atrocity tourism | Lived cost, not spectacle |
| Invincible protagonist | Disposable-extra audit |
| Past wars + unmediated modern politics | Period mediation |
| Generic adventure label | Keep institutional pressure |
