# Ghost story

Engine: unfinished business made present. Haunting as pressure — malevolence, grief, or both — arriving by crescendo into ordinary life.

## Light

Job: manage atmosphere and crescendo so presence of the dead (or believed-dead) changes the living — suggest more than show.

Contract: familiar settings make “this could happen to me” possible; reticence over afterlife bureaucracy.

Must-not-break:
- Presence affects the living — apparition, poltergeist, imprint, curse
- Atmosphere + managed crescendo over constant shock
- Reticence: don’t expose all supernatural machinery
- Establish haunt rules before climax use
- Aftermath cost — what cannot be recovered
- Amiable helper ghost ≠ dread promise (clear the shelf)

## Normal

**Genre contract**

- Ghost usually malevolent or odious in classic short form (James); benevolent ghosts shift toward fantasy/comfort — different promise
- Familiar settings/characters; slight haze of distance often helps

**M. R. James recipe (still portable)**

- Introduce actors placidly at ordinary business; ominous thing puts out its head unobtrusively → insistently → holds the stage
- Talk you might hear any day; antiquarian object/manuscript/whistle as pivot common
- Leave a loophole for natural explanation — so narrow it is barely practicable
- Horror + malevolence + reticence; avoid gore-for-kicks that kills atmosphere

**Novel-length expansion**
- James excelled at short form; novels need subplot/theme mirror (haunting = grief, guilt, inheritance)
- Escalation: odd detail → pattern → confrontation → aftermath cost 
- POV: close perception + possible unreliability 

| Mode | Flavor |
|------|--------|
| Antiquarian | Scholar + cursed find |
| Domestic haunt | House/family secret |
| Revenge ghost | Justice unfinished |
| Grief ghost | Love/loss; may soften malevolence — clear the promise |
| Folk ghost | Local legend, seasonal, communal  |

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Full afterlife bureaucracy | Restore reticence; imply |
| Jump scares, no crescendo | Rebuild James ladder |
| Amiable helper when sold dread | Soften promise or darken ghost |
| Bones of theory visible | Cut lecture on why ghosts exist |

## Hard

**Ambiguity loophole**

- Dual-readable signs (grief vs entity) only if that is the promise — gothic doubt mode
- Psychological haunt: mind/perception collapse can share pages with “real” ghost if rules stay consistent under either reading

**When to break malevolence default**

- Grief-ghost / comfort haunting is a different contract — market as such; do not bait Jamesian dread then deliver Casper
- Folk/communal ghosts may serve justice rather than sadism — still need crescendo and cost
