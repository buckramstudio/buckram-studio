# LGBTQ+ romance

Engine: same HEA/HFN romance contract with identity, community, and/or disclosure stakes that vary by submarket; pairing is not a pronoun swap on a default straight template.

## Light

Job: deliver category romance (central love + optimistic landing) with lived queer texture chosen deliberately — central conflict, texture, or nearly invisible.

Contract: pairing-specific markets (M/M, F/F, bi, trans, nonbinary, ace-spectrum) have different tropes and heat norms; comps matter.

Must-not-break:
- Attraction + obstacle isn’t only “society disapproves” wallpaper — personal Want/Need still drive
- Visibility/disclosure beat when relevant — or clear post-disclosure choice
- Black Moment tests the bond’s Truth, not only cardboard bigotry
- HEA/HFN honors the pairing’s lived future (no closet reset sold as category romance)
- Pairing conventions ≠ copy-paste hetero beats
- If erotic-primary without HEA → erotica pairing files, not this shelf

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- M/M, F/F, bisexual, trans, nonbinary, ace-spectrum romance are distinct markets with different tropes, heat norms, and gatekeeping histories
- Trade vs indie/KU ecosystems differ; comps and cover codes matter 
- Identity can be central conflict, texture, or almost invisible — **choose deliberately**; “issue book” vs “love story in a queer life” are different promises
- Own-voices expectations are reader-driven; craft still needs research humility and specificity 

**Obligatory pressures / moments**

- Attraction + obstacle with personal Want/Need
- Visibility / disclosure when relevant (coming out, being outed, chosen family vs blood) — or explicit post-disclosure frame
- Community texture (friends, scene, workplace, online) as pressure or support
- Micro-status and safety geography — who can hold hands where; how danger/ease shapes intimacy
- Black Moment that tests the bond’s Truth
- HEA/HFN that honors lived future

**Craft checklist**
- Heat and kink norms vary by lane — match comps
- Intersectionality: race, disability, class, faith change the obstacle map
- Avoid trauma-as-only-plot; joy and desire are also engines
- Microaggressions as texture ≠ sole antagonist engine

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Straight romance with pronouns swapped | Lived texture; read pairing comps |
| Bigotry-only antagonist | Couple engine + personal Want/Need |
| Fetishization / tragedy bait under HEA marketing | Honest endgame + respect |
| One queer pairing’s tropes as universal | Submarket-specific comps |
| Erotica without HEA sold as romance | Retag to erotica pairing files |

## Hard

**Issue-book vs love-story-in-a-queer-life**

- Centralized identity conflict can be the romance obstacle *if* it still runs through GMC and couple Truth — not PSA structure
- Post-disclosure / “already out” frames are valid; absence of coming-out is not absence of queer craft (community, safety geography, micro-status still available)
- Ace-spectrum and low-heat queer romance: intimacy contract must be explicit in positioning — don’t bait high-heat readers

**Cross-lane with erotica**

- Same pairing letters can mean romance, erotic romance, or erotica — apply HEA/sex-spine tests from / 
- When to break “community texture required”: only if isolation *is* the thematic pressure (closet, exile) and the void is intentional, not lazy worldbuilding
