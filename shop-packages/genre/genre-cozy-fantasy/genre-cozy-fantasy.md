# Cozy fantasy

Engine: **comfort, community, and low-to-moderate stakes in a fantastic world** — slice-of-life warmth, found family, craft/food/place love; hope and kindness as the emotional contract (conflict allowed on a dial, not erased).

## Light

Job: deliver comfort and belonging in a fantastic world — conflict without misery-forward trauma.

Contract: community / friendship / healing / everyday life load-bearing; hope and kindness as the felt promise.

Must-not-break:
- Slice-of-life warmth central to emotional development
- Found family / healthy bonds with real friction (gentle ≠ conflict-free)
- Stakes can be personal/local: belonging, livelihood, dignity
- If violence/trauma dominates felt experience, you’ve left the contract
- Marketing honesty — don’t bait grimdark or trauma-dump cozy buyers
- Magic rules still exist; cozy ≠ soft-magic chaos

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Emotive subgenre (like cozy mystery): promises comforting affect more than a fixed plot skeleton
- Setting grain: inn, shop, small town, single beloved location; tea and baking as recurring sensory anchors
- Label boom ~2020+ (BookTok; pandemic escapism); precedents claimed (*Hobbit*, *Howl*) before the marketing word
- Indie breakout pattern: Baldree *Legends & Lattes* → trade chase; often queer-friendly casts

**Conflict dial (spectrum)**

| Dial | Pattern | Example grain |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Small** | Little/no danger; world as warm stage | Tea dragons, gentle romance chores |
| **Medium** | Dash of threat; cinnamon rolls still win | Café vs local muscle; soft magical risk |
| **Large** | Epic dangers exist but cozy emotional energy + slice-of-life core persist | Tea shop + deadly queen — warmth still the promise |

**Obligatory pressures / moments**
- Nesting beat: claiming a place (lease, hearth, garden, shop)
- Welcome scene: stranger → guest → family
- Craft problem: recipe, spell, renovation, harvest — solvable with help
- Soft antagonist: bureaucracy, rival, loneliness, self-doubt — not only Dark Lords
- Table scene: shared food as covenant
- Scare that resolves toward safety; community win visible on-page

**Working checklist**
- Promise warmth in chapter one — voice and sensory welcome
- Competence at craft (coffee, magic tutoring, shopkeeping) as plot spine
- Humor and sensory pleasure (food, textiles, seasons)
- HEA/HFN-adjacent emotional landing even when not romance-shelved
- Gentle magic common; high magic OK if not misery-forward

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Conflict-free stagnation | Want + craft/community obstacle |
| “Cozy” label on grim plots with pastry cameos | Retag or restore warmth contract |
| Saccharine cast, no specificity/humor | Particular people; friction |
| Queer/found-family checkbox | Relationship scenes on-page |
| Never rests | Dwell time is part of the engine |

## Hard

**Dial edges (retag decisions)**
- Large-dial books still need cozy *emotional* energy — epic furniture alone doesn’t earn the label
- Slow-life isekai / portal comfort → also check 
- Mystery-forward with low gore in fantasy town → may be cozy mystery hybrid 
- Contrast: if cruelty and moral darkness are the pleasure → 

**When to break “small stakes only”**
- Personal stakes can be life-changing (exile, livelihood, dignity) without battlefield POV
- Romance heat OK if warmth/community remain the shelf promise — don’t bait erotica-only readers
