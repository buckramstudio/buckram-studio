# Fantasy erotica

Engine: **secondary-world or magic-system constraints on desire** — courts, curses, magical binding, nonhuman customs — with sexual journey primary (erotica) or HEA+sex load-bearing (erotic romance / romantasy-heat).

## Light

Job: make magic/world rules change the bedroom — secondary-world constraints are erotic engines, not elf costumes on human porn.

Contract: magic affects intimacy; power is political; heat/plot balance honest to shelf (erotica vs romantasy HEA).

Must-not-break:
- Magic/rules affect desire logistics — binding, heat magic, telepathy, potion costs
- World texture through romantic/erotic pressure — not textbook dumps
- Power is political — court rank, species law, temple rule shape who may refuse
- Adult characters only — “young apprentice” aesthetics must remain adult-coded
- Quest padding fails erotica; lore-free orgies fail fantasy readers who came for both
- Magical compulsion: claim desire or honest dark framing

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- High/secondary-world fantasy + explicit sexual spine ≠ automatic romantasy — **billing** matters: love HEA vs sexual becoming vs quest
- Magic that compels lust, fertility rites, political marriages, fae bargains = erotic engines when they change agency
- Monster/creature fantasy often overlaps → 

**Reader promises**
1. Magic/rules affect the bedroom
2. World texture without textbook
3. Power is political
4. Heat/plot balance for the chosen shelf

**Consent / power craft**
- Magical geas / mating compulsion: claiming of desire or dark framing
- Species power gaps: counterweight agency
- Ritual sex as worldbuilding: character-level yes, not only priestly decree — unless the book is *about* coerced rite (dark honesty)

**Structures that fit**
- Political marriage → sexual negotiation becomes alliance plot
- Curse broken/deepened through intimacy
- Magic school/temple (adult) initiation arc
- Court intrigue alternating with escalating encounters

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Human porn in elf costumes | Magic/culture must change desire logistics |
| Sanderson-law lecture mid-act | Teach rules through cost to the dynamic |
| Romantasy HEA bait → grim open ending | Position as fantasy erotica |
| Quest drowns sex-spine | Cut side quests or switch shelf |
| Nonhuman consent handwave | Embodiment + refusal possibility |

## Hard

**Earth-masquerade vs secondary world**
- Paranormal (masquerade-earth) vs secondary-world fantasy erotica — different lore loads; don’t dump UF case plots into fantasy erotica
- Romantasy distribution often demands HEA — if you need open sexual-journey ending, market as fantasy erotica

**When to break “magic must change every act”**
- Breath/aftermath that renegotiates bargains or court standing still counts as spine tissue
- Soft-magic intimacy still needs *some* constraint (privacy, species custom, political cost)
