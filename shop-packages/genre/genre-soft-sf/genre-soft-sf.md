# Soft / sociological science fiction

Engine: **culture, language, psyche, and institutions as the novum** — soft sciences (or human emotional consequence) foregrounded; physics may be lighter or handwaved so the thought experiment can stay on people and systems.

## Light

Job: make the speculative *social* hypothesis the engine — people and institutions under a new rule.

Contract: psychology, sociology, anthropology, linguistics, or political systems create the estrangement; character and culture carry the book.

Must-not-break:
- One cultural/psychological novum cluster — not contradictory soft miracles
- Extrapolate daily life: manners, law, love, work under the new social rule
- Soft ≠ sloppy: track social rules as carefully as hard SF tracks delta-v
- Estrangement still required — contemporary drama in futuristic costume isn’t SF
- Enabling physics may be furniture — declare the handwave and stop apologizing
- Get the people right (inbox craft rule)

## Normal

**Two useful definitions** (Brave New Words / Nicholls cluster)

1. **Soft-sciences SF** — extrapolations of psychology, sociology, anthropology, linguistics, political science (not primarily physics/engineering)
2. **Science-light SF** — science not load-bearing; character, society, mythic feel dominate

Neither pole is a strict taxonomy. Soft is a tendency on an axis opposite hard — “not very precise” (Nicholls) but craft-useful.

**Genre shape**
- Wells model (Anderson): concentrate on characters’ emotions and interactions more than the gadget’s patent
- 1950s “inward” break (McGuirk): Bester, Leiber, Bradbury, Blish — characterization over tech demo; New Wave grows from this
- McGuirk splits: **humanist SF** (human condition / human-driven change) vs **SF noir** (dark human nature, often dystopian)
- Touchstones often cited: Le Guin (*Left Hand of Darkness*, *The Dispossessed*), Herbert *Dune* (politics over hardware), Vance/Delany linguistic engineering

**Soft sciences as engines**
- Anthropology / gender / kinship — alien or future societies that estrange *our* defaults
- Linguistics — Sapir–Whorf playgrounds (*Languages of Pao*, *Babel-17*, *Arrival*)
- Political science / utopia-dystopia — institutions as speculative machines
- Psychology — identity, memory, empathy tech, interior estrangement

**Obligatory pressures / moments**
- Norm collision: protagonist’s assumption fails against the society’s logic
- Translation crisis: language, gender, ritual, or value that won’t map 1:1
- Institutional Tuesday: bureaucracy, kinship, market, faith as lived pressure
- Intimate stakes under the novum (not only state-scale)
- Choice that reveals ethos; return with changed eyes

**Working checklist**
- People-logic consistency > gadget patent drawings
- Research real social science lightly, invent boldly, stay internally coherent
- Avoid “Planet of Hats” monocultures — texture factions and dissent

|---------|-----|
| Soft as excuse for incoherent physics *and* people | Lock social rules |

## Hard

**Axis edges (retag)**
- Soft sociological classics can still be “hard” about anthropology/linguistics — hardness is rigor of the chosen science, not only physics 
- If institutional cruelty + closed future leads → also check / 
- Space-opera clothes with soft-science heart: declare primary promise (spectacle vs social hypothesis)

**Humanist vs SF noir**
- McGuirk split changes ending and tone contracts — hope/agency vs dark human nature
- Cyberpunk inherits some soft-SF DNA (systems, street-level institutions) — don’t force soft label if tech noir leads 

**When to break science-light permission**
- If climax depends on a physics cheat that contradicts earlier soft-science rigor, you’ve broken reader trust twice
- Suvin warning (parent SF): costume without estrangement isn’t the soft-SF project
