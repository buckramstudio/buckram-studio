# Sports romance

Engine: athlete / team / public-performance milieu pressures the central love story; remove the game and the romance should weaken.

## Light

Job: make sport complicate intimacy — schedule, body, team, and public eye as courtship pressure.

Contract: romance-primary HEA/HFN; sport supplies clocks and identity stakes; heat varies (sweet→erotic) but endgame stays romance.

Must-not-break:
- Performance stage that matters to identity
- Body ledger beat (injury, aging, exhaustion) affecting the bond
- Career vs couple choice (trade, relocation, retirement, cycle)
- Black Moment tied to sport consequence, not only generic breakup
- HEA accounts for athletic life
- Sport rules legible enough for stakes

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Common spines: player/player, player/coach (power ethics), player/reporter, athlete/single parent, rivals-to-lovers
- League/season calendar = external clocks; trade/draft/injury as turns
- Public vs private load-bearing: media, fans, teammates, brand
- Non-romance sports fiction = different primary engine 

**Working checklist**
- Dual GMC: athletic Want ≠ romantic Need
- Avoid play-by-play dumps; POV limitation in set-pieces
- Power-imbalance pairings need consent/ethics craft
- Research labor realities (contracts, amateur status, NIL) when plot-relevant
- Training / practice earns the set-piece only if capability or character changes

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Jersey costume romance | Force scenes only the sport can generate |
| Win-the-championship ignores couple contract | Couple landing must still earn HEA |
| Toxic team culture romanticized without cost | Show residue / choice |
| Interchangeable sport labels | Specific rules, calendar, body costs |

## Hard

**Parent sports structure × romance**
- Contest clock / body ledger / team micro-society / public vs private — each should pressure the bond, not only decorate
- Themes of sports fiction (glory vs cost, loyalty vs self) become couple tests when Want and Need collide at a trade deadline or injury

**Power / ethics edges**
- Coach–player, doctor–athlete, reporter–source: romance readers still track agency; blur ≠ erase 
- Fame asymmetry: one partner’s brand can erase the other’s privacy — Black Moment material

**When to break “sport must matter”**
- Never for this shelf — if sport is only meet-cute wallpaper, you have contemporary romance with a hobby; retag or rewrite
- Speculative sports (blood tournaments, magical leagues) still need rule clarity and body/public pressure
