# Lost-world / expedition

Engine: discovery of a hidden place (valley, plateau, city, pocket ecosystem) + isolation; wonder and threat braid; getting in is plot, getting out is character.

## Light

Job: earn wonder through ordeal and contact ethics — hidden place must change the traveler or the outside world.

Contract: late-Victorian adventure-romance lineage (unknown civilization and/or extinct-life pocket); modern variants hide via anomaly, deep time, magic, or secondary-world displacement.

Must-not-break:
- Expedition roster with conflicting motives (science, greed, faith, escape)
- Threshold crossing clearly marked
- Inhabitants with politics — not only monsters or noble-savage diorama
- Update colonial templates: who gets to “discover,” who already knew
- Return changes outside world or traveler — else tourism
- Map / relic / knowledge as contested object

## Normal

**Genre shape**

- Began as late-Victorian adventure romance; remains popular into 21st c.
- Formative: Haggard *King Solomon’s Mines*; Doyle *The Lost World*; Burroughs; Hilton *Lost Horizon* (Shangri-La meme / paradise idealization)
- Contents: lost civilization, prehistoric survivors, utopia/dystopia pocket, hollow-earth variants
- Early geography: “unexplored” Africa, South America, Central Asia, Australia → later Antarctica / poles as refuge
- Satire branch (Bulwer-Lytton *Vril*, Butler *Erewhon*) uses discovery for social critique — different promise than romantic adventure

**Modern problem (Stableford / Becker)**

- Increasing geographic knowledge → lost lands must be well hidden or displaced beyond magical/dimensional boundary
- Displacement transforms significance toward secondary world / otherworld (Stableford)
- Logical evolution toward planetary romance when Earth corners run out (Becker)

**Working checklist**

- Wonder beat that earns the expedition
- Hostility of place or inhabitants with internal logic
- Civilization contact ethics (exploit vs understand)
- Return or remain choice with cost to both worlds
- Survival ledger when isolation bites 

**Obligatory pressures / moments**
- Threshold crossing into isolation / discovery
- Wonder beat
- Hostility with logic
- Contested map / relic / knowledge
- Contact ethics
- Return or remain

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Museum tour of wonders | Ordeal + turn |
| Extractive treasure unexamined | Who pays; who already knew |
| Dinosaurs as only joke | Ecosystem + politics |
| Portal voids exit cost | Carry knowledge/scar out |

## Hard

**Romance adventure vs satire vs utopia dial**

- Romantic adventure: ordeal + discovery pleasure
- Swiftian satire: pocket world as mirror — signal early
- Paradise / superior inhabitants (*Lost Horizon* lineage): philosophy/social comment may outrank chase — still need threshold cost

**Colonial critique (craft obligation)**

- Genre rose with Western archaeology “discovering” civilizations — templates often exoticize
- Deane et al. critique of imperial masculinity in lost-world fiction — update who holds knowledge and agency

**Sibling routing**

- Scarcity/environment first → 
- Period journey without hidden world → 
- Otherworld displacement dominant → fantasy/SF secondary-world tools

**When to break “unexplored corner”**
- Anomaly / deep time / sealed ethics / dimensional hide is on-contract for modern settings
- Hollow earth / prehistoric pocket still works if rules of isolation stay consistent
