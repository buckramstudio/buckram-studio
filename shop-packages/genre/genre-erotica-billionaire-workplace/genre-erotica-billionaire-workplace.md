# Billionaire / workplace-power erotica

Engine: **institutional or wealth asymmetry as erotic fuel** — boss/employee, owner/talent, rich/poor logistics — among **consenting adults**. The job or money must change who can say no, who is watched, and what exposure costs.

## Light

Job: make money/title/institution change refusal power — asymmetry is the heat source, not shopping montages.

Contract: secrecy/exposure stakes; competence on both sides; payoff matches erotica vs erotic-romance shelf.

Must-not-break:
- Asymmetry legible — money, title, visa, housing, fame
- Less-powerful adult can refuse without losing shelter/job/status — or honest dark framing
- Secrecy / exposure stakes (gossip, HR, board, press)
- Competence fantasy — powerful partner good at *something*; junior partner not only decorative
- Strip test: steamy CR billionaire that stands without sex-spine ≠ erotica
- Adults only; no illegal content guidance

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- **Billionaire** lane: fantasy capital — private jets, NDAs, reputation machines — often erotic romance with HEA; erotica versions emphasize sexual awakening under luxury control aesthetics
- **Workplace** lane: office, hospital, studio, kitchen, academia (adult students) — HR reality vs fantasy contract
- Stacks with kink when D/s is formalized; otherwise power is informal but structural

**Reader promises**
1. Asymmetry is legible
2. Secrecy / exposure stakes
3. Competence fantasy
4. Payoff matches shelf — erotic romance owes HEA that renegotiates power; erotica may end at claimed desire

**Consent / power craft**
- NDAs and contracts as kink theater vs real silencing — know which story you mean
- Public/private logistics + discovery risk
- Avoid “teaching/training” without consent context 

**Structures that fit**
- Forbidden office → escalation → choice between job and desire
- Fake dating / marriage of convenience for wealth optics → sexual real
- Rival companies / hostile takeover as erotic enemies-to-lovers
- Assistant/principal travel pressure-cooker

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Billionaire as personality | Specific empire + specific wound |
| HR-blind fantasy sold as realism | Pick fantasy contract; signal it |
| Coercion as cute | Show exit power or dark-lane honesty |
| Only shopping & jets | Sex-spine / relationship change beats |
| Interchangeable “boss” | Voice + competence + particular desire |

## Hard

**Fantasy contract vs realism**
- Readers accept luxury fantasy if the *power-to-refuse* problem is still dramatized — or you must retag toward dark when exit is impossible
- Formal D/s negotiation ≠ informal boss power — don’t borrow SSC vocabulary as cover for unexamined coercion

**When to break “HEA renegotiates power”**
- Erotica may refuse corporate marriage; still show what desire claimed or cost
- If optimistic couple ending + love primary → erotic romance path
