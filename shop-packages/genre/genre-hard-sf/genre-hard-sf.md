# Hard science fiction

Engine: **science/engineering constraint as plot pressure** — known or carefully extrapolated science is the backbone; problems solvable (or tragically insoluble) *within* those rules; enabling miracles minimized or paid for.

## Light

Job: make physical/biological constraint create the conflict — not lecture wallpaper.

Contract: procedural rigor and scientific spirit; readers will audit; climax must not silent-break stated rules.

Must-not-break:
- State or strongly imply rules early enough to play fair
- Science creates conflict (resource, vacuum, latency, biology)
- Prefer depth of one system over kitchen-sink tech
- Character want must survive the rigor — puzzle alone ≠ novel
- Honest handwave or none — no free miracle at climax
- Aftermath of rigor: scar, debt, or new constraint

## Normal

**Genre shape**
- Allen Steele (SFE): established or carefully extrapolated science as backbone
- Respect natural explanations over supernatural/transcendental ones
- Continuum, not binary: hardness = how far before enabling devices dominate
- Later real-world disproof needn’t strip the label if reasoning was honest to its era

**Hard vs soft vs opera**

| | Hard SF | Soft SF | Space opera |
|--|---------|---------|-------------|
| Emphasis | Tech/science consequences | People, culture, soft sciences | Adventure, empire, spectacle |
| Enabling tech | Minimized or rigorously paid for | May handwave physics | Often assumed (FTL etc.) |
| Failure mode | Lecture without pulse | Costume novelty | Horse opera with rayguns |

**Obligatory pressures**
- Constraint reveal → working the problem (hypothesis → test → iterate) → cost of enabling device → human under physics → honest handwave or none → aftermath of rigor
- “Cold equations” tradition: constraint forces moral/logistical cruelty without cartoon villainy

**Working checklist**
- One central scientific/engineering novum (or tightly linked cluster)
- Extrapolate Tuesday: jobs, law, love, lunch
- Reveal through character problem-solving (*The Martian* pattern)
- Research feeds: NASA, arXiv, consults — 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Auctorial lecture / numbing dump | Scene problem-solving |
| Silent rule-break at climax | Pay cost or foreshadow limit |
| Hard furniture, soft people | Cast wants under orbits |
| Confirmation-bias comfort puzzles | Allow insoluble or tragic constraints |
| Kitchen-sink tech | Deepen one system |

## Hard

**Hybrids & continuum edges**
- Opera scale + rigor (Reynolds, Liu, *The Expanse*) is valid hard-adjacent
- Soft sociological classics can still be “hard” about anthropology/linguistics
- SFE notes political branding mistaken for hardness — irrelevant to craft rigor

**Geek scrutiny as feature**
- Readers audit (Mesklin equator; Ringworld soil slide) — treat as design pressure, not insult
- Enabling classics that smuggle FTL/ESP still claim scientific spirit — know where you sit on the continuum

**When to break pure hardness**
- One paid enabling device OK if consequences are worked and climax doesn’t invent a second free miracle
- If spectacle/empire leads and physics is assumed → label honestly
