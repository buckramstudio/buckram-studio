# Grimdark

Engine: agency under rotten systems — morally gray people forced to choose in a world where right action is costly, compromised, or seemingly futile; darkness with argument, not wallpaper.

## Light

Job: make bleak systems press real choices — cynicism needs a point; characters choose under rot.

Contract: antiheroes/gray leads with a tether; institutional cruelty; consequence that sticks; hope-flicker scarce therefore precious.

Must-not-break:
- Compromise scene: lesser evil / survival over purity — both options cost
- System reveals itself as procedure, not one cartoon tyrant
- Violence with aftermath (injury, trauma, political fallout, moral stain)
- Character investment *more* important, not less
- World needs texture beyond mud: culture, art, beauty, humor
- Sexual violence as cheap grit is a known genre sin — avoid as wallpaper

## Normal

**Definitions (useful cluster)**

Term from Warhammer 40K tagline. Critical notes that stick:
- **Roberts:** “nobody honourable / Might is Right”; cynicism about *our* world as much as “realistic” past
- **Valentine:** psychology of sword-heroes + dark realism of kingdom politics
- **Shurin:** grim tone + realism + **agency** — characters choose, aren’t predestined to beat the Dark Lord
- **Bourke (critique):** darkness-for-darkness / nihilism that absolves moral responsibility — useful as *failure mode*
- Contrasts: hopepunk, noblebright, sweetweird; anti-Tolkien reaction common while still owing Tolkien’s architecture

**Conventions**

- Tone: bleak, violent, amoral systems — not only “gritty aesthetic”
- Protagonists: antiheroes / gray — still need tether (code, grudge, love, humor)
- War and politics as engines; wounds stick, oaths break, victories sour
- Magic (if any) often costly, sinister, unequally weaponized — limits still rule
- Loss transforms cast: ideals, people, nations

**Obligatory pressures**

- Compromise scene; system-as-procedure; virtue tax (decent act hard, rarely rewarded)
- False victory / pyrrhic beat; humor counterweight; tether threatened

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Cruelty mistaken for depth | Argument + tether + consequence |
| Total flatness (no hope/humor/beauty) | Contrast; prevent numbness |
| Nihilism absolving everyone | Restore moral responsibility (Bourke trap) |
| Bigger swords, bloodier blood | Emotional nuance over gore inflation |
| Shock deaths, no ripple | Causal/thematic aftermath |

## Hard

**Agency vs predestination**

- Shurin’s “fantasy Protestantism”: characters choose under rotten odds — if prophecy/Dark Lord defeat is locked, you may have epic with grit paint, not grimdark
- Anti-Tolkien reaction still borrows epic architecture — know what you’re dismantling

**When to break “no hope”**

- Scarce hope-flicker is structural (makes darkness weigh); extinguishing all light → reader numbness, not mastery
- Hopepunk/noblebright are deliberate counters — hybridize only with honest comps
- Sexual violence: if diegetic and necessary to the argument, treat as human trauma with cost — never spice
