# Erotic romance

Engine: **romance HEA/HFN + sex as load-bearing growth** — remove the sexual journey and the love story collapses; keep the optimistic couple ending and you are still *romance*, not erotica.

## Light

Job: grow the couple through sexual intimacy — and still land optimistic couple ending.

Contract: HEA/HFN required; sex cannot be stripped without damaging the love story; consent and consequence are story tissue.

Must-not-break:
- Optimistic couple ending mandatory (romance-protective readership)
- Strip test: if story survives without sex → spicy romance, not erotic romance
- Sex progresses information, power, or emotional risk — not quota friction
- Heat volume ≠ erotic romance; **structural job of sex** does
- Pairing/trope does not auto-classify the book
- Don’t bait romance readers into no-landing erotica

## Normal

**Boundary table**

| Form | Core question | Where arc develops | HEA/HFN |
|------|---------------|--------------------|---------|
| **Steamy / spicy romance** | Will they build lasting romantic love? | Mostly *outside* sex scenes; sex supports | Required |
| **Erotic romance** | Same + sexual intimacy as growth engine | Majority of emotional/relationship arc *in* sexual journey | Required |
| **Erotica** | Who am I / what do I desire sexually? | Sexual self-discovery *is* the spine | Optional |

RWA-style framing: strong, often explicit sexual interaction is inherent to love story, character growth, and relationship development.

**Reader promises**
- Explicit language more straightforward than euphemism-heavy sensual romance; still needs character + nameable plot without the acts alone
- Consent craft: enthusiastic, ongoing; power-imbalance/CNC frames need diegetic negotiation
- Aftercare / consequence when intensity is the point
- Retail miscategorization common — define by structure, not store shelf alone

**Structures that fit**
- Romance beat sheet  **plus** sex as the room where Lies crack
- Initiation/awakening inside a becoming-couple frame
- D/s or kink negotiation as the relationship’s trust ladder toward HEA/HFN
- Novella pressure-cooker still needs Black Moment *for the couple*, not only for the dynamic

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Spicy CR mislabeled erotic romance | Strip test |
| Erotic romance without HEA | That’s erotica — recategorize |
| Interchangeable partners | Specific desire + voice |
| Quota sex / porno-motion | Purpose test + |
| Romance readers baited into no-landing erotica | Honest blurbs; structure match |
| Taboo acts without craft cost | Consequence; know publisher red lines |

## Hard

**Structural edges**
- Majority-of-arc-in-sex is a craft test, not a page-count fetish — a few load-bearing scenes can qualify if the relationship cannot advance without them
- Kink erotic romance vs dark romance: negotiated trust ladder  vs coercion aesthetics with HEA  — different reader contracts
- NA age-band + erotic romance heat: adult age is part of consent/shelf honesty 

**When to break high-explicit default**
- Closed-door erotic romance is rare but possible if sexual *journey* (negotiation, first intimacy risk, aftermath) still carries growth — heat volume is secondary to structural job
