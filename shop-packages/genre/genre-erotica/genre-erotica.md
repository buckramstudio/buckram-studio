# Erotica (genre engine)

Sexual journey as the story’s core — not “romance with more sex.” Style bans / anatomy realism → . Scene function, heat contracts, consent → . Romance HEA engine → . Erotic romance → . Subgenres → .

## Light

Job: make sexual self-discovery / desire negotiation the spine — strip sex and the novel collapses.

Contract: arousal + meaning; HEA/HFN optional; plot is sexual in nature (power, exposure, libido crisis, claimed desire).

Must-not-break:
- Strip test: remove sex/sexual-journey scenes → remaining text should not still be a complete novel
- Enthusiastic, ongoing, specific consent (modern digital-first expectation)
- Each major sexual beat changes information, power, or emotional risk
- Aftercare / consequence is story tissue after intensity
- Pairing/trope does **not** auto-make a book erotica
- Don’t bait romance readers into a no-landing sexual journey

## Normal

**Boundary table**

| Form | Core question | Sex’s job | HEA/HFN |
|------|---------------|-----------|---------|
| **Romance** (any heat) | Will these people build lasting romantic love? | Supports relationship arc | **Required** |
| **Steamy / spicy romance** | Same as romance | Present, purposeful; arc not *about* sexual becoming | **Required** |
| **Erotic romance** | Romance + sexual intimacy as growth engine | Cannot strip sex without collapsing story | **Required** |
| **Erotica** | Who am I / what do I desire / what does this encounter change sexually? | Sexual component *is* the plot spine | **Optional** |
| **Porn (craft contrast)** | Setup → acts | Titillation only; character unchanged | N/A |

Literary/cultural “erotic literature” is broader than commercial novel erotica — this note targets novel-length commercial erotica. Define by **structure**, not store shelf alone.

**Craft promises**
- Arousal + meaning: body heat is the purchase point; character change/theme/discovery still required
- Sexual self-discovery / negotiation of desire as slice-of-life with sex as the lens
- GMC and conflict still exist — sexual in nature
- Romance may be subplot; permanence optional — market honestly

**Consent as craft**
- Verbal or clearly embodied; silence ≠ yes
- Power-imbalance and CNC/noncon *fantasy* frames need diegetic negotiation or clear fantasy contract
- Consent failures meant as harm should read as harm (consequence), not spicy garnish
- Dubcon as unexamined default is a hangover — know the promise and cost

**Common structures**
1. Initiation / awakening — novice crosses a threshold; climax = claimed or refused false desire
2. Erotic relationship development — D/s or dynamic built scene-by-scene
3. Serial encounter / constellation — unity via theme or character change
4. Pressure-cooker novella — few locations, escalating encounters
5. Erotic romance shape — only if you truly want HEA/HFN 

**Working checklist**
- Alternate heat with breath (aftermath, logistics, jealousy, work, fear)
- State sexual Want and Need in one sentence each
- Midpoint changes what they will risk or admit
- Final image desire-integrated — not only orgasm count

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Sex interchangeable; any partner would do | Specific desire + voice in body |
| Romance readers angry at missing HEA | Recategorize or earn HFN/HEA |
| Erotica readers bored by non-heat plot | Sexualize the spine |
| Euphemism fog / porno-motion | |
| Quota sex | Purpose test  |
| Dark acts without consequence | Cost on trust/safety/self/plot |

## Hard

**Retail vs structure**
- Advertising/retail often punishes the erotica label; romance dumped into “erotica” filters is common — craft honesty still beats bait-and-switch reviews
- Literary erotic tradition (arouse + satire/critique) may not follow commercial novella pacing — don’t force digital-first beats onto literary projects without intent

**Edge dials**
- Dark erotica: darkness as experiential core; HEA not required → 
- Negotiated kink trust ladder → 
- If optimistic couple ending is mandatory and sex load-bearing → 

**When to break “sex every act advances” purity**
- Breath/aftermath scenes that change power or fear still count as spine tissue
- Serial/constellation form may delay “one forever partner” — unity must still be thematic or character-based
