# Romance (genre engine)

Market promise: a central love story + emotionally satisfying optimistic ending. Couple beats → . Heat scene craft → . Erotica (distinct) → . Erotic romance → .

## Light

Job: deliver a central love story whose climax and landing answer “will these people make it?” with HEA or HFN.

Contract: readers buy knowing the couple will land; suspense is *how*, not *whether*. Love stories that end in death/breakup/better-alone exit *category* romance.

Must-not-break:
- Central love story — conflict/climax tied to the relationship
- Emotionally satisfying optimistic ending (HEA or HFN)
- Black Moment threatens the couple hard enough that landing feels earned
- Heat lane named and kept cover-to-climax
- Yearning is the engine; tension ≠ only external fight
- Comp titles match subgenre *and* heat

## Normal

**Core contract**

RWA-style mass-market baseline:

1. Central love story — plot revolves around people developing romantic love
2. HEA (durable commitment signal) or HFN (solid romantic landing now; future not fully locked)

Skeleton: Meet → Attraction → Obstacles → Midpoint shift → Black Moment → Grand Gesture → HEA/HFN — detail in .

**Heat / spice scale**

No single industry standard — still must **name a lane**. Mis-selling heat tanks reviews.

| Lane | On-page intimacy |
|------|------------------|
| Sweet / clean / wholesome | Little/no arousal; kissing limited or absent |
| Warm | Kissing; sex alluded |
| Sensual | Lust + titillation; softer/euphemistic sex |
| Steamy / spicy | Explicit on-page; desire foregrounded |
| Erotic romance | Explicit + sexual journey drives arc — still HEA/HFN → |

- When sex can be stripped and story stands → steamy/spicy romance
- When stripping sex collapses arc *and* HEA/HFN required → erotic romance
- Sex spine + couple ending optional →

- Dual/alternating POV common — distinct voices if used
- Grand gesture earned by prior growth
- Tropes are expectation tools, not plot substitutes 

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Ambiguous breakup sold as romance | Earn HEA/HFN or leave the shelf |
| Gesture without growth | Build Black Moment cost first |
| Heat mismatch cover↔interior | One lane end-to-end |
| Trope checklist, no conflict | Specific Want/Need + obstacles |

## Hard

**HEA vs HFN dial**

- HEA: marriage/forever-intent/settled partnership signal
- HFN: common in series, YA/NA age, or when full forever would feel false
- Neither is ambiguous breakup; both optimistic about *this* relationship

**Romance vs women’s fiction / erotica**

- Midpoint/climax answer “will they end up together?” → romance; “will she become a fuller self?” → 
- Escapism convention: phone/baby/rent rarely derail the emotional through-line the way realism-first fiction allows
- Category (~50–55k line identity) vs single-title (~90–110k+ subplot room)

**When to break optimism**
- Tragedy/love-story-without-landing is valid literature — do not sell as category romance
- Dark romance still usually owes HEA/HFN unless positioning states otherwise
