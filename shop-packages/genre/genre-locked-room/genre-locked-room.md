# Locked-room / impossible crime

Engine: the crime appears physically impossible; the spectacle is logic. Reader wants the “how” as much as the “who.”

## Light

Job: stage a prima facie impossible crime whose rational solution restores physics — fair play before the reveal.

Contract: howdunit load-bearing; no supernatural cheat unless openly supernatural mystery (different shelf).

Must-not-break:
- Every fact the solution needs is available before the reveal
- State constraints early: locks, keys, sightlines, time window
- Know the full physical sequence before drafting Act II
- Prefer solutions that feel simple in retrospect — avoid seventeen-coincidence Rube Goldberg
- Culprit is a character the reader has spent time with
- Atmosphere may borrow gothic fog; answer stays material under Golden Age rules

## Normal

**Genre contract**

- Closed circle or witness-constrained stage common
- Fair play: Knox / Van Dine spirit
- Howdunit brand promise; whodunit still matters

**Classic solution families (Carr / Adey lineage)**

1. Not actually locked — lock/trick, mistaken sealing
2. Victim already dying/dead before the “seal”
3. Suicide / accident staged as murder
4. Hidden entrance — foreshadowed (Knox: at most one secret passage, prepared)
5. Outside mechanism — string, ice, timing device, remote trigger
6. Killer never left — hidden, disguised, or misidentified among discoverers
7. Timing / testimony illusion — who entered/left when is the lie

Modern: digital “locked room,” delayed trap, accomplice seals after, virtual alibi via tech.

**Fair-play design checklist**

- Plant mechanism clues in neutral detail — never dump at reveal only
- Suspects: Motive / Means / Opportunity + personal secret
- Eliminate false solutions on-page; every red herring gets an explanation

**Atmosphere × puzzle**

Carr combo: ingenious plot + gothic atmospheric misdirection while answer stays material.

**Beat sketch**
1. Place rules + closed-circle tensions → 2. Impossible crime → 3. Map/timesheet/physical audit → 4. False solutions → 5. Midpoint new impossibility or alibi collapse → 6. Re-read Act I → 7. Gathering/demonstration → 8. Reveal reclassifies known facts

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Supernatural cheat under fair-play promise | Material solution or retag |
| Solution needs unseen facts | Plant earlier |
| Rube Goldberg coincidences | Simplify mechanism |
| Culprit is a stranger | Make culprit known character |
| Atmosphere without puzzle payoff | Pay down impossibility with logic |

## Hard

**Knox secret-passage discipline**

- At most one secret passage, and it must be prepared — mastery is foreshadow without spotlight
- Digital locked rooms inherit the same fairness: camera/metadata constraints stated before the twist

**When to break Golden Age purity**
- Hybrid with PI/noir cost  may soften fair-play for atmosphere — then don’t market as pure howdunit puzzle
- Openly supernatural impossible crime is a different shelf — say so in comps
