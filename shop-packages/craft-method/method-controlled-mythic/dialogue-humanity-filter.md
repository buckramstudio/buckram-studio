# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Controlled mythic

## Register boundaries

- Formal where role/ritual requires; everyday speech direct human.
- Duty via claims/refusals/promises/naming/silence not destiny speeches.
- Ceremonial orderly unless conflict breaks for reason.
- Stance/witness/object/consequence.
- Remove pseudo-archaic and unsupported abstractions.

## Subtext gap

- Obligation and communal weight via naming, refusal, silence, and material consequence.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Ceremonial orderly unless conflict breaks for reason.
- Role-bound formal register until breach is narratively earned.

## Narration around speech

- Dialogue human outside role-bound formal.
- Do not force destiny speeches or pseudo-archaic diction on every speaker.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Is dialogue formal only where role/ritual requires?
2. Does everyday speech stay direct and human?
3. Is duty conveyed via claims/refusals/promises/naming/silence not destiny speeches?
4. Does ceremonial speech stay orderly unless conflict breaks for reason?
5. Are pseudo-archaic diction and unsupported abstractions removed from dialogue?
