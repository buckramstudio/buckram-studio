# Series Style Bible — Controlled mythic

## Method promise

Create scale and timeless weight through elemental nouns, formal cadence, archetypal pressure, and selective figurative language held inside strict budgets.

## Narrator priority

Name the material world clearly, enlarge only what carries moral or communal weight, and return every abstraction to action, object, body, or consequence.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third/first/restrained omniscient; no shifts within scene.
- Specificity high landscape/body/labor/ritual/material consequence.
- Grave-warm; emotion via duty/loss/vow/refusal/relation.
- Figurative: one governing image family/scene; max one simile/metaphor per paragraph; never mix image domains in one sentence.
- Abstract must be grounded by concrete action/object same paragraph.
- Tone: Warm 50/Clinical 50; Confident 90/Hedged 10; Serious 95/Playful 5.
- Dialogue formal where role/ritual requires; everyday speech direct human.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Labor/travel | Concrete rhythmic | Grandiosity |
| Ritual/decision | Formal spare | Pseudo-archaic clutter |
| Landscape | Relational material | Scenic catalog |
| Memory/inheritance | Selective consequential | Lore dump |
| Violence/loss | Lucid grave | Aestheticized suffering |

## Scene movement

- Open: material action or elemental condition + active human obligation; not universal declaration.
- Rhythm: concrete action→widened relation/inheritance→return present consequence.
- Close: concrete image/vow/cost/crossing/separation/changed relation; may resonate not explain.

## Anti-patterns

- Casual banter, dense technical procedure, frequent comedy, decorative luxuriance.
- Do not use pseudo-archaic pronouns/verb endings/indiscriminate inversions.
- Do not stack epithets/kennings/metaphors/symbolic nouns.
- Do not personify every natural element.
- Do not make unsupported universal claims.
- Do not convert every action into fate/destiny/legend/eternity.
- Do not obscure agency with passive grandeur.
- Do not use purple color/jewel/perfume/celestial catalogs as elevation shortcut.
- Scale earned by consequence; most action sentences plain.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening joins concrete action/condition to human obligation?
2. Agency/physical sequence clear?
3. Figurative one image family?
4. No paragraph exceeds figurative budget?
5. Every abstraction grounded same paragraph?
6. Formal readable no pseudo-archaic?
7. Plain action outnumber elevated declarations?
8. Dialogue human outside role-bound formal?
9. Suffering concrete consequential not aestheticized?
10. Close image/consequence without explanation?
