# Forbidden Index — Controlled mythic

## Diction bans

- Do not use pseudo-archaic pronouns/verb endings/indiscriminate inversions.
- Do not stack epithets/kennings/metaphors/symbolic nouns.
- Do not personify every natural element.
- Do not make unsupported universal claims.
- Do not use purple color/jewel/perfume/celestial catalogs as elevation shortcut.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Never use ellipses/exclamation in narration.
- Never mix image domains in one sentence.
- Never exceed max one simile/metaphor per paragraph.
- Em dashes rare only.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not convert every action into fate/destiny/legend/eternity.
- Do not obscure agency with passive grandeur.
- Never aestheticize suffering.

## Structural bans

- Do not close with explanation when resonance suffices.
- Never use universal declaration as opening.
- Abstract must be grounded by concrete action/object same paragraph.

## Anti-caricature

- Scale earned by consequence.
- Most action sentences plain.
- Formal widening only for thresholds/vows/communal stakes/irreversible choices/aftermath.
