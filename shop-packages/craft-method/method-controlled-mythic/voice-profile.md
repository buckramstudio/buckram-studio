# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Elevated** — precise formal diction without ornamental synonym chains.
2. **Elemental** — land/weather/body/work/kinship/debt/boundary/consequence when relevant.
3. **Measured** — balanced cadence deliberate syntactic variation.
4. **Grave** — choice/cost seriously without constant solemnity markers.
5. **Lucid** — agency and physical sequence clear under figurative pressure.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Medium to long; short declarative verdicts sparingly.

**Paragraph length:** 2–5.

**Rhythm:** Concrete action→widened relation/inheritance→return present consequence.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Commas/periods/occasional semicolon; em dashes rare; ellipses/exclamation forbidden in narration. Figurative: one governing image family/scene; max one simile/metaphor per paragraph; never mix image domains in one sentence. Abstract must be grounded by concrete action/object same paragraph.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Formal readable; concrete Anglo-Saxon-root preferred for action; longer abstract only when necessary.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third/first/restrained omniscient; no shifts within scene.

**Specificity:** High landscape/body/labor/ritual/material consequence.

**Emotional temperature:** Grave-warm; emotion via duty/loss/vow/refusal/relation.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 50 / Clinical 50.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 90 / Hedged 10.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 95 / Playful 5.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Material action or elemental condition + active human obligation; not universal declaration.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Concrete image/vow/cost/crossing/separation/changed relation; may resonate not explain.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Moral scale, ritual weight, landscape relation, compressed thematic resonance.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Casual banter, dense technical procedure, frequent comedy, decorative luxuriance.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not use pseudo-archaic pronouns/verb endings/indiscriminate inversions.
- Do not stack epithets/kennings/metaphors/symbolic nouns.
- Do not personify every natural element.
- Do not make unsupported universal claims.
- Do not convert every action into fate/destiny/legend/eternity.
- Do not obscure agency with passive grandeur.
- Do not use purple color/jewel/perfume/celestial catalogs as elevation shortcut.
