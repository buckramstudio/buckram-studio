# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Sensory** — exact source/location/material/intensity/bodily contact not generic beauty.
2. **Lyrical** — pattern via cadence/recurrence/relation not ornamental density.
3. **Intimate** — sensory selection tied to viewpoint attention and stakes.
4. **Fluid** — vary sentence length by perception/action; preserve grammar.
5. **Disciplined** — limit image families; remove abstract significance language.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Mixed; medium baseline; longer connected perception; short rupture/recognition.

**Paragraph length:** 1–5 around one sensory center or action.

**Rhythm:** Present sensory contact→association/memory pressure→changed present action.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Commas/periods; dashes max 1/paragraph for perceptual turn; semicolons rare; ellipses/exclamation forbidden. Fragments max 2/1000 words for rupture/sensory isolation; never consecutive paragraphs. Image: one primary family/scene; ≤2 active sensory channels/paragraph unless overload is explicit event.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Material sensory precise active; abstracts need concrete grounding.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third or first.

**Specificity:** Very high texture/sound source/smell source/temp/light/taste/body contact.

**Emotional temperature:** Warm to intense but not named ahead of evidence.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 70 / Clinical 30.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 75 / Hedged 25.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 90 / Playful 10.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Active sensory contact + present task/body/relationship; not free-floating scenic.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Changed image/sensation/gesture/material condition; do not decode recurrence.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Embodied memory, emotional layering, precise atmosphere, image continuity.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Tactical exposition, rapid multi-party dialogue, procedural clarity, prolonged sparse-nav action.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not use beauty/haunting/ethereal/timeless/somehow/ineffable/significance as description substitutes.
- Do not stack multiple metaphors or sensory channels in one sentence.
- Do not personify setting by default.
- Do not attach symbolic explanation to recurring image.
- Do not use fragments as decorative emphasis.
- Do not let memory detach from present trigger/consequence.
- Do not obscure who acts, where bodies are, or what physically changes.
