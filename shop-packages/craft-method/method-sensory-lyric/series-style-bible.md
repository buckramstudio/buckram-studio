# Series Style Bible — Sensory lyric

## Method promise

Build emotional pressure through precise sensory selection, recurring material images, and controlled syntax variation while keeping agency, setting, and meaning legible.

## Narrator priority

Select the sensory detail the viewpoint cannot ignore, connect it to present action or memory, and let recurrence alter meaning without explaining symbolism.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third or first.
- Specificity very high texture/sound source/smell source/temp/light/taste/body contact.
- Warm to intense but not named ahead of evidence.
- Image: one primary family/scene; ≤2 active sensory channels/paragraph unless overload is explicit event.
- Fragments max 2/1000 words for rupture/sensory isolation; never consecutive paragraphs.
- Tone: Warm 70/Clinical 30; Confident 75/Hedged 25; Serious 90/Playful 10.
- Dialogue may stay plain; do not force lyrical imagery into every speaker; narration may select one sensory consequence of exchange.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Present contact | Exact source+body relation | Generic sensory adjective |
| Association | Brief triggered | Untethered reverie |
| Memory | Selective sensory correspondence | Full flashback by drift |
| Intensity | Syntax lengthen/rupture within budget | Fog |
| Return | Changed present action/image | Significance statement |

## Scene movement

- Open: active sensory contact + present task/body/relationship; not free-floating scenic.
- Rhythm: present sensory contact→association/memory pressure→changed present action.
- Close: changed image/sensation/gesture/material condition; do not decode recurrence.

## Anti-patterns

- Tactical exposition, rapid multi-party dialogue, procedural clarity, prolonged sparse-nav action.
- Do not use beauty/haunting/ethereal/timeless/somehow/ineffable/significance as description substitutes.
- Do not stack multiple metaphors or sensory channels in one sentence.
- Do not personify setting by default.
- Do not attach symbolic explanation to recurring image.
- Do not use fragments as decorative emphasis.
- Do not let memory detach from present trigger/consequence.
- Do not obscure who acts, where bodies are, or what physically changes.
- Lyric ≠ maximal description.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening sensory exact source+present function?
2. Each paragraph one sensory center or action?
3. No paragraph exceeds sensory-channel budget without explicit overload?
4. One primary image family?
5. Associations/memories have present triggers?
6. Every sensory detail/recurrence changes orientation/pressure/emotion/action?
7. Fragments/dashes within caps?
8. Abstract meaning grounded never substitutes description?
9. Agency/spatial logic clear?
10. Close returns changed material/sensation without decoding?
