# Forbidden Index — Sensory lyric

## Diction bans

- Do not use beauty/haunting/ethereal/timeless/somehow/ineffable/significance as description substitutes.
- Do not stack multiple metaphors or sensory channels in one sentence.
- Do not personify setting by default.
- Do not attach symbolic explanation to recurring image.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Never use ellipses/exclamation.
- Dashes max 1/paragraph for perceptual turn only.
- Never exceed fragments max 2/1000 words for rupture/sensory isolation.
- Never use fragments in consecutive paragraphs.
- Semicolons rare only.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not let memory detach from present trigger/consequence.
- Do not close by decoding recurrence.
- Abstract meaning grounded never substitutes description.

## Structural bans

- Do not obscure who acts, where bodies are, or what physically changes.
- Never open with free-floating scenic description.
- Never exceed ≤2 active sensory channels/paragraph unless overload is explicit event.

## Anti-caricature

- Lyric ≠ maximal description.
- Every sensory detail must orient, pressure memory, alter emotion, or change action.
- Delete decorative details that do none.
