# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Observant** — names functional objects, positions, routines, and visible behavior before inference.
2. **Wry** — permits one restrained judgment about inconvenience, vanity, or social mismatch; never performs for a laugh.
3. **Composed** — reports the anomaly without typographic or rhetorical alarm.
4. **Close** — filters selection through the viewpoint character's immediate concern and available knowledge.
5. **Wonder-struck** — allows altered attention and curiosity after the anomaly without declaring awe.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Mixed, medium declarative baseline; longer for orientation, shorter for recognition.

**Paragraph length:** One to four; anomaly gets own sentence or terminal clause, not isolated fragment by default.

**Rhythm:** Stable mundane sequence, one deviation, recalibration through action/attention.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Conventional; em dashes sparse; exclamation forbidden in narration; italics may not manufacture surprise. One dominant anomaly per beat.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Familiar concrete socially attentive; function/condition over decorative appearance.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third default; brief socially specific omniscient OK.

**Specificity:** High for logistics and wrong detail; medium elsewhere.

**Emotional temperature:** Warm-neutral before turn, alert controlled after.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 60 / Clinical 40.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 80 / Hedged 20.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 65 / Playful 35.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Task/routine/inconvenience/social expectation in motion; ≥2 ordinary anchors before anomaly.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Changed practical action, withheld social response, or anomaly persistence; no symbolic explanation.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Strangeness credible via ordinary discipline.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Chaos, anomaly stacking, abstract metaphysics, spectacle.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not label strange/impossible/magical/ominous/significant before evidence.
- Do not stack >1 new anomaly per beat.
- Do not use rhetorical questions to amaze.
- Do not turn wryness into quips/punchlines/collusion.
- Do not replace concrete observation with atmosphere words.
- Do not explain anomaly meaning in closing line.
