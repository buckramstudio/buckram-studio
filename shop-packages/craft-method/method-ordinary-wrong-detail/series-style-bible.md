# Series Style Bible — Ordinary to wrong detail

## Method promise

Ground the reader in an ordinary, legible situation, then alter the scene's meaning with one concrete anomaly reported in the same calm register as everything around it.

## Narrator priority

Orientation first, anomaly second, reaction third. The narrator notices practical surfaces and social behavior before interpreting significance.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third default; brief socially specific omniscient OK.
- Function/condition over decorative appearance.
- Specificity high for logistics and wrong detail; medium elsewhere.
- Warm-neutral before turn, alert controlled after.
- One dominant anomaly per beat.
- Tone: Warm 60/Clinical 40; Confident 80/Hedged 20; Serious 65/Playful 35.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Routine/orientation | Practical lightly social | Portent |
| First anomaly | Exact unamplified | Shock vocab |
| Recognition | Shortened attention/changed behavior | Explanatory awe |
| Escalation | Consequences of original anomaly | Unrelated marvels |
| Aftermath | Ordinary action under altered assumptions | Thesis |

## Scene movement

- Open: task/routine/inconvenience/social expectation in motion; ≥2 ordinary anchors before anomaly.
- Rhythm: stable mundane sequence, one deviation, recalibration through action/attention.
- Close: changed practical action, withheld social response, or anomaly persistence; no symbolic explanation.

## Anti-patterns

- Chaos, anomaly stacking, abstract metaphysics, spectacle.
- Do not label strange/impossible/magical/ominous/significant before evidence.
- Do not stack >1 new anomaly per beat.
- Do not use rhetorical questions to amaze.
- Do not turn wryness into quips/punchlines/collusion.
- Do not replace concrete observation with atmosphere words.
- Do not explain anomaly meaning in closing line.
- Not constant whimsical oddity.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening concrete routine before anomaly?
2. ≥2 mundane anchors before wrong detail?
3. First anomaly concrete precisely located?
4. Narration does not announce strange/meaningful?
5. Response is action/attention/social adjustment not exposition?
6. No competing unrelated anomalies?
7. Wryness understated no reader address?
8. No exclamation/typographic surprise?
9. Close preserves anomaly via consequence/image?
10. Close no thesis/symbolism?
