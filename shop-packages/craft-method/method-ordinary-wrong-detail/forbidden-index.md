# Forbidden Index — Ordinary to wrong detail

## Diction bans

- Do not label strange/impossible/magical/ominous/significant before evidence.
- Do not replace concrete observation with atmosphere words.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Do not stack >1 new anomaly per beat.
- Do not use rhetorical questions to amaze.
- Never use exclamation in narration.
- Never use italics to manufacture surprise.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not turn wryness into quips/punchlines/collusion.
- Never perform wryness for a laugh.
- Do not declare awe after the anomaly.

## Structural bans

- Do not explain anomaly meaning in closing line.
- Never close with symbolic explanation.
- Never isolate the anomaly as a fragment by default.

## Anti-caricature

- Not constant whimsical oddity.
- ≥half selected detail must be ordinary/functional.
- Wry capped at one brief turn per paragraph, usually rarer.
