# Series Style Bible — Talk-forward

## Method promise

Let dialogue perform most of the scene's action while lean narration tracks leverage, behavior, silence, and physical consequence.

## Narrator priority

Keep speakers oriented, make every exchange change knowledge or leverage, and preserve the gap between literal wording and actual intent.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third or first; cannot reveal other speaker intent as fact.
- Specificity high conversational objective/physical leverage/object transfer/distance/silence.
- Emotion from turn sequence not narrator labels.
- Dialogue paragraphs by speaker; narration blocks rarely >3 sentences during exchange.
- Em dashes only actual interruption; ellipses only genuine trailing off, max 2/scene.
- Interior max one concise response between turns unless deliberate pause.
- Tone: Warm 50/Clinical 50; Confident 80/Hedged 20; Serious 70/Playful 30.
- Orientation within first 3 turns.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Bid/request | Goal-bearing specific | Throat-clearing |
| Evasion | Responsive indirect | Random subject change |
| Pressure | Shorter/controlled by tactic | Universal shouting escalation |
| Silence/action | Changes leverage | Empty beat filler |
| Disclosure | Partial costly | Complete self-analysis |

## Scene movement

- Open: line or conversational action with live objective/imbalance; orientation within first 3 turns.
- Rhythm: bid→resistance/evasion→adjustment→pressure shift→consequential action/silence.
- Close: line/refusal/silence/exit/object action/changed arrangement that shifts relationship or next move.

## Anti-patterns

- Solitary reflection, landscape immersion, procedural exposition, agree-and-explain conversations.
- Do not use greetings/names/repeated questions unless pressure/relationship work.
- Do not let characters tell each other shared info for reader.
- Do not explain subtext in narration after exchange.
- Do not give every speaker same sentence length/profanity/wit/directness.
- Do not use action beats as random gestures between every line.
- Do not overuse interruptions/ellipses/fragments/one-word replies.
- Do not let banter postpone consequence indefinitely.
- Do not use speech-tag synonyms to manufacture intensity.
- Not transcript-only.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening live conversational objective/imbalance?
2. Orientation within 3 turns?
3. Dialogue majority in active conversational scenes?
4. Every substantive turn has response function?
5. Major speakers differ by tactic/syntax not gimmick?
6. Shared info not recited for reader?
7. Narration does not decode inferable subtext?
8. Action beats change leverage/physical consequence?
9. Interruption/ellipsis within declared uses/caps?
10. Close shifts relationship/leverage/knowledge/next action?
