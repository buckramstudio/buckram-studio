# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Talk-forward

## Register boundaries

- Define participants at draft by role/goal/withheld knowledge/directness/sentence shape/tactic; no identity stereotypes.
- Speaker-owned situation-specific register; narration plain.
- Major speakers differ by goal/knowledge/syntax/directness/tactic not accent gimmicks.

## Subtext gap

- Every major exchange: literal topic ≠ pursued objective.
- Each line answer/evade/reframe/pressure/concede/refuse.
- Preserve gap between literal wording and actual intent.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Label turns by function; cut functionless and shared-info exposition.
- Em dashes only actual interruption; ellipses only genuine trailing off, max 2/scene.
- Do not overuse interruptions/ellipses/fragments/one-word replies.

## Narration around speech

- Action only when changes leverage/concealment/object/distance/silence.
- Do not use action beats as random gestures between every line.
- Do not explain subtext in narration after exchange.
- Interior max one concise response between turns unless deliberate pause.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Are participants defined by role/goal/withheld knowledge/directness/sentence shape/tactic?
2. Does every major exchange have literal topic ≠ pursued objective?
3. Does each line answer/evade/reframe/pressure/concede/refuse something specific?
4. Do action beats change leverage/concealment/object/distance/silence?
5. Are functionless turns and shared-info exposition cut?
