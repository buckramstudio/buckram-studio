# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Talk-forward** — dialogue majority of scene words when conversation is active conflict.
2. **Lean** — narration only orientation/consequential action/timing/selective interior.
3. **Subtextual** — speakers pursue goals without routinely naming motive/emotion.
4. **Responsive** — each line answers/evades/reframes/pressures/ignores something specific.
5. **Distinct** — speakers differ by goal/knowledge/syntax/directness/tactic not accent gimmicks.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Short to medium.

**Paragraph length:** Dialogue paragraphs by speaker; narration blocks rarely >3 sentences during exchange.

**Rhythm:** Bid→resistance/evasion→adjustment→pressure shift→consequential action/silence.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Standard dialogue punctuation; em dashes only actual interruption; ellipses only genuine trailing off, max 2/scene. Prefer said/asked or action; no conspicuous tag synonyms. Interior max one concise response between turns unless deliberate pause.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Speaker-owned situation-specific; narration plain.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third or first; cannot reveal other speaker intent as fact.

**Specificity:** High conversational objective/physical leverage/object transfer/distance/silence.

**Emotional temperature:** Emotion from turn sequence not narrator labels.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 50 / Clinical 50.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 80 / Hedged 20.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 70 / Playful 30.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Line or conversational action with live objective/imbalance; orientation within first 3 turns.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Line/refusal/silence/exit/object action/changed arrangement that shifts relationship or next move.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Character conflict, pace, social power, voice distinction, implied emotion.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Solitary reflection, landscape immersion, procedural exposition, agree-and-explain conversations.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not use greetings/names/repeated questions unless pressure/relationship work.
- Do not let characters tell each other shared info for reader.
- Do not explain subtext in narration after exchange.
- Do not give every speaker same sentence length/profanity/wit/directness.
- Do not use action beats as random gestures between every line.
- Do not overuse interruptions/ellipses/fragments/one-word replies.
- Do not let banter postpone consequence indefinitely.
- Do not use speech-tag synonyms to manufacture intensity.
