# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Small-place dread

## Register boundaries

- Practical under uncertainty from role/obligation/local knowledge.
- Attach lines to action/distance/object handling not emotion labels.
- Remove dialogue that only names visible danger.

## Subtext gap

- Fear displaced into tasks/routes/timing/repairs/credibility.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Interruptions only as options narrow.
- No universal fragments.

## Narration around speech

- Dialogue complete enough to sound human under pressure.
- Do not force narrator compression on every speaker.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Does dialogue displace fear into practical conflict?
2. Are lines attached to action/distance/object handling not emotion labels?
3. Are interruptions limited to narrowing options?
4. Does any line only name visible danger (remove if yes)?
5. Does speech reflect role/obligation/local knowledge under uncertainty?
