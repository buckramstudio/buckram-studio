# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Grounded** — threat via common objects, maintenance, weather, sound, distance, routine.
2. **Intimate** — body response and private association close to present perception.
3. **Patient** — escalation via linked irregularities not spectacle.
4. **Darkly dry** — brief asides expose denial/bad luck after fear, never at peak danger.
5. **Unforgiving** — consequences remain after threat recedes.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Medium, tightening as options narrow; short for irreversible recognition/physical danger.

**Paragraph length:** 2–5 survey, 1–3 under threat.

**Rhythm:** Task→irregularity→practical explanation→confirming cost→narrowed choice.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Conventional; parentheses only brief defensive aside; ellipses forbidden for suspense; exclamation rare dialogue-only. Repeat prop only when condition/meaning worsens.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Ordinary tactile spatial bodily; directional material terms.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third or first with knowledge limits.

**Specificity:** High for routes/thresholds/sounds/temps/weight/fatigue/damage.

**Emotional temperature:** Controlled surface over rising strain.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 35 / Clinical 65.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 70 / Hedged 30.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 85 / Playful 15.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Routine with modest obligation and reason to remain; establish space normal use.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Cost, compromised safe assumption, or ordinary object newly threatening; never summarize lesson.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Sustained escalation limited geography/social.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Abstract cosmic, threat with no physical trace, danger solved without residue.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not announce dread/evil/doom/coming horror.
- Do not rely on darkness/silence/cold without specific change+consequence.
- Do not use repeated heartbeat/breath/gooseflesh as primary fear.
- Do not joke at peak danger.
- Do not let aside exceed one sentence or explain theme.
- Do not close with generalized fear/humanity/place thesis.
- Do not erase injury/fatigue/broken trust/material damage.
