# Forbidden Index — Small-place dread

## Diction bans

- Do not announce dread/evil/doom/coming horror.
- Do not rely on darkness/silence/cold without specific change+consequence.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Do not use repeated heartbeat/breath/gooseflesh as primary fear.
- Never use ellipses for suspense.
- Never let aside exceed one sentence or explain theme.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not joke at peak danger.
- Do not close with generalized fear/humanity/place thesis.
- Never perform dark asides at peak danger.

## Structural bans

- Do not erase injury/fatigue/broken trust/material damage.
- Never close with a summarized lesson.
- Never reset threat without residue after temporary safety.

## Anti-caricature

- Not nonstop menace.
- Preserve neutral activity, social friction, plausible explanations.
- Cap dark asides at 2/scene.
- Body signals one distinct per escalation step.
