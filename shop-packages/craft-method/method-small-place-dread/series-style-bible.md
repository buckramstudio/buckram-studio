# Series Style Bible — Small-place dread

## Method promise

Make a familiar bounded place progressively unsafe by combining practical irregularity, bodily response, remembered local knowledge, and pressure from other people.

## Narrator priority

Track what the viewpoint character can physically verify, what the body pays, and which ordinary obligation prevents easy escape.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third or first with knowledge limits.
- Specificity high for routes/thresholds/sounds/temps/weight/fatigue/damage.
- Controlled surface over rising strain.
- Repeat prop only when condition/meaning worsens.
- Tone: Warm 35/Clinical 65; Confident 70/Hedged 30; Serious 85/Playful 15.
- Cap dark asides at 2/scene; body signals one distinct per escalation step.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Normal routine | Practical familiar | Ominous foreshadowing |
| First irregularity | Skeptical inventory | Instant certainty |
| Failed explanation | Bodily spatial compression | Melodrama |
| Direct threat | Short choice-focused | Reflective essay |
| Temporary safety | Damage altered routine | Relief reset |

## Scene movement

- Open: routine with modest obligation and reason to remain; establish space normal use.
- Rhythm: task→irregularity→practical explanation→confirming cost→narrowed choice.
- Close: cost, compromised safe assumption, or ordinary object newly threatening; never summarize lesson.

## Anti-patterns

- Abstract cosmic, threat with no physical trace, danger solved without residue.
- Do not announce dread/evil/doom/coming horror.
- Do not rely on darkness/silence/cold without specific change+consequence.
- Do not use repeated heartbeat/breath/gooseflesh as primary fear.
- Do not joke at peak danger.
- Do not let aside exceed one sentence or explain theme.
- Do not close with generalized fear/humanity/place thesis.
- Do not erase injury/fatigue/broken trust/material damage.
- Not nonstop menace.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening familiar place+obligation?
2. First irregularity mundane verifiable?
3. Practical explanation before certainty?
4. Each escalation changes cost/access/trust/choice?
5. Fear via distinct bodily/behavioral evidence?
6. Dark aside after pressure not peak?
7. Dialogue displaces fear into practical conflict?
8. Threat leaves residue?
9. No paragraph only generic dark/silence/cold?
10. Close consequence/altered object not thesis?
