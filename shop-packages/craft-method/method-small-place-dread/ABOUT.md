# Small-place dread

## What this method changes

Make a familiar bounded place progressively unsafe by combining practical irregularity, bodily response, remembered local knowledge, and pressure from other people.

**Narrator priority:** Track what the viewpoint character can physically verify, what the body pays, and which ordinary obligation prevents easy escape.

## Best fit

Houses, workplaces, roads, neighborhoods, institutions, and other legible spaces where threat can corrupt routine and social trust.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Not nonstop menace. Preserve neutral activity, social friction, plausible explanations. Cap dark asides at 2/scene; body signals one distinct per escalation step.

- Do not announce dread/evil/doom/coming horror.
- Do not rely on darkness/silence/cold without specific change+consequence.
- Do not use repeated heartbeat/breath/gooseflesh as primary fear.
- Do not joke at peak danger.
