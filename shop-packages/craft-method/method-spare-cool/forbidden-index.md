# Forbidden Index — Spare cool

## Diction bans

- Do not stack metaphors/similes/adjectives/sensory channels.
- Do not use >1 figurative comparison per 1000 words.
- Do not use abstract nouns when concrete action/object works.
- Never exceed ≤1 descriptive adjective per concrete noun.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Never use semicolons/parentheses/ellipses/exclamation/decorative dashes in narration.
- Do not use fragments as recurring cadence.
- Never exceed fragments max 1/500 words for material noun or irreversible result.
- Adverbs only when they change action meaning.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not pair emotion label with bodily proof of same emotion.
- Do not explain action after it reveals motive.
- Never label emotion when behavior/attention can carry it.

## Structural bans

- Do not end successive paragraphs with weighty one-line pronouncements.
- Never close with interpretation or moral.
- Never delete connective info for cause/position/agency.

## Anti-caricature

- Not monotonously short.
- Require sentence-length variation and spatial logic.
- Do not delete connective info for cause/position/agency.
