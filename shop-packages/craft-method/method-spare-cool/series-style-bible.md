# Series Style Bible — Spare cool

## Method promise

Deliver force through exact nouns, clean declarative clauses, selective omission, and controlled emotional distance.

## Narrator priority

State what happened, what was handled, what changed, and what choice followed. Trust sequence and selected fact to carry implication.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third or first; no roaming commentary.
- Specificity high for tools/terrain/sequence/injury/distance/exchange.
- Cool; emotion via decision/attention/avoidance/labor/changed speech.
- Fragments max 1/500 words for material noun or irreversible result.
- ≤1 descriptive adjective per concrete noun; adverbs only when they change action meaning.
- Tone: Warm 25/Clinical 75; Confident 90/Hedged 10; Serious 95/Playful 5.
- Dialogue complete enough to sound human; do not force narrator compression on every speaker.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Observation | Exact noun/condition | Mood adjective |
| Action | SVO clarity | Choreography commentary |
| Reflection | One concrete trigger+bounded inference | Essay |
| Emotion | Behavior/attention | Label+explanation |
| Close | Fact/consequence | Moral |

## Scene movement

- Open: physical action, observed fact, or immediate decision; object/relation not atmosphere.
- Rhythm: declarative accumulation; occasional compound for cause-effect.
- Close: completed action, cost, refusal, or concrete remainder; no interpretation.

## Anti-patterns

- Lush setting, elaborate interior, comic narration, abstract argument.
- Do not stack metaphors/similes/adjectives/sensory channels.
- Do not use >1 figurative comparison per 1000 words.
- Do not explain action after it reveals motive.
- Do not pair emotion label with bodily proof of same emotion.
- Do not use fragments as recurring cadence.
- Do not end successive paragraphs with weighty one-line pronouncements.
- Do not use abstract nouns when concrete action/object works.
- Not monotonously short.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Most narration short-medium complete declarative?
2. Concrete nouns/physical verbs outnumber abstracts?
3. No forbidden punctuation in narration?
4. Modifiers within cap?
5. Figurative within budget no stack?
6. Emotion via choice/attention/action before labels?
7. Spatial/causal links clear?
8. Reflection bounded to present trigger?
9. Sentence length varies enough?
10. Close fact/consequence without explanation?
