# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Spare** — removes modifiers that do not alter identity/condition/scale/action.
2. **Exact** — named objects, body positions, quantities, verbs with physical consequence.
3. **Cool** — withholds emotional interpretation until behavior cannot carry it.
4. **Steady** — syntax legible under action; no panic clutter.
5. **Unsparing** — damage and choice without euphemism/inflation.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Short to medium; one independent clause baseline.

**Paragraph length:** 1–3; longer only for continuous physical procedure.

**Rhythm:** Declarative accumulation; occasional compound for cause-effect.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Periods/commas; forbid semicolons/parentheses/ellipses/exclamation/decorative dashes in narration. Fragments max 1/500 words for material noun or irreversible result. ≤1 descriptive adjective per concrete noun; adverbs only when they change action meaning.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Common concrete physically exact; technical OK if viewpoint knows.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third or first; no roaming commentary.

**Specificity:** High for tools/terrain/sequence/injury/distance/exchange.

**Emotional temperature:** Cool; emotion via decision/attention/avoidance/labor/changed speech.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 25 / Clinical 75.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 90 / Hedged 10.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 95 / Playful 5.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Physical action, observed fact, or immediate decision; object/relation not atmosphere.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Completed action, cost, refusal, or concrete remainder; no interpretation.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Clarity, pressure, physical credibility, weight through omission.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Lush setting, elaborate interior, comic narration, abstract argument.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not stack metaphors/similes/adjectives/sensory channels.
- Do not use >1 figurative comparison per 1000 words.
- Do not explain action after it reveals motive.
- Do not pair emotion label with bodily proof of same emotion.
- Do not use fragments as recurring cadence.
- Do not end successive paragraphs with weighty one-line pronouncements.
- Do not use abstract nouns when concrete action/object works.
