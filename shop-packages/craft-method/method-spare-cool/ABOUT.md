# Spare cool

## What this method changes

Deliver force through exact nouns, clean declarative clauses, selective omission, and controlled emotional distance.

**Narrator priority:** State what happened, what was handled, what changed, and what choice followed. Trust sequence and selected fact to carry implication.

## Best fit

Action, travel, labor, conflict, grief, and morally difficult decisions that gain weight when the narrator refuses ornament and commentary.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Not monotonously short. Require sentence-length variation and spatial logic. Do not delete connective info for cause/position/agency.

- Do not stack metaphors/similes/adjectives/sensory channels.
- Do not use >1 figurative comparison per 1000 words.
- Do not explain action after it reveals motive.
- Do not pair emotion label with bodily proof of same emotion.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters copies only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml. Missing sheets write silently; existing sheets offer Overwrite, Append, or Chat about.
