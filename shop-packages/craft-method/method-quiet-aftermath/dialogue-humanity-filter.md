# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Quiet aftermath

## Register boundaries

- Socially controlled; directness varies by history/role/risk.
- Refer to arrangements/objects/timing/remembered wording not entire emotional history.
- Distinguish said/remembered/now-interpreted.

## Subtext gap

- Dialogue preserves social control/subtext.
- Remove mutual-understanding or retrospective therapy summaries.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Pauses via consequential action/silence not repeated ellipses.
- Ellipses forbidden.

## Narration around speech

- Do not convert dialogue into complete emotional history recitation.
- Preserve omission and social control over declaration.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Is dialogue socially controlled with directness varying by history/role/risk?
2. Do lines refer to arrangements/objects/timing/remembered wording not entire emotional history?
3. Are pauses via consequential action/silence not repeated ellipses?
4. Are said/remembered/now-interpreted distinguishable?
5. Are mutual-understanding or retrospective therapy summaries removed?
