# Series Style Bible — Quiet aftermath

## Method promise

Organize scenes around what remains after change: altered routine, incomplete account, belated recognition, material residue, and emotion that the narrator approaches without fully resolving.

## Narrator priority

Attend to residue and discrepancy, distinguish present fact from remembered account, and allow recognition to arrive late, partially, or indirectly.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV first or close third; unreliability from limits/revisions not factual trickery.
- Specificity high objects kept/discarded, routines changed, words remembered, rooms altered, social omissions.
- Cool-warm; low surface intensity accumulating residue.
- Qualification only for genuine uncertainty/memory limits/self-correction; do not hedge every assertion.
- Memory: present trigger + return with changed understanding/action.
- Ellipses/exclamation forbidden.
- Tone: Warm 45/Clinical 55; Confident 55/Hedged 45; Serious 95/Playful 5.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Present routine | Exact subdued | Mood announcement |
| Memory | Bounded source-aware | Seamless omniscient replay |
| Discrepancy | Calmly stated | Twist theatrics |
| Recognition | Partial specific | Total self-diagnosis |
| Close | Image/remainder/omission | Summary |

## Scene movement

- Open: after event, during routine altered by absence/knowledge, or material remainder with changed status immediately legible.
- Rhythm: present remainder→remembered account→discrepancy→partial recognition→return present.
- Close: image/object/repeated action/omission/modest recognition that recontextualizes without completing meaning.

## Anti-patterns

- Rapid action, overt confrontation, triumphant resolution, comic pacing, exhaustive explanation.
- Do not announce poignancy/sadness/nostalgia/significance/healing.
- Do not make every sentence hedged/evasive.
- Do not conceal facts viewpoint certainly knows solely for twist.
- Do not convert memory into complete cinematic replay without present mediation.
- Do not explain final image/object.
- Do not end with forgiveness/closure/self-knowledge unless scene materially earned only that limited change.
- Do not use jokes to escape emotional residue.
- Quiet ≠ vague/bloodless/inert.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening aftermath/altered routine with concrete remainder?
2. Present scene has action/choice/encounter/changed understanding?
3. Every memory has present trigger?
4. Remembered fact vs present interpretation distinguishable?
5. Qualification marks real uncertainty not habitual vagueness?
6. Known facts not withheld solely for twist?
7. Recognition partial specific?
8. Dialogue preserves social control/subtext?
9. Final image/object/action/omission unexplained?
10. Close leaves residue not tidy catharsis?
