# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Quiet** — avoid rhetorical peaks; selected facts hold weight.
2. **Reflective** — present observation to bounded memory without abandoning scene.
3. **Restrained** — withhold total explanation of motive/feeling.
4. **Precise** — changed objects/routines/wording/absences.
5. **Belated** — notice implications after action or years of rehearsed interpretation.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Medium with careful qualification; short for recognition/irreversible fact.

**Paragraph length:** 2–5.

**Rhythm:** Present remainder→remembered account→discrepancy→partial recognition→return present.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Conventional punctuation; semicolons sparingly; dashes rare; ellipses/exclamation forbidden. Qualification only for genuine uncertainty/memory limits/self-correction; do not hedge every assertion. Memory: present trigger + return with changed understanding/action.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Plain exact temporal relational material.

**POV / first-person use:** First or close third; unreliability from limits/revisions not factual trickery.

**Specificity:** High objects kept/discarded, routines changed, words remembered, rooms altered, social omissions.

**Emotional temperature:** Cool-warm; low surface intensity accumulating residue.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 45 / Clinical 55.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 55 / Hedged 45.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 95 / Playful 5.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** After event, during routine altered by absence/knowledge, or material remainder with changed status immediately legible.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Image/object/repeated action/omission/modest recognition that recontextualizes without completing meaning.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Regret, memory, ambiguity, emotional residue, subtle self-understanding shifts.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Rapid action, overt confrontation, triumphant resolution, comic pacing, exhaustive explanation.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not announce poignancy/sadness/nostalgia/significance/healing.
- Do not make every sentence hedged/evasive.
- Do not conceal facts viewpoint certainly knows solely for twist.
- Do not convert memory into complete cinematic replay without present mediation.
- Do not explain final image/object.
- Do not end with forgiveness/closure/self-knowledge unless scene materially earned only that limited change.
- Do not use jokes to escape emotional residue.
