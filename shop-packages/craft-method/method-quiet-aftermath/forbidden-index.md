# Forbidden Index — Quiet aftermath

## Diction bans

- Do not announce poignancy/sadness/nostalgia/significance/healing.
- Do not explain final image/object.
- Do not use jokes to escape emotional residue.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Do not make every sentence hedged/evasive.
- Never use ellipses/exclamation.
- Qualification only for genuine uncertainty/memory limits/self-correction.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not end with forgiveness/closure/self-knowledge unless scene materially earned only that limited change.
- Never perform total self-diagnosis at recognition.
- Low surface intensity accumulating residue.

## Structural bans

- Do not conceal facts viewpoint certainly knows solely for twist.
- Do not convert memory into complete cinematic replay without present mediation.
- Every memory must have present trigger.
- Never close with summary instead of image/remainder/omission.

## Anti-caricature

- Quiet ≠ vague/bloodless/inert.
- Scene still needs present action/choice/encounter/changed understanding.
- Ambiguity from incomplete knowledge/mixed motive not missing basic info.
