# Series Style Bible — Clip thriller

## Method promise

Keep every beat under pressure by centering immediate objectives, obstacles, operational observation, fast choices, and consequences that alter the next move.

## Narrator priority

At every moment make clear what the viewpoint wants now, what blocks it, what usable information changes, and what action follows.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV tight close third or first; strict knowledge limits.
- Specificity high for exits/hands/distance/timing/LOS/obstacles/usable objects.
- Hot situation, cool narration.
- Interior thought max 2 sentences before external action unless planning beat.
- Backstory max 1 sentence under pressure and only if changes present choice.
- Tone: Warm 20/Clinical 80; Confident 90/Hedged 10; Serious 95/Playful 5.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Scan | Tactical inventory | Scenic tourism |
| Decision | Direct brief | Debate after commitment |
| Movement | Spatially anchored verbs | Floating camera |
| Impact | Exact immediate result | Delayed generic reaction |
| Pause | Resource/status check | Philosophical drift |

## Scene movement

- Open: active objective/deadline/pursuit/problem in first paragraph.
- Rhythm: objective→scan→obstacle→choice→consequence→revised objective.
- Close: reversal, new cost, worsened objective, or forced next action; not reflection alone.

## Anti-patterns

- Leisurely atmosphere, discursive interiority, multi-page explanation, action without stake change.
- Do not open with weather/waking/biography/atmosphere unless active obstacle.
- Do not narrate every movement equal weight.
- Do not insert interior essays while threat demands action.
- Do not fake urgency via fragments alone.
- Do not conceal known spatial facts solely for surprise.
- Do not repeat objective without changing obstacle/cost.
- Do not end on vague promise danger is coming.
- Speed ≠ constant staccato.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. First paragraph immediate objective/problem?
2. Each major beat changes obstacle/info/cost/action?
3. Attention prioritizes tactical detail?
4. Spatial relations clear in movement?
5. Reflection ≤2 sentences under pressure?
6. Backstory only if changes present choice?
7. Clipping does not replace causal clarity?
8. Dialogue changes leverage/info?
9. No known spatial fact hidden for surprise?
10. Close forces/changes next action?
