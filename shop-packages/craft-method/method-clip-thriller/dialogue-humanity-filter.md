# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Clip thriller

## Register boundaries

- Leverage/info/delay/command/misdirection.
- Rarely state full objective.
- Anchor position/hands/exits/time/control.

## Subtext gap

- Information and leverage change through what is withheld, delayed, or redirected.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Interruptions need tactical reason.
- No universal one-word replies.

## Narration around speech

- Remove banter that pauses pressure without leverage change.
- Dialogue changes leverage/info.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Does dialogue change leverage or info?
2. Do lines rarely state full objective?
3. Are interruptions limited to tactical reason?
4. Are position/hands/exits/time/control anchored?
5. Is banter without leverage change removed?
