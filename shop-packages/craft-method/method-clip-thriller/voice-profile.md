# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Clipped** — short paragraph units; remove transitions sequence already supplies.
2. **Alert** — threat/access/timing/leverage/anomaly over decorative setting.
3. **Decisive** — observation→choice same or next paragraph.
4. **Concrete** — space/time/objects/body when tactically relevant.
5. **Pressurized** — each beat narrows time/options/safety/certainty.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Short; medium for tactical synthesis.

**Paragraph length:** 1–3; one-sentence OK for turns not every action.

**Rhythm:** Objective→scan→obstacle→choice→consequence→revised objective.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Periods dominate; colon for inventory; forbid semicolons/ellipses; dashes rare. Interior thought max 2 sentences before external action unless planning beat. Backstory max 1 sentence under pressure and only if changes present choice.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Operational spatial kinetic plain.

**POV / first-person use:** Tight close third or first; strict knowledge limits.

**Specificity:** High for exits/hands/distance/timing/LOS/obstacles/usable objects.

**Emotional temperature:** Hot situation, cool narration.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 20 / Clinical 80.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 90 / Hedged 10.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 95 / Playful 5.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Active objective/deadline/pursuit/problem in first paragraph.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Reversal, new cost, worsened objective, or forced next action; not reflection alone.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Momentum, readable action, tactical suspense, cause-effect.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Leisurely atmosphere, discursive interiority, multi-page explanation, action without stake change.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not open with weather/waking/biography/atmosphere unless active obstacle.
- Do not narrate every movement equal weight.
- Do not insert interior essays while threat demands action.
- Do not fake urgency via fragments alone.
- Do not conceal known spatial facts solely for surprise.
- Do not repeat objective without changing obstacle/cost.
- Do not end on vague promise danger is coming.
