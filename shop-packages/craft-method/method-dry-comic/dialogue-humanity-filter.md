# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Dry comic

## Register boundaries

- Sincere goals; no shared comedy awareness.
- Status via overprecision/evasion/correction/literal compliance.

## Subtext gap

- Mismatch between claim and reality; reader completes the joke.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Delayed response OK.

## Narration around speech

- No comic interpretation after landing line.
- Delete duplicate joke narrator vs tag.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Do characters pursue sincere goals without shared comedy awareness?
2. Does status show via overprecision/evasion/correction/literal compliance?
3. Is comic interpretation after landing line removed?
4. Are duplicate jokes between narrator and tag deleted?
5. Does character humor move stakes when it exceeds narrator budget?
