# Series Style Bible — Dry comic

## Method promise

Produce humor by reporting disproportion, flawed logic, status behavior, and inconvenient consequence with a straight narrative face.

## Narrator priority

Preserve literal clarity, identify the mismatch between claim and reality, and leave room for the reader to complete the joke.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third or lightly omniscient.
- Specificity high for rules/objects/claims/consequences.
- Cool surface; underlying concern real.
- Comic turns: max 1 deliberate narrator turn/paragraph and 3/scene; character humor may exceed if stakes move.
- Parentheses max 1/1000 words.
- Tone: Warm 45/Clinical 55; Confident 90/Hedged 10; Serious 55/Playful 45.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Sincere goal | Straight specific | Pre-mocking |
| Status display | Exact social observation | Sneering adjective |
| Mismatch | Concise juxtaposition | Explanation |
| Consequence | Practical real | Reset for next gag |
| Pain/grief | Humor recedes | Punchline |

## Scene movement

- Open: sincere objective/rule/social arrangement/self-image; no standalone joke.
- Rhythm: setup as normal→precise mismatch→practical consequence; no laugh line.
- Close: consequence/failed dignity/literal fact exposing mismatch; no tag explaining funny.

## Anti-patterns

- Slapstick, gag chains, sentimental uplift, narrator performance detached from stakes.
- Do not use rimshot syntax, joke-question answers, or second punchline.
- Do not call absurd/ridiculous/hilarious/comic/ironic.
- Do not wink/address reader.
- Do not make every description a comparison.
- Do not sacrifice causality/intelligence for gag.
- Do not place narrator humor at peak grief/injury/terror.
- Do not repeat comic trait until catchphrase.
- Not relentless snark.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Sincere objective/rule before mismatch?
2. Humor from specific contradiction?
3. Narration never labels humor?
4. No second explanatory tag?
5. Narrator turns within budget?
6. Characters retain intelligence?
7. Stakes survive joke?
8. Humor recedes at peak pain/danger?
9. Narrator never addresses reader?
10. Close exposes mismatch via fact/consequence?
