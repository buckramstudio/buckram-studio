# Dry comic

## What this method changes

Produce humor by reporting disproportion, flawed logic, status behavior, and inconvenient consequence with a straight narrative face.

**Narrator priority:** Preserve literal clarity, identify the mismatch between claim and reality, and leave room for the reader to complete the joke.

## Best fit

Social friction, bureaucracy, group dynamics, flawed plans, embarrassment, and serious situations where humor should sharpen rather than dissolve stakes.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Not relentless snark. Most sentences ordinary narrative. Humor from current stakes; consequences intact.

- Do not use rimshot syntax, joke-question answers, or second punchline.
- Do not call absurd/ridiculous/hilarious/comic/ironic.
- Do not wink/address reader.
- Do not place narrator humor at peak grief/injury/terror.

## Apply behavior

Install previews the pack in Library. Apply to Style Filters copies only the targets listed in apply-manifest.yaml. Missing sheets write silently; existing sheets offer Overwrite, Append, or Chat about.
