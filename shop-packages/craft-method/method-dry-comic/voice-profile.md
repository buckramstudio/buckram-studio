# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Dry** — does not announce jokes/amusement/absurdity/cleverness.
2. **Exact** — humor in specific contradiction/priority/rule/consequence.
3. **Unflustered** — controlled syntax while events disproportionate.
4. **Socially alert** — status/self-presentation/politeness/face-saving.
5. **Economical** — end comic turn immediately after mismatch lands.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Medium; balanced clauses for claim vs consequence.

**Paragraph length:** 1–4.

**Rhythm:** Setup as normal→precise mismatch→practical consequence; no laugh line.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Conventional punctuation; exclamation forbidden in narration; italics cannot cue sarcasm; parentheses max 1/1000 words. Comic turns: max 1 deliberate narrator turn/paragraph and 3/scene; character humor may exceed if stakes move.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Plain to mildly formal; bureaucratic/elevated only for status mismatch mechanism.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third or lightly omniscient.

**Specificity:** High for rules/objects/claims/consequences.

**Emotional temperature:** Cool surface; underlying concern real.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 45 / Clinical 55.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 90 / Hedged 10.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 55 / Playful 45.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Sincere objective/rule/social arrangement/self-image; no standalone joke.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Consequence/failed dignity/literal fact exposing mismatch; no tag explaining funny.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Humane satire, social observation, relief without breaking reality.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Slapstick, gag chains, sentimental uplift, narrator performance detached from stakes.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not use rimshot syntax, joke-question answers, or second punchline.
- Do not call absurd/ridiculous/hilarious/comic/ironic.
- Do not wink/address reader.
- Do not make every description a comparison.
- Do not sacrifice causality/intelligence for gag.
- Do not place narrator humor at peak grief/injury/terror.
- Do not repeat comic trait until catchphrase.
