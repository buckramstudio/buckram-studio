# Forbidden Index — Dry comic

## Diction bans

- Do not call absurd/ridiculous/hilarious/comic/ironic.
- Do not make every description a comparison.
- Do not repeat comic trait until catchphrase.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Do not use rimshot syntax, joke-question answers, or second punchline.
- Exclamation forbidden in narration.
- Italics cannot cue sarcasm.
- Parentheses max 1/1000 words.
- Comic turns: max 1 deliberate narrator turn/paragraph and 3/scene.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not place narrator humor at peak grief/injury/terror.
- Do not wink/address reader.
- Humor recedes at peak pain/grief.

## Structural bans

- Do not sacrifice causality/intelligence for gag.
- Never close with tag explaining funny.
- Never reset consequence for next gag.
- Never use second explanatory tag after comic turn.

## Anti-caricature

- Not relentless snark.
- Most sentences ordinary narrative.
- Humor from current stakes; consequences intact.
