# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Clinical report

## Register boundaries

- Official/private shorthand/guarded admission/direct command.
- Status via who asks/records/interrupts/answers conditionally/refuses.
- Formal role order until meaningful breach.
- Documents/seating/timing/access/record-making when alter power.
- Remove lines explaining already-visible dynamics.

## Subtext gap

- Personal consequence suppressed by role language; guarded admission and conditional answers carry what records omit.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Formal role order until meaningful breach.
- Status via who asks/records/interrupts/answers conditionally/refuses.

## Narration around speech

- Dialogue register changes with role/privacy.
- Do not force institutional compression on every speaker outside official context.
