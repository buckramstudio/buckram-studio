# Clinical report

## What this method changes

Reveal human stakes through procedural sequence, institutional language, selective inventory, and the telling gap between what systems record and what people endure.

**Narrator priority:** Establish role, procedure, evidence, deviation, and disposition; allow emotion to emerge from selection, omission, and the cost of classification.

## Best fit

Bureaucracy, investigation, organizations, professional environments, secrecy, compromise, and scenes where role language suppresses personal consequence.

## Hard limits

**Anti-caricature:** Not bloodless/uniformly passive. Active syntax for accountable actions. One precise human remainder where procedure cannot contain consequence; never sentimental spotlight.

- Do not confuse euphemism with narrator endorsement.
- Do not use passive to hide identifiable actor.
- Do not dump acronyms/ranks/process for texture.
- Do not label cold/corrupt/guilty/innocent/broken without observable basis.
