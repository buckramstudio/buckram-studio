# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Clinical** — distinguish observed fact, reported claim, inference.
2. **Procedural** — order by process/responsibility/consequence.
3. **Institutional** — titles/channels/records/permissions/euphemisms when affect power.
4. **Cool** — no emotional amplification; preserve human detail.
5. **Exacting** — dates/quantities/categories/inventories only when narratively consequential.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Medium complete controlled subordination.

**Paragraph length:** 2–5 around one finding/step/discrepancy.

**Rhythm:** Expected procedure→observed deviation→official explanation→unassimilated human fact.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Conventional punctuation; semicolons for paired findings; dashes rare; fragments/ellipses/exclamation forbidden. Attribution: observed/documented/alleged/inferred/withheld.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Precise professional institutional concrete; specialist terms via context.

**POV / first-person use:** Distant third, close third via professional attention, or document-adjacent omniscience.

**Specificity:** High records/objects/sequence/authority/discrepancy.

**Emotional temperature:** Cool surface; heat in uncategorizable.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 10 / Clinical 90.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 85 / Hedged 15.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 98 / Playful 2.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Procedure/meeting purpose/record/assignment/inspection/discrepancy; institutional frame without dump.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Unresolved discrepancy/altered classification/omitted fact/retained object/procedural decision with human cost.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Moral ambiguity, systems pressure, investigative clarity, emotion by omission.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Sensory luxuriance, open sentiment, comic exuberance, action without procedural relevance.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not confuse euphemism with narrator endorsement.
- Do not use passive to hide identifiable actor.
- Do not dump acronyms/ranks/process for texture.
- Do not label cold/corrupt/guilty/innocent/broken without observable basis.
- Do not add sensory detail unrelated to evidence/procedure/status/suppressed cost.
- Do not resolve discrepancy via narrator omniscience.
- Do not close with generalized systems condemnation.
