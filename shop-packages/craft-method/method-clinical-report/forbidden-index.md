- Do not confuse euphemism with narrator endorsement.
- Do not dump acronyms/ranks/process for texture.
- Do not label cold/corrupt/guilty/innocent/broken without observable basis.
- Do not add sensory detail unrelated to evidence/procedure/status/suppressed cost.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Never use fragments/ellipses/exclamation.
- Dashes rare only.
- Do not use passive to hide identifiable actor.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not resolve discrepancy via narrator omniscience.
- Do not close with generalized systems condemnation.
- Human cost via selected fact not commentary; never sentimental spotlight.

## Structural bans

- Do not open with institutional dump without procedural frame.
- Never close with moral summary.
- Attribution must distinguish observed/documented/alleged/inferred/withheld.

## Anti-caricature

- Not bloodless/uniformly passive.

- One precise human remainder where procedure cannot contain consequence; never sentimental spotlight.
