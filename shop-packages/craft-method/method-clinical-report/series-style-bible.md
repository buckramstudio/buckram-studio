# Series Style Bible — Clinical report

## Method promise

Reveal human stakes through procedural sequence, institutional language, selective inventory, and the telling gap between what systems record and what people endure.

## Narrator priority

Establish role, procedure, evidence, deviation, and disposition; allow emotion to emerge from selection, omission, and the cost of classification.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV distant third, close third via professional attention, or document-adjacent omniscience.
- Specificity high records/objects/sequence/authority/discrepancy.
- Cool surface; heat in uncategorizable.
- Attribution: observed/documented/alleged/inferred/withheld.
- Tone: Warm 10/Clinical 90; Confident 85/Hedged 15; Serious 98/Playful 2.
- Dialogue official/private shorthand/guarded admission/direct command.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Routine process | Exact neutral | Menace |
| Evidence | Source-qualified | Omniscient certainty |
| Deviation | Comparative concrete | Melodrama |
| Human cost | Selected detail no commentary | Sentimentality |
| Decision | Role authority consequence | Moral summary |

## Scene movement

- Open: procedure/meeting purpose/record/assignment/inspection/discrepancy; institutional frame without dump.
- Rhythm: expected procedure→observed deviation→official explanation→unassimilated human fact.
- Close: unresolved discrepancy/altered classification/omitted fact/retained object/procedural decision with human cost.

## Anti-patterns

- Sensory luxuriance, open sentiment, comic exuberance, action without procedural relevance.
- Do not confuse euphemism with narrator endorsement.
- Do not use passive to hide identifiable actor.
- Do not dump acronyms/ranks/process for texture.
- Do not label cold/corrupt/guilty/innocent/broken without observable basis.
- Do not add sensory detail unrelated to evidence/procedure/status/suppressed cost.
- Do not resolve discrepancy via narrator omniscience.
- Do not close with generalized systems condemnation.
- Not bloodless/uniformly passive.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening procedure/record/assignment/discrepancy?
2. Fact/report/inference/withheld distinct?
3. Institutional detail affects power/process/consequence?
4. Deviations concrete comparative?
5. Passive does not conceal known actors?
6. Human cost via selected fact not commentary?
7. Dialogue register changes with role/privacy?
8. Narration does not endorse euphemism?
9. Discrepancy not solved by unearned omniscience?
10. Close procedural choice/classification/omission/remainder with human cost?
