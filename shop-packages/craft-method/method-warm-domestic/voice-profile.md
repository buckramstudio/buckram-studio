# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Warm** — comprehensible need without erasing harm.
2. **Tactile** — handled objects/food/fabric/temperature/wear/arrangement as relational evidence.
3. **Close** — small judgments/corrections in viewpoint attention.
4. **Patient** — practical activity and conversational avoidance accumulate meaning.
5. **Gently exact** — affection/obligation/irritation/embarrassment/habit via behavior.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Medium with subordinate clauses for social qualification/memory.

**Paragraph length:** 2–5; shorten when gesture changes relationship.

**Rhythm:** Task/hospitality→observation of another→private correction/memory→spoken deflection→altered care.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Conventional punctuation; parentheses rare; dashes for self-correction not constant interruption; exclamation dialogue-only. Memory brief object/routine-triggered; return present within paragraph or next.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Familiar tactile relational precise about domestic labor.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third.

**Specificity:** High for objects in use/food/clothing/rooms/habits/exchanges.

**Emotional temperature:** Warm surface, active friction underneath.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 85 / Clinical 15.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 65 / Hedged 35.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 65 / Playful 35.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Care work/preparation/arrival/tidying/feeding/fixing/waiting/ordinary relational task.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Changed care gesture, object accepted/refused/left, or practical decision with relational consequence.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Layered relationships, humane conflict, emotion in daily life.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Impersonal spectacle, purely tactical, contempt-only, sentiment without behavior.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not equate domestic work with automatic virtue.
- Do not use food/warmth/soft fabric as generic comfort without relational function.
- Do not sentimentalize every care act.
- Do not explain subtext immediately after gesture/dialogue.
- Do not make every character equally articulate about feelings.
- Do not resolve long conflict via one perfect speech.
- Do not use quirk lists as characterization.
