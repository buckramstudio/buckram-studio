# Series Style Bible — Warm domestic

## Method promise

Reveal relationship pressure through ordinary care, household logistics, tactile objects, social memory, and the gap between what people do for one another and what they can say.

## Narrator priority

Notice who performs care, who receives it, who avoids it, and how ordinary spaces and tasks register changing relationships.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third.
- Specificity high for objects in use/food/clothing/rooms/habits/exchanges.
- Warm surface, active friction underneath.
- Memory brief object/routine-triggered; return present within paragraph or next.
- Tone: Warm 85/Clinical 15; Confident 65/Hedged 35; Serious 65/Playful 35.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Routine care | Concrete unshowy | Caregiver halo |
| Irritation | Socially specific behavior-led | Contempt caricature |
| Memory | Brief object-triggered selective | Biography dump |
| Conflict | Indirect consequential | Therapy-summary dialogue |
| Repair | Partial action | Complete emotional resolution on demand |

## Scene movement

- Open: care work/preparation/arrival/tidying/feeding/fixing/waiting/ordinary relational task.
- Rhythm: task/hospitality→observation of another→private correction/memory→spoken deflection→altered care.
- Close: changed care gesture, object accepted/refused/left, or practical decision with relational consequence.

## Anti-patterns

- Impersonal spectacle, purely tactical, contempt-only, sentiment without behavior.
- Do not equate domestic work with automatic virtue.
- Do not use food/warmth/soft fabric as generic comfort without relational function.
- Do not sentimentalize every care act.
- Do not explain subtext immediately after gesture/dialogue.
- Do not make every character equally articulate about feelings.
- Do not resolve long conflict via one perfect speech.
- Do not use quirk lists as characterization.
- Warmth coexists with inconvenience/resentment/asymmetry/failed care.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening relationally meaningful practical activity?
2. Tactile handled/consequential not decorative?
3. Each major person comprehensible need without absolution?
4. Care as labor/choice/habit/debt/negotiation?
5. Memory triggered by present objects/routines?
6. Dialogue subtext via offers/refusals/deflection?
7. Narration does not explain gesture twice?
8. Conflict specific not contemptuous?
9. Repair partial behavioral?
10. Close practical gesture/object with relational consequence?
