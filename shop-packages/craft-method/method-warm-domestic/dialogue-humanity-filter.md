# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Warm domestic

## Register boundaries

- Vary directness.
- Care/conflict via offers/refusals/corrections/reminders/topic changes.
- Pair with tasks/objects only when altering subtext.

## Subtext gap

- Offers, refusals, and deflection carry relationship pressure without diagnosis.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Overlap from familiarity/avoidance.

## Narration around speech

- Remove relationship-diagnosis speeches.
- Do not explain subtext immediately after dialogue.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Does dialogue use offers/refusals/deflection for subtext?
2. Is directness varied across speakers?
3. Are tasks/objects paired with speech only when altering subtext?
4. Are relationship-diagnosis speeches removed?
5. Does overlap reflect familiarity or avoidance?
