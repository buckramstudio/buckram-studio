# Forbidden Index — Warm domestic

## Diction bans

- Do not equate domestic work with automatic virtue.
- Do not use food/warmth/soft fabric as generic comfort without relational function.
- Do not use quirk lists as characterization.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Do not explain subtext immediately after gesture/dialogue.
- Never use exclamation in narration.
- Parentheses rare; dashes for self-correction not constant interruption.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not sentimentalize every care act.
- Do not make every character equally articulate about feelings.
- Do not resolve long conflict via one perfect speech.
- Never perform complete emotional resolution on demand.

## Structural bans

- Do not explain subtext immediately after gesture/dialogue.
- Never close without changed care gesture, object accepted/refused/left, or practical decision with relational consequence.
- Never reduce conflict to contempt caricature.

## Anti-caricature

- Warmth coexists with inconvenience/resentment/asymmetry/failed care.
- Cozy detail only for items used, relationally noticed, or materially changed.
