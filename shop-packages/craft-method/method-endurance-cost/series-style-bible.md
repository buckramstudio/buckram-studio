# Series Style Bible — Endurance cost

## Method promise

Make hardship credible by tracking the accumulating effects of exertion, environment, scarcity, injury, compromise, and incomplete recovery.

## Narrator priority

Record available resources, bodily capability, environmental resistance, choices made to continue, and the cost carried into the next beat.

## Non-negotiable defaults

- POV close third or first.
- Specificity very high capability/terrain/supply/injury effect/effort.
- Cold-to-grave; tenderness via labor/sacrifice not consolation language.
- Cost ledger: every major exertion changes ≥1 of energy/mobility/time/supplies/exposure/pain/trust/moral position.
- Recovery consumes time/safety/resources/assistance/opportunity; never reset by scene transition.
- Dashes/ellipses/exclamation forbidden in narration.
- Tone: Warm 20/Clinical 80; Confident 90/Hedged 10; Serious 99/Playful 1.

## Register map (Situation|Required register|Forbidden drift table)

| Situation | Required register | Forbidden drift |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Baseline | Current capability/deficit | Generic toughness |
| Exertion | Action+mechanical body effect | Pain poetry |
| Injury | Functional consequence | Aestheticized damage |
| Rest/care | Resource exchange incomplete recovery | Reset |
| Choice | Explicit material/moral tradeoff | Noble abstraction |

## Scene movement

- Open: demand on body/resources + current deficit before new pressure.
- Rhythm: demand→effort→immediate loss→adaptation→carried deficit.
- Close: carried cost, worsened capacity, necessary compromise, incomplete shelter, consumed resource, or next obligation made harder.

## Anti-patterns

- Effortless heroics, reset pacing, decorative brutality, catharsis erasing material conditions.
- Do not use suffering as proof of virtue.
- Do not repeat generic pain/exhaustion/blood/cold without changing function/cost.
- Do not let injured bodies perform full capacity without adaptation/consequence.
- Do not aestheticize wounds/starvation/exposure/death.
- Do not insert jokes neutralizing active suffering.
- Do not grant food/sleep/shelter/medicine/rescue without source and cost.
- Do not close on tidy catharsis/spiritual cleansing/claim hardship made someone whole.
- Not unmodulated misery.

## Revision scan (10 yes/no checks)

1. Opening bodily/resource demand + baseline?
2. Every major exertion changes named cost-ledger category?
3. Injury/fatigue alter capability?
4. Recovery consumes time/safety/resources/aid/opportunity?
5. Supplies have sources and depletion?
6. Pain detail functional not aesthetic?
7. Practical care without erasing cost?
8. Dialogue under strain speaker-specific purposeful?
9. No silent condition reset on scene transition?
10. Close carries deficit/compromise/worsened obligation?
