# Voice Profile

> Narrative voice fingerprint for this project. Story bible and personas override this file when they conflict.

See also: `pipeline/shared/craft-floor/anti-ai-rules.md`

## How to read this file

Every field below is a **hard constraint** on output, not a soft preference. If a constraint conflicts with what the writer just asked for, surface the conflict — do not silently override the profile.

**Profile basis:** Craft method pack

## Voice adjectives

Confirm 3–5 words with Chat about. Add one line of operational evidence (observable rule, never fictional prose):

1. **Material** — weight/distance/temperature/damage/supply/terrain/bodily function when consequential.
2. **Grim** — refuses comforting interpretation without decorating suffering.
3. **Patient** — accumulates cost across beats/scenes.
4. **Unsparing** — preserves injury/exhaustion/debt/moral compromise.
5. **Plain** — direct syntax; limited figurative around pain.

## Sentence-level patterns

**Average sentence length:** Short to medium; longer only for linked physical process or cumulative inventory.

**Paragraph length:** 1–4.

**Rhythm:** Demand→effort→immediate loss→adaptation→carried deficit.

**Punctuation tendencies:** Periods/commas; semicolons rare; dashes/ellipses/exclamation forbidden in narration. Cost ledger: every major exertion changes ≥1 of energy/mobility/time/supplies/exposure/pain/trust/moral position. Recovery consumes time/safety/resources/assistance/opportunity; never reset by scene transition.

## Voice register

**Vocabulary:** Bodily material environmental logistical plain.

**POV / first-person use:** Close third or first.

**Specificity:** Very high capability/terrain/supply/injury effect/effort.

**Emotional temperature:** Cold-to-grave; tenderness via labor/sacrifice not consolation language.

## Tone axes

Place the voice on each axis with one sentence of explanation:

**Warm ↔ Clinical:** Warm 20 / Clinical 80.

**Confident ↔ Hedged:** Confident 90 / Hedged 10.

**Serious ↔ Playful:** Serious 99 / Playful 1.

## Structural habits

**How scenes / chapters tend to open:** Demand on body/resources + current deficit before new pressure.

**How scenes / chapters tend to close:** Carried cost, worsened capacity, necessary compromise, incomplete shelter, consumed resource, or next obligation made harder.

**What this voice is noticeably good at:** Physical credibility, cumulative consequence, survival pressure, morally weighty endurance.

**What this voice noticeably avoids or struggles with:** Effortless heroics, reset pacing, decorative brutality, catharsis erasing material conditions.

## What not to write

Voice-specific bans (also see forbidden index and craft floor):

- Do not use suffering as proof of virtue.
- Do not repeat generic pain/exhaustion/blood/cold without changing function/cost.
- Do not let injured bodies perform full capacity without adaptation/consequence.
- Do not aestheticize wounds/starvation/exposure/death.
- Do not insert jokes neutralizing active suffering.
- Do not grant food/sleep/shelter/medicine/rescue without source and cost.
- Do not close on tidy catharsis/spiritual cleansing/claim hardship made someone whole.
