# Forbidden Index — Endurance cost

## Diction bans

- Do not repeat generic pain/exhaustion/blood/cold without changing function/cost.
- Do not aestheticize wounds/starvation/exposure/death.
- Do not grant food/sleep/shelter/medicine/rescue without source and cost.

## Syntax and rhythm bans

- Never use dashes/ellipses/exclamation in narration.
- Semicolons rare only.

## Emotional handling bans

- Do not use suffering as proof of virtue.
- Do not insert jokes neutralizing active suffering.
- Do not close on tidy catharsis/spiritual cleansing/claim hardship made someone whole.
- Tenderness via labor/sacrifice not consolation language.

## Structural bans

- Do not let injured bodies perform full capacity without adaptation/consequence.
- Never reset cost by scene transition.
- Recovery must consume time/safety/resources/assistance/opportunity.
- Every major exertion must change ≥1 cost-ledger category.

## Anti-caricature

- Not unmodulated misery.
- Include neutral procedure/cooperation/practical care/temporary relief but account for cost and incomplete restore.
