# Dialogue Humanity Filter — Endurance cost

## Register boundaries

- Speech shortens with exertion but not universal fragments.
- Concern via instruction/resource sharing/conceal incapacity/refusal/triage.
- Speaker-specific under strain; purposeful not toughness/despair declarations.

## Subtext gap

- Track breath/position/assistance/task when functionally changed.
- Remove toughness/despair declarations duplicating visible behavior.

## Turn-taking and interruption

- Interruption from urgency/physical limit only.
- No universal fragments under strain.

## Narration around speech

- Dialogue under strain speaker-specific purposeful.
- Do not let dialogue duplicate visible bodily cost already shown in narration.

## Dialogue revision scan

1. Does speech shorten with exertion without universal fragments?
2. Is concern shown via instruction/resource sharing/conceal incapacity/refusal/triage?
3. Are interruptions limited to urgency/physical limit?
4. Are breath/position/assistance/task tracked when functionally changed?
5. Are toughness/despair declarations duplicating visible behavior removed?
