# Builder: Romance couple

GMC for both leads + relationship beat checklist. Theory → market → .

## Light

Job: one couple sheet before drafting the romance through-line (CR / HR / PNR / romantasy / RS).

Contract: dual POV = fill both columns; Black Moment is wound-rooted, not cheap misunderstanding; HEA/HFN proves both GMC threads.

Must-not-break:
- Why only *these* two (perfect-match / sees behind armor)
- Each partner’s GMC + how the other is their Conflict
- Black Moment cost = deepest Fear activated (late + sustained)
- Grand gesture proves growth (not random fireworks)
- Optimistic ending lands for *both* GMC threads
- Closer after emotional risk that shows Essence; distance after Identity retreat
- Suspense is emotional (how / when) — not plot surprise about whether they end together
- Heat lane locked early; intimacy advances emotional arc / reveals character

## Hard

**Poly / RH / multi-lead**
- Duplicate Partner B block per additional lead
- Central romantic question must still be answerable per pairing or as constellation with clear promise

**Heat dial × beat map**
- Lock heat early; signal in marketing — no universal industry scale
- Midpoint intimacy shift scales with heat (kiss / confession / night / truth) — promise must match shelf
- Intimacy scenes must advance emotional arc / reveal character
- Dark romance / RS: Black Moment may include external threat — still wound-rooted for emotional half; late + sustained

**Perfect-match diagnostic**
- If either could swap for a generic love interest, “why these two” failed — rewrite Essence visibility
- Identity vs Essence: intimacy scenes show Essence; fights often Identity retreat — plan both

**Chat-about verbs** (when iterating this sheet)
- **Intake** — heat lane / shelf promises / what already exists before inventing beats
- **Stress-test** — remove external obstacle; check Fear-loss still bites
- **Canon-lock** — after Book N, dump relationship state + open promises + heat lane for next book
- **Canon paste** — re-attach dual GMC + beat statuses before inventing new beats

**Handoff / scope**
- **Produces:** dual-GMC couple sheet + Fear-loss lines + beat statuses + heat-lane lock.
- **Next:** romance arc beside external plot ; series canon-lock if Book N+ ; scene/chapter cards for on-page beats.
- **Not this sheet:** erotic intimacy builder; market comps dump ; full external beat cosmology .

**Methods cited:** Dixon GMC (dual); HEA/HFN optimistic-ending practice; emotional-suspense / barrier diagnostics. Paraphrased — not a substitute for the primary texts.
**Refuse:** no universal heat scales; no chemistry laundry lists as the sheet’s spine; Chat verbs are operational patterns, not craft law.
