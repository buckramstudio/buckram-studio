**Order of use**
1. Place bible header (region, map, home vs foreign, travel speed rules)
2. Recurring place cards for load-bearing sites
3. Travel / route ledger
4. Sparse territory / faction map
5. Place-as-character audit
6. Series continuity stubs

**5–6. Audits**

- ☐ Climate / season calendar notes

## Hard

**Setting-as-character (craft bridge)** — character-tool analogues for bible work (scene blocking stays on stage sheet)
- Character-tool analogues: personality→rules of how place behaves; interiority→mood/secrets; conflict→obstacle/enabler; arc→place or perception changes
- Describe-while-acting (blocking), not postcard block — scene work lives on 

**Chat-about verbs** (location-specific; discuss sheet merges with stage)
- **Canon paste** — re-attach place cards + travel ledger before inventing new cities

**Series / dual-timeline edges**
- Ownership and disaster stubs prevent book-2 geography retcons
- Dual-timeline travel sanity lives with — location bible supplies distances; timeline owns chronology
- Expand-don’t-add: deepen ramifications of existing sites before inventing new cities 
- Stage (this scene’s blocking space) ≠ Location (durable bible) — keep the hard split

**When to break “sparse faction map”**
- Epic / multi-region war needs denser zone table — still require plot relevance per row
- Single-room novels: one place card + stage cards may suffice; don’t fake a continent
