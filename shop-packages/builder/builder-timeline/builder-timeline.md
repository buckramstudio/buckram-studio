# Builder: timeline

Chronology / dual-timeline / elapsed-time worksheet. Theory stays in craft (`craft-dual-timeline`, flashbacks, series) — this sheet is fillable braid chrome.

## Light

Job: keep absolute clocks and reading order honest; force equal-partner dual tracks before Act Two braid polish.

Contract: log both abs and story order; past earns page (changes present), not tourism; convergence designed, not hoped for.

Must-not-break:
- Story order ≠ chronological order — log both
- Ages / injuries / messages travel on believable clocks
- Dual timelines: each track has Want/clock/turns — or demote past to flashback
- Every switch changes what the reader wants from the other track
- Convergence type named before drafting braid
- Orientation convention consistent; ban double-shift (jump eras *and* reverse within era)
- No character in two places without warp rule
- Mystery crime timeline reconciles with clue ledger

**Methods cited:** weak `builder-timeline` abs/story logs; `craft-dual-timeline` handshake/orientation/Weiland families; Novelry dual cut-points; River Editor alternation/convergence; Writers in the Storm chrono-first + double-shift ban; Oscar Ghostwriting convergence/mirror/revelation planning docs. Paraphrased — not craft law.
**Refuse:** past-as-tourism; flashback dressed as equal braid without Want/clock; Chat verbs are operational patterns, not craft law.

## Hard

**Edge cases**
- Time travel / loops: clock rules must state what can change; else continuity pass will thrash
- Series gaps: log between-book elapsed time in 
- Prophecy / flashforward: FUTURE = reference only in AI packets
- Opening on “wrong” timeline is a promise — hardest when it looks like flashback bait
- Long mid-story episode on barely introduced past cast → invest Present first or lean past casts

**Chat-about verbs**
- **Intake** — single clock vs dual braid; what already exists
- **Abs log** — force chronological table before story-order map
- **Braid stress** — equal-partner switch pressure convergence mirrors revelation orientation
- **Travel sanity** — elapsed-time table vs “rush” symptom
- **Refuse** — past tourism; unverified braid; Chat verbs are operational patterns, not craft law

**Handoff / scope**
- **Produces:** clock rules abs log story-order map convergence/mirror/revelation/orientation cards travel sanity.
- **Next:** outline beats that pressure switches ; continuity pass; mystery clue ledger if crime clock.
- **Not this sheet:** full dual-timeline theory essay (`craft-dual-timeline`); series bible gaps alone .
