**[example stub]** BBC row + lore-via-conflict (structural only — swap placeholders)

| Step | Fill | Story test |
|------|------|------------|
| Belief | “Debts of favor outrank written law.” | Forces who they protect first |
| Behavior | Refuses to report a cousin to the clerk | Argument in public queue |
| Consequence | Loses license / status with outsiders | Exile threat = next Want |

Lore-via-conflict (4 lines, not tourist dump):
1. POV needs a stamp the clerk won’t give without a name.
2. Cousin asks silence — belief says kinship wins.
3. Refusal costs the license that funds the trip.
4. Reader learns the rule because it *hurts*, not because someone lectures.

**Setting-as-character quick test**

- Recurring place, or road-trip never returns?
- Could the story happen almost anywhere with a paint swap?
- What *fundamentally* changes if the place changes?

**Handoff / scope**
- **Produces:** capped bible stubs + BBC chain + Forbidden/continuity facts the draft can query.
- **Next:** location catalog / stage cards for on-page places; series bible for multi-book locks (, , ).
- **Not this sheet:** encyclopedia continents/pantheons; scene blocking; magic L/W/C depth .

**Future shop overlays** (fields only — no file splits now): sci-fi implication/tech envelope vs romance intimacy-pressure map — see §5.

**Methods cited:** Sanderson laws (serve-story / expand-don’t-add); culture-as-conflict (Belief→Behavior→Consequence); hollow-iceberg reveal via POV. Paraphrased — not a substitute for the primary texts.

**Refuse:** no invented cosmologies as default width; no tourist lore dumps; Chat verbs are operational patterns, not craft law.
