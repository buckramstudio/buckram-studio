## Hard

**Hook vs cliffhanger debate**
- Hooks (question, decision, revelation, threat, promise, emotion, reversal, escalation, resonant close) preferred as default
- Cliffhanger = mid-action cut at peak — rare; needs earned setup; fatigue if every chapter
- Genre intensity: literary / character-forward → softer reopen; thriller / serial → harder cliffs still earn setup

**Chapter vs scene**
- Scene = story unit (Want/obstacle/turn); chapter = packaging + pause + promise management
- Multi-scene chapters still need one dramatic movement or they read as anthology

**When to break “ends at inhale”**
- Resonant close / rest-stop chapters after major set-pieces — still leave a larger thread open
- Book/part endings may exhale; next unit must reopen pressure

**Handoff / scope**
- **Produces:** one-chapter promise → accomplish → reopen card + MICE nest + hook mode.
- **Next:** living-outline sync / next-chapter carry-in ; scene GCD if unit pressure soft .
- **Not this sheet:** full novel beat sheet; every-chapter cliffhangers; abandoned open MICE doors.

**Methods cited:** Card / Kowal MICE Quotient (nest FILO); chapter hook / inhale practice (hooks over cliffhangers). Paraphrased — not a substitute for the primary texts.
**Refuse:** no every-chapter cliffhangers; no abandoned open MICE threads without a park marker; Chat verbs are operational patterns, not craft law.
