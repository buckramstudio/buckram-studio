## Normal

**Temperament → artifact picker — situation rows**

| Situation | Prefer |
|-----------|--------|

Prefer: premise → codex → chapter cards → self-correction pass → draft one chapter at a time. Do not one-shot the novel outline without a codex.

**Post-draft X-ray** (Tiffany Yates Martin sagging-middle lenses)

| Lens | Ask |
|------|-----|
| Cohesion | Does every beat serve the controlling conflict? |
| Arc stall | Did protag stop changing / choosing? |
| Deflated stakes | Did risk/reward meaning go stale? |
| Lagged tension | Is suspense delayed without visible cost of delay? |

**Scene spreadsheet columns (Snowflake 8):** `POV` `what happens` `page estimate` `chapter #` `story day` `but/therefore to next` `subplot id` `MICE thread` optional GCD/Sequel stage — reorder freely; ~100 scenes common.

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Timeline mush | Story-day column + chapter clock |

## Hard

**School hybrids that change decisions**
- Snowflake → STC/Weiland overlay at step 8: map scenes to beats without restarting step 1
- Wells reverse fill → then alias onto Weiland/STC  — don’t keep three parallel sheets forever
- Pantser mid-book: reverse outline + beat overlay beats restarting Snowflake from zero
- Series / mystery: plotter tools + continuity bible — plantser tent poles alone under-serve fair-play

**Advanced diagnostics**

| Symptom | Cause | Tool |
|---------|-------|------|
| Spark dies in outline | Over-specified connective tissue | Plantser card; leave middle open |
| Structure debt after pants | No reverse outline | Post-draft change map |
| Act Two fog | No tent poles / disasters | Zero draft middle→end or Snowflake step 2 disasters |
| Draft ≠ outline forever | No living sync | Ritual card + 60s rule |
| Sagging middle after draft | Stakes/arc/cohesion lag | X-ray four lenses + but/therefore |

**When to break Snowflake order:** cycle back from 7 to 1–6 when characters teach you; skip step 9 if conflict-free; never treat steps as sacred if draft is teaching harder truths.

**Handoff / scope**
- **Produces:** living outline artifact (Snowflake tracker / Wells fill / scene spreadsheet / chapter cards) with day MICE but/therefore columns when used.
- **Next:** draft one chapter at a time with promise cards ; beat content / causal QA .
- **Not this sheet:** inventing a second beat cosmology; polishing a zero draft into a fake first draft.

**Methods cited:** Snowflake Method; Wells 7-point reverse fill; living-outline sync practice; Tiffany Yates Martin sagging-middle X-ray. Paraphrased — not a substitute for the primary texts.
**Refuse:** no fossil outline after diverge; no one-shot novel outline without codex; Chat verbs are operational patterns, not craft law.
